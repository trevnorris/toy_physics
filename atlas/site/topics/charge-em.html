Electromagnetism and charge bookkeeping

The electromagnetic sector has a controlled zero-mode Maxwell limit, but charge sign and coupling bookkeeping are not derived from throat circulation or geometry alone.

paper-backed 11 selected graph nodes · 5 paper-pending source entries

Physical Picture

The EM story is intentionally split into a localized parent sector, a brane zero-mode limit, and charge-branch bookkeeping. That prevents older shorthand symbols from being mistaken for physical electric charge.

Magnetic or vortical circulation can be real structure without being the definition of electric charge sign.

Mathematical Core

The safe public statement is a controlled Maxwell reduction in the zero-mode brane limit. Beyond that limit, mixed and transverse components must be tracked rather than erased.

The graph carries separate firewalls for charge sign, q_eff/q_star bookkeeping, and kappa_rho notation.
