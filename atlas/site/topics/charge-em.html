Electromagnetism and charge bookkeeping

The electromagnetic sector has a controlled zero-mode Maxwell limit, but charge sign and coupling bookkeeping are not derived from throat circulation or geometry alone.

paper-backed 14 selected graph nodes · 5 paper-pending source entries

Physical Picture

The electromagnetic sector is not introduced as an isolated 3+1 Maxwell theory. It starts as a localized 4+1 gauge field with a transverse localization profile, then admits an ordinary brane Maxwell reading only after a controlled zero-mode reduction.

That split matters for charge. Electric sign is carried by an oriented charge branch label, while the observed brane coupling is dressed by localization. The public page should therefore distinguish the microscopic branch charge, the effective brane charge, and any magnetic or vortical circulation data.

Magnetic or vortical circulation can be real structure in the model without being the definition of electric charge sign. Treating circulation as charge would collapse two different physical sectors and would corrupt later atom, lepton, and response-packet claims.

The mixed brane-bulk EM core is also retained in the parent theory. In a strict zero-mode brane reduction those channels are suppressed, but the research reader should not present them as nonexistent.

Mathematical Core

The localized Maxwell sector uses a localization profile Z(w). Its integral controls the effective brane coupling, which is why the same parent charge branch can appear with a dressed brane strength after canonical normalization.

The zero-mode Maxwell equation is the safe public reduction: under axial, zero-mode, and far-field assumptions it gives an effective 3+1 Maxwell law with a localization-dressed coupling.

The charge-normalization equation records the bookkeeping rule. The microscopic branch charge q_star and the observed q_eff are related by the localization integral, while eta_Q carries the electric sign label.

Beyond the zero-mode limit, mixed and transverse components such as A_w, J^w, and F_{mu w} must be tracked rather than erased. Those channels are exactly why this topic links forward to the moving-throat and response-packet pages.

The graph carries separate firewalls for charge sign, q_eff/q_star bookkeeping, and kappa_rho notation because each blocks a different tempting shortcut.

Status

This topic is mostly paper-backed by the 4D parent, Maxwell, and plasma papers. The mixed-sector moving-throat plumbing is still partly notes-backed and should remain visible as paper pending.
