Foundations: superfluid medium, bulk arena, and throat defects

The model starts with a declared bulk superfluid arena, finite brane-bulk throat defects, and a parent action that fixes the fields used downstream.

paper-backed 6 selected graph nodes · 7 paper-pending source entries

Physical Picture

The reader should begin with the physical objects rather than the PN outputs. The bulk is the ambient dynamical medium, the brane is the observed sector, and a particle is modeled as a finite throat-like defect connecting brane and bulk support structure.

This topic separates what is declared in the parent model from what is later obtained by projection or reduction. That distinction matters because many later results are exact only after a specified closure or controlled limit.

Mathematical Core

The parent level uses a GNLS-type bulk sector together with localized electromagnetic degrees of freedom. The graph records this as the current declared parent action, not yet as the promoted moving-wall action.

The important reader-level invariant is provenance. Every later claim should say which parent object, projection, closure, or branch computation it depends on.

Status

This page is backed by published 4D and ontology papers where available. Any moving-throat wall dynamics introduced later remains additive and must not be read back into the frozen parent paper unless a later paper explicitly adds it.
