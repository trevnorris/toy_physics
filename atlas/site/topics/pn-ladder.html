Physical Picture

This page is the research reader's map of the published PN chain. It should be read as a sequence of increasingly strict matching claims, not as one undifferentiated declaration that every physical branch has been solved.

The 1PN and 2PN conservative sectors are full assembly results inside their declared closure hierarchy. They show that the effective two-body dynamics can reproduce the standard EIH and ADM targets once the earlier dictionary and closure data are supplied.

The 2.5PN result changes the problem. It is dissipative rather than conservative, and it narrows the live route to the orbital or worldtube STF quadrupole channel while leaving the passive/outgoing normalization as branch data still to be supplied.

The 3PN conservative result inherits that structure. It closes the conservative assembly in a fixed ADM chart while making the grouped real P2 payload visible as the next microscopic moving-throat target.

The 4PN local sector is assembled, but the hereditary tail coefficient points back to the same quadrupole-normalization gate exposed at 2.5PN. That is the reason the page says local/conditional rather than unconditional full 4PN closure.

This framing helps readers avoid an overclaim: a local algebraic match is not always the same as a solved physical branch.

Mathematical Core

The key public math is the equality of the model's effective two-body Lagrangian or Hamiltonian with standard PN targets at each controlled stage.

At 1PN the displayed equality is the direct EIH match. At 2PN the Legendre-transformed Hamiltonian matches the ADM target. At 3PN the fixed-chart conservative split separates the kinetic image, grouped P2 middle block, and geometry completion.

The 2.5PN bridge should be understood as a status transition rather than just another rung: it demotes scalar and dipole dangers, isolates the STF quadrupole route, and leaves an explicit normalization gate.

The 4PN tail bridge is therefore not a new independent fit. Its coefficient is inherited from the same outgoing quadrupole normalization that appears on the 2.5PN page.

The dedicated quadrupole page carries the normalization equation and open gate details; this page carries the reading order and status logic for the PN chain.

Status

The 1PN, 2PN, and 3PN conservative claims are paper-backed exact assemblies within their stated hierarchies. The 2.5PN and 4PN statements are also paper-backed, but their status is conditional because they expose or inherit the same branch-normalization requirement.

The frozen papers should remain fixed. If later work closes the outgoing quadrupole gate, the website should add a later-development section and update the graph status rather than rewrite the original PN status.

Safe summary: lower conservative rungs close within declared closures; dissipative and hereditary rungs depend on target-blind branch export of the quadrupole normalization.
