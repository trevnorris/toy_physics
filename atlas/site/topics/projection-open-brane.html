Projection versus reduction: why the brane is an open subsystem

Projection defines what a brane observer measures; reduction is an extra controlled dynamical simplification and cannot be silently identified with projection.

paper-backed 7 selected graph nodes · 2 paper-pending source entries

Physical Picture

A brane observer sees projected quantities, not the entire bulk state. The brane sector is therefore open: information, support, and boundary data can live outside the reduced brane description.

This is the conceptual reason later readouts must be treated carefully. A response packet is a measured or compressed output, not automatically a new physical throat object.

Mathematical Core

The projection kernel gives an exact measurement definition. A zero-mode or brane-localized reduction is a separate approximation step with its own assumptions.

Public explanations should preserve this order: parent fields first, projection second, controlled reduction third, downstream claims last.

Status

The graph contains an explicit firewall blocking the invalid shortcut "projection equals reduction." Public pages should keep that warning visible.
