0PN momentum into a finite mouth from the brane side. Working calculation, 2026-11-14.
Repository base 8e9808153a745ac4ed8ac6d99f1d5e328ea3d96c. The two scripts are local additions.
The sampling domain is the full ball in three brane coordinates at a flat w cut.
Q is the prescribed effective 3-D volume drain rate. A radial sink weight assigns incoming
mass per time to points on that opening. The removed material carries the local potential-flow
velocity. Both preparations use identical target weights. Only scalar pressure is present.
The numerical calculation integrates every source-target ring pair, including relative azimuth.
Unit rates, radii 1 and 1.5, centre separations 4 and 8. The scaled loads have units of inverse length squared.
The dipole control changes the angular weighting at fixed integral in the two compared preparations.
Each numerical load is the cross contribution. The self term is identical in pair and control.
