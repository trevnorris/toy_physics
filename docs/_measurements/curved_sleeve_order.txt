Use the real ordered-fraction energy with constant a_B and kappa_B, and a reversible
parcel variation carrying chi. For a smooth carrier and fixed remaining fields the
bare energy gives force density -n grad(mu_n) + mu_chi grad(chi). In signed distance
d to a regular interface, H(d) = div N on its equidistant surface. A planar kink
q(d) solves f_B'(q) = kappa_B q'', and therefore mu_chi = -kappa_B H(d) q'.
No expansion in curvature was used in this local identity. Its constant-tension
surface reduction uses a thin, locally planar layer. Neither a thin layer nor its
alignment to the archived radius is established for an actual converting throat.

The graph r=R(w) has an S^2 angular section in four-space. SymPy differentiates its
unit normal in 4D radial coordinates. Wolfram varies 4*pi*R^2*sqrt(1+R'^2) and
computes the axial area force and rim identity. Both use the archived M1c shape.
The force on the open side is positive along +w for the integrated reported shape.
It is a surface-tension force on the interface and does not include end/collar
reactions, total pressure, other stresses or the reaction on the receiving bulk.

In the separate diffuse-profile test q(d) = 1/(1+exp(d/ell)). Set chi = q -
epsilon*exp[-(d/b)^2] for small positive epsilon. Sample at the same spatial
point d=0 on the original mid-surface, holding n and the reference geometry fixed.
At that point both perturbations give delta chi = -epsilon and delta chi' = 0.
In the outputs the normalized response is (d force_N/d epsilon)/(a_B*q'(0)).
q'(0) is negative. The two responses have opposite signs in either normalization.
Neither profile disturbance gives a conversion kinetics, a pressure-driven steady
state, or an integrated change of throughflow.
