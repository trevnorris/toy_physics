Signed brane-side momentum current across the periodic radius-1 feed patch.
Positive speeds use archived solutions. The negative-speed fields are their x reflections. All four are checked against the signed bulk equation, reconstructed imposed feed twist, inward current law and depth flux.
per_speed is the positive-minus-negative momentum current divided by twice the speed magnitude. aperture uses the boundary-law stress on the full opening. aperture_layers independently takes finite differences of the feeding field in the normal direction. Neither is a mechanical support measurement.
