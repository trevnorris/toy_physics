## A. Independent physics from source laws

### A1. Bulk balance and stress (measured structure)

From the parent matter Lagrangian and the Noether report:

- \(g_i = m\rho v_i\)
- \(\Pi_{ij}^{\mathrm{matter}} = m\rho v_i v_j + \delta_{ij}P(\rho) + \sigma_{Q,ij}\)
- \(\sigma_{Q,ij} = \frac{\hbar^2}{4m}\Big[\frac{\partial_i\rho\,\partial_j\rho}{\rho}-\partial_i\partial_j\rho\Big] = -\frac{\hbar^2}{4m}\,n\,\partial_i\partial_j\log n\) (identity)
- \(\partial_t g_i + \partial_j\Pi_{ij} = q_\star\rho(E_i+v_j B_{ij}) - \rho\partial_i V_{\mathrm{conf}}\)

With \(m=\hbar=1\) as in the archived runs, the mixed quantum flux is \(\sigma_{Q,xz}= -\frac14 n\partial_x\partial_z\log n\).

### A2. Promoted confinement and wall (parent)

\[
V_{\mathrm{conf}}=V_{\mathrm{wall}}(\Sigma/\ell_c),\qquad
\delta\Sigma=-\eta,\qquad
\delta V_{\mathrm{conf}}=-\frac{V_{\mathrm{wall}}'(\Sigma_0/\ell_c)}{\ell_c}\eta.
\]

Body force on the carrier: \(f_i^{V}=-\rho\partial_i V_{\mathrm{conf}}\). Virtual work on a geometry displacement at fixed \(n\) is opposite that body-force work. Densitized wall action supplies endpoint axial traction \(\propto T_w\partial_w q\) for dipole \(\eta=q(w)\mu\).

### A3. Stationary periodic crossflow identity

No lateral body force, periodic \(x,y\), steady state \(\Rightarrow\) \(\partial_z\int\Pi_{xz}\,dA=0\). On a uniform feed plane with \(v_x=U\) and constant \(n\): pressure and mixed-density \(xz\) integrals vanish, so

\[
L_x(U)=m J(U)\,U.
\]

At the mouth plane, support of \(j_N\) and of the imposed \(\partial_N\log n\) lies in the aperture, so the same integrated \(L_x\) is the feeding-side aperture load.

### A4. Benchmark density-restoring energy (conditional / imposed)

Law \(\partial_N u=-g_X(u-1)\) with \(g_X=(1-|\mathbf x-X\hat x|^2/a^2)_+^3\) is generated by

\[
E_R=\tfrac12\int g_X(u-1)^2\,dV
\]

as an amplitude boundary energy. Force on a rigid translation of the profile at fixed trace: \(F_X=-\partial_X E_R\). With \(s=1-((x-X)^2+r^2)/a^2\),

\[
\partial_X g_X\big|_{X=0}=\frac{6x}{a^2}s_+^2,
\]

so

\[
F_X=-\int\frac{(u-1)^2}{2}\cdot\frac{6x}{a^2}s_+^2\,dV.
\]

Integration by parts on the mouth plane with compact \(g_X\) identifies this with the quantum \(\int\sigma_{Q,xN}\) contribution built from \(\partial_N\log n=2\partial_N\log u=-2g_X(1-1/u)\).

A single real surface energy of \((u,\theta)\) cannot also generate the intake laws \(j=su^2\) (fixed shape) and \(j\propto su^2\) (fixed rate): amplitude variations induce \(\int\eta\,\delta j=2\int j\eta^2\) or \(2\int j(\eta-\langle\eta\rangle_j)^2\), both strictly positive on the saved fields, while a pure \(e(u,\theta)\) would have to reconcile a phase-independent amplitude derivative with a phase derivative that depends on \(u\).

### A5. Conditional phase-continuous transmission

For a thin real \(e(\mathbf x-X,n)\), single continuous \(\psi\), \(c=\hbar^2/(2m)\):

\[
c[\partial_N\psi]=e_n\psi
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
[\partial_N\log n]=\frac{2e_n}{c},\qquad [j_N]=0.
\]

Tangential divergence of \(ne_n-e\) removes a pure edge flux; the net interface force at fixed field is \(-\partial_X\int e\). Example \(e=\lambda c g_X(\sqrt n-1)^2\) gives receiving slope \((\lambda-1)g_X(u-1)\). \(\lambda=0\) is smooth continuation; \(\lambda=1\) matches the benchmark feeding restoring law with zero receiving amplitude slope. Both leave the physical surface functional open (`u1_body_dynamics_inputs.yaml` throat functional `OPEN_INPUT`).

### A6. Parent confinement sideways projection

For \(\delta R=\mu\,\delta q(w)\), spherical translation gives first-order radial shift \(\mu\delta X\) and zero first-order volume change. At fixed \(n\),

\[
\partial_U f_x(w,0)=\frac{4\pi}{3}\int_0^\infty r^2 n_1(r,w)\,\partial_r V_{\mathrm{conf}}\,dr,
\]

load on geometry, opposite \(-\int n\partial_x V_{\mathrm{conf}}\). Confinement depends on \(q(w)\) without \(\partial_w q\) or a \(\delta(w)\) mouth term, so a shrinking collar virtual displacement gives work \(\to 0\). Wall tension separately supplies endpoint force \(\frac{4\pi}{3}T_w(0)\partial_w q(0)\).

### A7. 0PN holding response (not determined here)

With feeding load \(L_x\) and net passed load \(E_x\), held support satisfies \(H_x=E_x-L_x\) (edge/other forces booked separately). The linear datum is \(-\partial_U H_x(a,0)\). Archives supply \(L_x\) and conditional restoring pieces; they do not supply measured \(E_x\) or \(H_x\).
