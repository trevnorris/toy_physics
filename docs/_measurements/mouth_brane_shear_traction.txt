Time-dependent tangential traction of the homogeneous 3D brane exterior at 0PN. Working result pending review.
Repository base fd667ea9291b4430a96afe3fd932926232f5507d.
Exterior of a sphere, rotational shear only, constant positive coefficients, zero initial perturbation, decaying Laplace solution. No bare mouth kinetic term. Total actuator force unmeasured.
SymPy derives the displacement curl and angular traction, independently checking the integrated field momentum. Mathematica works from the azimuthal curl, solves for the boundary traction and recovers the step in the time domain.
A direct tangential tie and an unforced traction-free rim are two conditional boundary choices, not selections for the saved drain. The ideal shear problem is not a numerical trajectory of its nonuniform feeding field.
