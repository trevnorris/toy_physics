Parent confinement projected onto rigid translation of each spherical cross-section. Working result pending independent review.
Angular displacement is q(w)*mu with mu the x direction cosine. V_r is the unshifted radial potential derivative. n1 is the dipole coefficient of number density per unit incident speed. The force is minus the geometric energy derivative at fixed carrier.
For the axial work check f0 is an illustrative constant load per unit length and L is the interval length. With s=w/L the trial profiles are (1-s)^2(1+2s) and that expression plus s^2(1-s)^2. On the shrinking collar use L=h, the first profile, and a test load f0+f1*w. The genuine endpoint work weight is the value of this profile at the mouth.
No numerical wall profile, endpoint traction or incident-flow fields are inputs to this symbolic calculation.
