Let s be the externally specified sideways speed. At fixed radius, mu is the
cosine to the fixed incident direction. The test density includes the baseline
normal gradient g, an intrinsic linear dipole h, an even quadratic term e, and
a cubic term k. The cut displacement is s eta mu in the normal direction. All
coefficients are free. These fields test differentiation and sampling without
asserting a dynamical origin. SymPy projects angular samples; Wolfram uses
Cartesian equatorial bands of a sphere inside the three-dimensional aperture.
The current is the local signed number current relative to the moving cut.
The intrinsic and sampled slopes differ when a tilted entrance cuts an existing
density gradient. A manufactured reading gives no value for the physical slope.
