SymPy differentiates a moving discontinuity. Mathematica differentiates the inventory on both sides of a moving cut. Both include carrier transport and mixed stress.
The Python numerical readout differences the two total face fluxes. The Wolfram readout uses summation by parts against the masked face density. The common grid and local receiving-slope variations are diagnostic inputs. No center trajectory was produced.
