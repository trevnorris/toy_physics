Weak one-mouth current selection in the feeding-slab exterior. Working calculation, pending independent review.
Unit ambient number density and solver units for velocity and phase. The letter a is the radius of the three-dimensional opening, a_0 a common reference radius, H the slab depth and m the constituent mass. The displayed current is the coefficient of epsilon. The mode assumes fixed lateral reservoir phase and a reflecting opposite face; q is a mode amplitude of the trial normal phase slope.
The two example laws refer to an imposed weak normal speed on a common aperture shape. Both calibrate to the same mouth at a_0. They test whether the exterior plus this calibration selects a radius law. No grid solve or physical current selection is claimed.
