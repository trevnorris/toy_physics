The SymPy calculation varies n V and eliminates a dynamical mode via the Euler–Lagrange equation in Laplace space. Mathematica independently translates a Cartesian potential, checks the opposite carrier force and completes the square at harmonic frequency. The conservative mode calculation is conditional on a kinetically normalized stable dipole. It neither inserts a bare mouth mass nor identifies the dipole of the physical drain.
For the density-responsive continuation of the saved radius-1, rate-0.25 preparation the amplitude and phase are independent trial traces at fixed center. Python differences the normalized flux at every sample and then pairs with the dipole test h=x g_X. Mathematica computes the net work along the two phase legs of a closed rectangle. Both use the same saved state. The Wolfram binary contains only its raw real face, imaginary face and current from the previously verified extraction.
Mixed work from the analytic weighted-variance readouts differs by 8.6736173798840355e-19 between engines. The finite-variation values are within 6.6967785483651454e-13 of the analytic result. The physical four pushes were not run.
