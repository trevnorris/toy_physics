The four trajectories are prescribed, radius 1 and inward number rate 0.25. The model has no bare kinetic term for the geometric center in these tests.
SymPy uses the centroid shape derivative, a multiplier for an entrained field and a Laplace initial-value solution for elastic coupling. Mathematica uses angular virtual work, field power and a retarded oscillator solution. The examples are conditional and do not fit an extra mass to the drain.
The difference between examples is additional actuator force for the same motion. The numeric outputs are the known additive feeding-side term in the total balance. No total physical forces are reported.
