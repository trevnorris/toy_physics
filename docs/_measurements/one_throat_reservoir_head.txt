For a stationary ungauged carrier, mechanical head per constituent contains
the kinetic term, EOS enthalpy, confinement and quantum potential. Integrating
dp/n for p=K n^5 gives the difference of h=(5K/4)n^4. The pressure-only
formula requires homogeneous resting far states with equal potential.
The full signed energy drop has not been measured for a native draining throat.

For the archived conservative segment, both boundaries lie on a stationary
one-frequency carrier with m=hbar=mu=1 and K=4/5. Its endpoint densities
are 1 and 81/100. SymPy uses its real amplitude and constant current;
Wolfram uses the real and imaginary Cartesian fields. This is an exact
head evaluation on the old equations; no old shooting jobs were replayed.
Residual pressure enthalpy by itself does not give total head.
