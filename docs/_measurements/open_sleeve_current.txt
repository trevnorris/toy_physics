One-throat transport and sideways probe calibration, 0PN working result.
Repository baseline 8e9808153a745ac4ed8ac6d99f1d5e328ea3d96c. Independent physics review pending.

The stationary complex neutral carrier satisfies
 -(hbar^2/2m) Laplacian psi + [(5K/4)|psi|^8 - mu] psi = 0
with hbar=m=1, K=0.5, bath number density 1, and mu=(5K/4)n_bath^4.
Here m is the mass of one constituent; the solver takes no throat inertia input.
Stage-1 tensor-grid measure 4*pi*r^2 dr dw. Axisymmetric tube r<1, 0<w<1.85;
finite receiver r<2, 1.85<w<3.7. Tube/receiver join is an internal face, no sink.
No normal derivative on the tube, receiver side and shoulder. Feed amplitude 1;
far-end field 1+0i. Feed phase is an unknown solved for the prescribed positive
inward number rate or zero. This reduction uses fixed reflecting geometry and
neutral carrier dynamics; no nonlinear order/wall fields or brane return solve.
It does not use the frozen Stage-1 carrier profile. No input for holding open
the native mouth is inferred. The model's exteriors use a volume rate in a
different domain; no conversion to a far-field gravity source is inferred.

Newton solves real and imaginary field balances and the integral rate equation.
The clearance checks use 2e-13 for maximum absolute integrated solve residual,
1e-10 absolute for number rates and 1e-8 for cell net rate per volume. Rates
increase toward positive w. Current on internal links is (hbar/m) times area
times Im(conj(psi_left) psi_right) divided by center spacing. The independent
saved-field readout uses the face midpoint and finite difference normal gradient;
it tests assembly of the budgets, not independent physical dynamics. Density
and phase depend on the geometry and bath choices. The test does not establish
stability or determine the rate selected by a physical throat.

The geometric check integrates the dipolar traction on a spherical rim using
32-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature in the direction cosine. The code computes
the aperture centroid from the full radial volume moment and checks known test
geometries. No force was applied to an evolving mouth. For the missing reaction
see software/em_charge_attribute/reports/u1_body_dynamics.md lines 20, 329-331,
420-425 and docs/em_u1_body_definition.md lines 56-68.
