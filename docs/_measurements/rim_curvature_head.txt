The real order wall may continue smoothly from the brane into the sleeve. Pieces
of one regular constant-tension sheet cancel their shared endpoint variation.
In a sharp description of a physical junction, a sheet ending on the two-sphere
contributes sigma times outward unit conormal times area to the energy gradient.
The collar area energy has gradient (2 tau/a) e_r per area. Reaction forces
have the opposite sign. A real junction can also have bending edge forces and
moments, anchoring, loads, and a restriction on allowed contact displacements.

Follow a stationary, unidirectional normal tube from feed to bulk. The profiles
depend only on z; the normal volume measure is A(z) dz. H=A'/A. I=A n u is
the conserved number rate, positive toward the bulk. Assume n,A,M positive and
finite on the nonconstant order transition. The equal-depth bare energy has
constant gradient coefficient k. The conditional material force from a
reversible displacement is mu_order q'. The pure-relaxation law is
u q'=-M mu_order. There is no side exchange, extra kinetic source, external
work in the layer or independently maintained wall drive in this test.

Euler projection including this order force gives n B'=mu_order q', where
B=m u^2/2 + V_conf + h(n) + Q for a steady conservative ungauged carrier.
Then B'=-I q'^2/(A n^2 M). Integral power transfer to the carrier from the
steady layer is -I^2 Rq with Rq=integral q'^2/(A n^2 M) dz. B has energy
per constituent units; I^2 Rq has energy per time units. Flow from feed to bulk
requires positive available head in this closed set of sectors; the condition
does not prove hydraulic or phase matching, a stationary profile, or stability.
An independent drive, further work or moving boundary changes the energy balance.
Endpoint normal stresses and physical collar conormal forces live on different
cuts. Normal-tube fields alone do not compute tangential edge fluxes at a seam.
