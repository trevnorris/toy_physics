In the solver's fixed radial measure let B = integral r^4 n d^3x at each w.
The confinement energy per dw is k B/R^4. Positive R variation is outward.
Q_R denotes the generalized outward work force, -partial_R E at fixed field.
Path-A uses the signed wall RHS S_psi = -Q_R. The axial force on the carrier
from the radial part of the confining potential is R' Q_R per dw. The axial
trap, side loads, inlet momentum flux and momentum storage remain separate.

Take a fixed, flat outlet section, oriented +w (bulkward). In smooth matter
the raw flux per area is m n u_w^2 + K n^5 + sigma_Q,ww, with additional
stresses when their sectors are active. The quiescent uniform carrier
reference contributes p_b. Across a common internal cut the absolute flux
is included once. If the reference has motion, gradients or other stress,
its corresponding full normal flux must be used for an excess measurement.

Independent straight-segment check, unit area, no gauge or order stress.
Let a=sqrt(n)>0 and j=n u_w be the constant number flux. The stationary
carrier has chemical potential mu and smooth static potential V(w):
  hbar^2 a''/(2m) = (V + 5 K a^8/4 - mu) a + m j^2/(2a^3).
Using this equation in the raw matter momentum flux gives
  Pi = hbar^2 a'^2/(2m) + m j^2/(2a^2) + (mu-V)a^2 - K a^10/4,
  dPi/dw = -n dV/dw.
At local a=1, mu=m=hbar=1, K=p_b=4/5, V=0 the reference-subtracted
value is (a'^2+j^2)/2. These are local IVP data of a regular ODE; the
specified p_b is a comparison stress, and no boundary solution connects
these cuts to a real reservoir. Wolfram uses the real and imaginary
fields and their spatial translation stress. SymPy uses the amplitude
equation and the hydrodynamic normal momentum flux. Neither uses their
example phase slope or current as a physical throat boundary condition.
