Unequal-mouth virtual work, paired minus matched isolation.
Positive x points from radius 1 to radius 1.5. Restoring force and the centered energy difference have solver force units. Each derivative holds the carrier trace fixed.
mixed and current_fd contract the amplitude response of the phase-current law with a dipole variation. uniform_rate_fd and uniform_expected give the integrated current response to a uniform fractional amplitude change. The reciprocal contraction from varying the restoring law with phase is zero.
