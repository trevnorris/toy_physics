Unequal-mouth interaction momentum in a steady inviscid constant-density exterior. Working result pending independent review.
Two independent positive monopole volume rates Q_1 and Q_2. The plane normal points from mouth 1 to mouth 2; l and b are positive center-to-plane distances, with d=l+b. The ring coordinate is s. The code differentiates both velocity potentials, subtracts both isolated xx momentum currents, and integrates over the complete separating plane. A direct midpoint integral checks the result.
The k_i example assigns outward transfer in units of the local signed arrival and assumes steady fields and no independent force. It checks momentum accounting. It supplies no physical k_i, holding force, source-to-rate map, or acceleration.
