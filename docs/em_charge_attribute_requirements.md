## 1. The reframe (and why it removes the gravity-killer — *conditionally*)

Every prior EM wall came from asking **one degree of freedom** — the collective mechanics (compression/shear) — to be *both* a physical density wave (gravity) *and* a gauge structure (EM). No single DOF can be both. Real matter avoids this: **gravity ← mass-energy; EM ← charge**, a *separate* property of the same constituent.

Apply it: the constituents carry a **charge-attribute** (internal DOF) distinct from **mass/density**. Gravity = mechanics of the mass/density sector (unchanged). EM = dynamics of the charge sector. **Because the mass-superfluid is `U(1)`-neutral, it cannot Anderson–Higgs the emergent photon → the `B_eff→0` killer does not return.** Still **one medium, one constituent, two attributes** (like a real electron) — not two substances.

**Panel correction (important):** the decoupling is **"free from *Higgsing*, NOT free from *coupling*."** Neutrality kills the `B_eff` route, but a constraint-preserving `ε(ρ)`-type dielectric coupling still rides on the density (a back-door gauge–matter coupling) — it must be *shown* to stay on the deconfined side of the phase diagram (Fradkin–Shenker), not assumed. And neutrality is **mandatory and must be enforced at the operator level** (`∇·E = ρ_q` with `ρ_mass` gauge-neutral), not sloganeered from "mass ≠ charge."

## 4. Sub-question 2 (softness) — DRILLED, and it holds

**Does superfluid softness (gapless phonons / a flowing lattice) *necessarily* confine the compact `U(1)`?** Panel answer (unanimous, cited): **No, not in 3+1D.**

- **Polyakov's inevitable instanton confinement is a *2+1D* result.** In **3+1D** compact `U(1)` has a **rigorously established weak-coupling deconfined phase** (Guth 1980; Banks–Myerson–Kogut 1977). Our EM lives on the 3D brane = **3+1D** → the favorable case.
- **"Soft lattice ≠ soft constraint":** the elastic modulus `c_44` (→ phonons/flow) and the ice-rule energy `J_ice` (→ monopole gap) are **independent**. The lattice can flow while the ice rule stays hard.
- **Real evidence:** acoustic phonons already coexist with every real quantum-spin-ice candidate; explicit **magnetoelastic** calculations find photon/phonon **renormalization and decay, not compulsory confinement** (Simon–Patri–Kim; Seth–Bhattacharjee–Moessner). The phonon–gauge couplings (`u_{ij}E_iE_j`, `δρ F²`, …) are **perturbatively irrelevant at the 3+1D Coulomb fixed point**. *"Superflow is not a relevant destroyer" (Codex).*
- **Not a counterexample:** "monopole supersolids" in spin ice (Bojesen–Onoda 2017) are a *Higgs/confining* mechanism (charged monopoles condensing), not Coulomb-photon coexistence.

**Contingent kill mechanisms (real, avoidable — this is where a no-go could still hide):** (i) a *charged* condensate Higgsing the photon; (ii) the ice-rule DOF being **atom positions** (then superflow tunneling directly creates monopoles) rather than a **separate internal DOF**; (iii) dislocation proliferation changing the constraint graph; (iv) strong magnetoelastic lowering of the monopole gap; (v) accidental dimensional reduction to 2+1D.

**The survival condition (X):** a **gauge-neutral superfluid condensate** + a **finite monopole gap** + the **ice-rule on an internal DOF decoupled from the phase** + **3+1D**. All checkable; none a theorem.
