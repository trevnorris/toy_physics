A single medium that is *simultaneously* **crystalline** (a lattice — broken translation symmetry) **and superfluid** (frictionless flow — broken phase symmetry). This is the phase that lets the bulk **flow (→ gravity's drain survives) *and* carry a lattice (→ a home for the constraint)** — as **one medium in one phase**, not a second substance.

## 3. The lock, correctly understood — a *constraint*, not a stiffness or a field

The lock = a **nonholonomic (velocity-level) constraint** tying moving-throat translation (current) to brane-shear rotation (`B`). This dodges *every* prior wall:

- **Not a brane elastic stiffness** → it does not stiffen the compression mode → **gravity survives.** (The lock we killed in v1 was a *position-level* stiffening of curl/div — `B_eff→0`. A *velocity-level* constraint is a different animal and doesn't touch the density mode.)
- **Not a separate bulk gauge photon** → EM waves stay on the brane as shear → **no `1/r³` localization problem.** (The heavy "bulk photon imprinted on the brane" version is `[GRAVEYARD]`.)
- **Hosted by** the supersolid bulk **lattice** and/or the **throat–shear coupling** itself — Maxwell's "second species / sublattice," the reviewers' "lattice constraint."

If the constraint exists, **Faraday (`∇×E=−∂B/∂t`), the dual sign, and the Ward tie arrive together** (as in Maxwell). **Faraday — homeless in v1 — now has a home: it *is* the constraint.**

## 4. The unified sign pivot (this absorbs Codex's electric-sign catch)

Codex's v1 catch: a genuine scalar `h`-branon gives **attraction** between like charges (scalars attract, like gravity), so the claimed electric repulsion was unearned. The reframe absorbs this cleanly by making **both** EM signs consequences of the *one* constraint:

- **No constraint** → EM degenerates to **scalar exchange** → like-charge **attraction** + **no magnetism** = **a second attractive scalar force (a fifth force), NOT electromagnetism.** *(The honest no-go fallback.)*
- **Constraint present** → the **dual sign** (like charges repel, like currents attract) is a consequence of the constraint/gauge structure, exactly as in Maxwell. Repulsion is **not** a scalar-exchange result — it's the lock.

So the entire EM sector reduces to a single yes/no: **does the constraint exist?**
- **Yes** → EM-like, with the correct dual sign (toward Maxwell).
- **No** → scalar attraction + no magnetism = a **characterized no-go** ("this medium yields gravity + light + a *second attractive scalar*, not EM").

Both outcomes are first-class and publishable.
