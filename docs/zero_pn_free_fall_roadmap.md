Gravity, light and charge must follow from compatible lower-level laws of this material. These are physical premises set by the user. The current coherent-carrier calculation tests one sector of them.

Record 5 is retired as a physical bulk candidate. Its classical particles and Langevin bath remain an archived demonstration of shear relaxation. Future microscopic receiver or interface calculations must retain superfluid coherence and account for the material's energy and momentum. Apparatus work, outgoing waves and any allowed quantized vortices still require measurement in their applicable regimes. Zero shear viscosity alone does not set the force on a moving mouth.
