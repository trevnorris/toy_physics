## Purpose and scope

### source-paper-4d-1pn-full--controlled-1pn-scope — Controlled conservative 1PN scope

Status: `lifecycle=current` · `evidence=provisional` · `memory_review=ai_draft`

The paper presents a sector-by-sector construction of the conservative two-body first post-Newtonian sector of a four-spatial-dimensional brane–bulk toy model. It distinguishes exact identities, controlled reductions, protocol-dependent closures, and exact algebra after chosen coefficients are inserted. It does not claim an assumption-free derivation from a solved moving-throat bulk background or coverage of radiation reaction, dissipative leakage, spin, strong-field dynamics, higher PN orders, or the absence of every preferred-frame effect.
