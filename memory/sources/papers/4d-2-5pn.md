### source-paper-4d-2-5pn--conservative-no-go — Conservative kernels cannot supply 2.5PN reaction

Status: `lifecycle=current` · `evidence=derived` · `memory_review=ai_draft`

A local conservative response kernel that is real and even in frequency produces only time-reversal-even operators. Within the frozen conservative hierarchy, such terms may renormalize static, inertial, or elastic response but cannot generate a nonzero local dissipative 2.5PN reaction term; a retarded odd-frequency sector is required.

### source-paper-4d-2-5pn--breathing-vertex — Continuity makes the breathing outlet derivative-coupled

Status: `lifecycle=current` · `evidence=derived` · `memory_review=ai_draft`

For a smooth breathing family whose static displacement remains static, the paper derives from linearized projected continuity that the outlet source begins as
\[
\delta S_{\rm leak}(\mathbf x,\omega)=(-i\omega)\delta q(\omega)B_q(\mathbf x).
\]
Thus the breathing-to-outlet vertex is \(\dot q\)-like or zero rather than a direct \(q\)-like source. This reclassifies the paper’s breathing counterexample but does not eliminate every scalar outlet in an arbitrary PDE completion.

### source-paper-4d-2-5pn--normalization-gap — Physical moving-throat normalization remains open

Status: `lifecycle=current` · `evidence=open` · `memory_review=ai_draft`

The completed moving-throat PDE must still establish the physical passive/outgoing isotropic quadrupole branch and determine its canonical even pair, equivalently satisfying
\[
\hat m_0^2\Gamma_5=\frac{2G}{5c^5}.
\]
The branch relations already make the next even moment and odd coefficient dependent on that even pair, so the remaining gap is a single normalization problem rather than missing tensor structure, parity, sign, or representation content. Singular or anisotropic source maps, noncompact outlets, failure of small-body scaling, or non-passive completions lie outside the theorem envelope.
