# Lean toy-model research proposal

## Purpose and authority

This bundle is the current **research plan** for testing the one-medium
brane--bulk toy model. It organizes falsifiable questions, required
calculations, compatibility gates, and possible outcomes. It does not report
that those calculations have succeeded, establish current derivation status,
or claim that the real universe is described by the model.

`research_proposal.tex` controls the eight-chapter assembly. The migration
handoff controls how older proposal material is used and explicitly demotes the
former forty-chapter structure to reference material. Within scientific
questions, the proposal defers to canonical definitions and current executable
evidence rather than silently revising them.

## Central research question

The proposal asks whether **one frozen parent medium** can simultaneously
support the required light, gravity, electric, magnetic, radiative, inertial,
throat, and reservoir behavior without private sector-by-sector retuning. If
$\Lambda_S$ is the viable region for a mandatory sector, the decisive test is

$$
\Lambda_{\rm common}=\bigcap_{S\in\mathcal S_{\rm required}}\Lambda_S
\ne\varnothing.
$$

A collection of individually successful sectors on mutually incompatible
branches or parameter points is not a common solution.

## Proposed ontology, not established output

The planned model has preferred time and four Euclidean spatial dimensions.
One globally conserved compressible medium appears as an ordered,
finite-thickness, shear-supporting brane state and a de-structured bulk state.
Light is sought as a two-polarization guided transverse branch; a localized
classical photon packet is a separate, stronger hypothesis. Particles are
hypothesized to be finite supported and oriented throats. Their motion,
acceleration, and dressed medium response are intended to account for charge,
magnetism, radiation, passive response, and inertia.

Localized one-way throat drainage and distributed bulk-to-brane return through
the porous brane are distinct processes. The final particle program must close
them with the shared reservoir as one material, momentum, energy, and—where
applicable—entropy fixed-point problem.

These statements define what the proposal will test. For the present scientific
status of the corresponding mechanisms, use the topic pages linked below.

## Work sequence and decision gates

The program is deliberately capability-first and far-field-first:

1. turn each desired sector into explicit operator, source, normalization,
   boundary, loss-channel, and failure requirements;
2. intersect those requirements in a Medium Capability Matrix;
3. if the intersection survives, freeze the fields, equations, dynamical
   branch, coefficients, sources, boundaries, and reservoir data as Candidate
   Parent System V1;
4. solve one stable finite brane and its complete constrained spectrum;
5. rederive every mandatory far-field sector on that same background;
6. only then attempt supported, moving, and interacting throats and the global
   reservoir fixed point.

Chapter 8 packages that sequence as gates G0--G7: controlled contract;
far-field capability contracts; common region/Candidate V1; stable background
and common far field; supported throat/species basis; moving and accelerating
closure; two-throat/contact closure; and local--global reservoir closure.

## Status and failure discipline

- A symbolic identity, reduced model, imposed geometry, solver run, and
  physical branch authorize different strengths of claim.
- Mandatory conditions are conjunctive; success in one sector cannot compensate
  for a mandatory failure elsewhere.
- A failed approximation, branch, or frozen candidate rejects only that named
  level unless the obstruction is shown across the admitted architecture.
- A healthy guided light branch may survive even if the stronger localized
  photon-packet hypothesis fails.
- A changed field, constitutive term, coefficient, source definition, boundary
  ensemble, or reservoir law creates a revised candidate and reopens actual
  downstream dependencies. It does not license post-result repair.
- Even full declared survival would establish a controlled classical analog in
  stated regimes—not exact GR, exact electromagnetism, quantum theory, a full
  particle spectrum, or empirical identification with nature.

## Important reading limitation

The handoff names separate ontology, companion-plan, derivation-ledger, and
status-audit documents as higher authorities within their scopes. Some of those
are root `docs/`, root `notes/`, or PDE-ledger material deliberately excluded
from this memory. Therefore this capsule records the proposal's plan and
authority boundary; it must not be used as a substitute for the current
research and software capsules.

The proposal also predates several current repository outcomes. Its healthy
odd Coulomb carrier, conserved oriented current, common Candidate V1, and
supported throat are research targets—not claims that override the current
native-$P$, electric-boundary, or Stage-1 results.
