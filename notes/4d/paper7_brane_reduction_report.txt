2) What the Mathematica warnings mean (and why they are not physics errors)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
You reported warnings like:
  Div::sclr: The scalar expression vT does not have a divergence.
  Div::sclr: The scalar expression J_brane does not have a divergence.
  Integrate::idiv: Integral of ... does not converge ...

These are *export/evaluation artifacts*, not model problems.

Cause: the previous ExportPaperTeX helper stripped HoldForm before TeX conversion.
That accidentally lets Mathematica *evaluate* symbolic objects that were intended to
stay formal, e.g.
- Div[vT,{x,y,z}] where vT is a symbolic transverse vector placeholder,
- Integrate[J^N, dw] where J is unspecified,
- ∫ ρ ∂_a V_conf d^4X where ρ is symbolic and Mathematica tries to integrate only ∂_a V_conf.

Result: Mathematica tries (and often fails) to prove convergence or to interpret
vT/J_brane as scalars.

Fix (in v5): ExportPaperTeX now keeps everything held during TeX conversion
(SetAttributes[ExportPaperTeX, HoldAll] and never removes HoldForm).
This removes those warnings and preserves the intended expressions.
