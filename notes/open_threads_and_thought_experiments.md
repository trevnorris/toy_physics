## 1. Puncture-creation energy / self-energy → the magnitude `R1` (2026-07-22, user thought experiment)

**Framing.** Private thought experiment; quick — banked to revisit, not yet worked. User will raise it again.

**The picture.** Creating a particle = creating a `±w` puncture in the brane (like pushing into a balloon). The
indentation pulls the surrounding brane into `w`; that costs a **finite** elastic energy stored in the bent brane
(`∫|∇h|²`, the exterior `h~1/r` field energy, `~1/a` — finite *because* there is a real core `a`, not a mathematical
point). The standing waves that hold the puncture open also anchor it: a **soliton/defect-core balance** between the
brane's restoring tension (wants to close the puncture) and the trapped standing-wave pressure (holds it open). So
"creating a particle" pays a computable "tugging into `w`" energy = the classical **self-energy** structure (the `r_e`
scale; note `r_e` = self-energy scale ≠ mouth diameter `a`).

**⭐ The constituent chain (user's correction — get this exact).** It is NOT "trapped transverse energy = mass." Rather:
the **trapped transverse energy** (light held in the throat) **enables the FLOW of superfluid** (the drain), and **the
FLOW is the mass** (gravity = flow between drains). So the trapped transverse energy *sustains the throat that drains*;
the **flow it enables** is the gravitating mass — the transverse energy is the constituent, the flow is the mass, and the
*same* structure holds the `±w` puncture open, whose bend `h` is the **electric field**. One throat, three faces:
trapped-transverse-energy (constituent) → superfluid flow / drain (mass, gravity) + held-open `±w` puncture (charge,
the `h` field).

**Why it's interesting — two live targets it points at:**
1. **Pins the magnitude `R1`.** Making the energy balance *quantitative* — bending rigidity vs standing-wave pressure
   fixing the core size `a` and the deflection amplitude `h_A` — is exactly the calculation that would fix the currently
   **unbounded core normalizations `c_a, c_ξ`** (`R1_REQUIRED(magnitude)`). I.e. this thought experiment *is* the physical
   content of the sim-deferred nonlinear throat solve, viewed as an energy variational problem.
2. **Charge–mass / equivalence-principle coupling.** A charged particle's rest mass then carries a computable
   charge-field piece (charge and mass not independent). Whether that piece gravitates identically is the flagged
   `MIXED_SCALAR_EP_RISK` (scalar-mediated EP deviation) — this picture is what would decide it. (⚠ the self-energy is a
   *component* of `mc²`, not additive on top of it; the sharp content is the charge-field fraction of the mass + whether
   it obeys EP, not "more than `mc²`".)

**⚠ Sector caution (do not conflate — this is exactly the stage-033 departure).** The static **electric** field is `h`,
the **out-of-brane embedding bend** (the `w`-pinning). **Light and magnetism** are a *different* mode — the **in-brane
transverse shear** `u_T` (2 photon polarizations, `c_γ²=μ_R/ρ_br`). Maxwell fuses E/B/light into one `F_μν`; our model
keeps them **distinct** (E = `h` out-of-brane scalar; B/light = `u_T` in-brane transverse vector), related only by the
moving-throat boost, NOT exact Maxwell unification — precisely the `NATIVE_P_NO_EMERGENT_GAUSS` / "EM is not exact
Maxwell" result (stage 033). "The electric and magnetic fields are just the transverse sector" is the Maxwell habit the
model deliberately breaks. What survives of the unifying instinct: the transverse sector is the *constituent* (trapped →
flow → mass; and holds the puncture = charge), but E is the held-open bend, not the transverse wave itself.
