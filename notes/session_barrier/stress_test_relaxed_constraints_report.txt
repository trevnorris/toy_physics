========================================================================

It keeps the exact reduced formulas fixed by the file stack and makes only one
explicit phenomenological choice: the sign-changing source branch feeds back into
the primitive Yukawa amplitudes beta_U, beta_W through a linear function of R[sigma].

Interpretation
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Leakage and J^w E_w both rise as r decreases because E0(r) was chosen to sharpen
   at small separation. That is the open-system export channel.
2) The UV cross-coupling makes V nonzero, so epsilon_eta is not invariant once
   chi_lambda grows. The exact energy-drop formula quantifies the amount of
   transfer-shape energy drained into the dressing leg.
3) The sign-changing sigma branch can push g[sigma] outside the positive-source range.
   That is the explicit compensated-branch stress test requested in Task 3.
4) The plotted V_eff(r) is lower than Coulomb over part of the near-contact range
   for this parameter set, but the reduction is still carried only by short-range
   families plus open-system export; no new long-range attractive law is inserted.
