\section{Current stage-source rule}

The canonical narrative source is now the per-stage \TeX{} tree under
\ReaderPath{paper/stages/}.  The archive appendices assemble those stage files
in stage order, while the main parts provide the theorem-block synthesis layer.

\section{Escalation path}

When a chapter in the reader build is not enough, the escalation path is:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item reader chapter and its stage range;
  \item archive stage appendices for the matching part;
  \item canonical stage file plus the matching SymPy/Mathematica audit scripts;
  \item verification trackers for cross-layer audit status.
\end{enumerate}
