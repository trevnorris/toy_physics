\section{Current stage-source rule}

The paper source is two-layered, not a single assembly tree.  The
theorem-block synthesis layer is inline in \ReaderPath{paper/parts/} and in
\ReaderPath{paper/appendices/stage_appendix_part*.tex}.  The stage-card layer
is \ReaderPath{paper/stages/}; those cards are the canonical per-stage
provenance and audit anchors, and the archive appendices input them in stage
order.  For provenance questions beneath either paper layer, the decisive
audit record is the per-stage notes layer, especially \ReaderPath{notes/stages/}
and \ReaderPath{notes/stages/review/}, read together with the relevant scripts,
notebooks, and trackers.

\section{Escalation path}

When a chapter in the reader build is not enough, the escalation path is:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item reader chapter and its stage range;
  \item theorem-block synthesis in the matching main part or archive stage
  appendix;
  \item canonical stage card plus the matching SymPy/Mathematica audit scripts;
  \item \ReaderPath{notes/stages/}, \ReaderPath{notes/stages/review/}, and any
  matching per-stage notes tree for decisive provenance questions;
  \item verification trackers for cross-layer audit status.
\end{enumerate}
