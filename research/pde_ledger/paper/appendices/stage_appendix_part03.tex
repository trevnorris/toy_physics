This appendix is the detailed verification ledger for the third theorem block of the moving-throat derivation.  The corresponding main-text chapter, \cref{chap:part03_support_family1}, gives the readable theorem narrative.  The purpose here is stricter: to keep the stage-by-stage algebra, branch conditions, support-completion maps, parent-overlap thresholds, and explicit Family--1 verdict in a form that a reader can audit without reconstructing the 253-stage source tree.

Part~II ended with a selected finite-throat test.  In its most compressed form, that test asked for a softening depth \(\xi\) satisfying a normalization locus and a support-feasibility inequality,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-part03-entry-FG}
F(\xi_{\rm req},\delta)=R_{\rm target},
\qquad
M_{\rm mix}\leq G(\xi_{\rm req},\delta).
\end{equation}
Part~III asks how the quantities in \eqref{eq:app-part03-entry-FG} are populated by an explicit finite-throat continuum branch, how rank--2 support completion changes the selected mode, how coherent D/N support supplies the missing loading, and whether the first concrete Family--1 wall branch passes the support/source side of the outgoing-quadrupole theorem gate.

The final support-side result of this appendix is deliberately conditional but strong.  Under the natural minimal isotropic contact-plus-pole conservative precursor,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-part03-minimal-module-preview}
Y_Q^{\rm cons}(\omega)
=\frac34+\frac{1/4}{1-\omega^2/\Omega_Q^2},
\qquad
\Omega_Q=\frac{3c_s}{2a},
\end{equation}
the explicit Family--1 support/source side selects
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-part03-final-ratio-preview}
\boxed{
\rho_\alpha=\frac43,
\qquad
\zeta_{\rm req}=\frac13,
\qquad
\mathrm{Pe}_{\rm req}=0}.
\end{equation}
Thus, within the declared reduced branch, the support/source side is not the remaining bottleneck.  The remaining reduced gap is the Part~IV problem: deriving the minimal isotropic conservative quadrupole precursor from the actual grouped real \(P_2\) and geometry branch.

\paragraph{Audit philosophy.}
This appendix intentionally separates exact reduced algebra from actual-branch realization.  Determinants, Schur complements, product laws, support-ratio identities, monotonicity signs, gain thresholds, and Family--1 arithmetic are exact once their closure is declared.  The closures themselves---finite-throat continuum extraction, coherent D/N support, thin-wall branch selection, shell-weighted density extraction, and the minimal contact-plus-pole conservative quadrupole precursor---are not upgraded to assumption-free theorems of the unsolved nonlinear moving-throat PDE.

\paragraph{Notation warnings.}
The symbol \(G\) is used both for Newton's constant and for support-feasibility functions; this appendix writes the latter as \(G(\xi,\delta)\), \(G_q\), \(G_U\), or \(G_{\rm tr}\).  The variable \(\rho\) in the continuum placement map is a dimensionless mixed-sector ratio, not the bulk mass density.  The loading ratio \(\rho_\alpha\) is likewise not density.  The labels \(20,21,22\) are grouped real \(P_2\) lanes, not spacetime indices.

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-part03-demand-variables}

\zeta_{\rm req}

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-part03-Rquad-def-preview}
R_{\rm quad}=\zeta_{\rm req}-\zeta_{\rm phys}.
\end{equation}
Thus \(R_{\rm quad}<0\) means support exceeds demand, \(R_{\rm quad}=0\) means saturation, and \(R_{\rm quad}>0\) means support failure inside the declared support family.
\end{definition}

\section{Part III verification summary}
\label{sec:app-part03-verification-summary}

The Part~III verification ledger establishes a chain of exact reduced implications:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-part03-summary-chain}
\begin{aligned}
\text{Stage 037 continuum data}
&\Rightarrow
\text{Stage 038 placement product}
\\
&\Rightarrow
\text{Stages 039--044 rank--2 support closure}
\\
&\Rightarrow
\text{Stages 045--051 coherent D/N support threshold}
\\
&\Rightarrow
\text{Stages 052--069 parent support/source gain theorem}
\\
&\Rightarrow
\text{Stages 070--078 explicit Family--1 wall data}
\\
&\Rightarrow
\text{Stages 085--090 one-ratio finish line.}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

The most important audit points are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item The selected branch variables are not free: they are extracted from a finite-throat continuum Schur complement.
  \item The dimensionless placement map has an exact product law, so the target and mixed load cannot be varied independently once \((\epsilon_W,\Lambda)\) are fixed.
  \item The first non-flat \(U\) correction preserves scalar placement but breaks source/loading collinearity unless \(\delta_U=0\) or \(\rho_0=0\).
  \item Rank--2 support completion is analytic because the determinant is linear in support loading.
  \item The first coherent local D/N support kernel forces tracking rather than an arbitrary rank--2 rescue.
  \item Support enhancement is controlled by \(S(\zeta;\epsilon)\), and the lowest symmetric twin gives exact doubling.
  \item If non-twin support is needed, overlap boost, Robin softening, and transport bias are all bounded by explicit finite-throat formulas.
  \item The source transport bias has a drift-diffusion origin and is selected by a coupled support/source fixed point.
  \item The microscopic gain projects back to parent GNLS/confinement overlaps, so insufficient parent confinement loading or poor source/support coherence produces an exact no-go in the declared channel.
  \item The canonical tanh-wall Family--1 branch fixes \(\Lambda_\ell=\eta=37\), \(\chi_s=37/2\), and \(\kappa=12321/5\).
  \item The explicit Family--1 wall-depth datum is not starved for the minimal branch; its remaining support-side input is only \(\rho_\alpha\).
  \item The minimal contact-plus-pole quadrupole precursor gives \(\rho_\alpha=4/3\), \(\zeta_{\rm req}=1/3\), and \(\mathrm{Pe}_{\rm req}=0\), safely inside the Family--1 success region.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{verificationchecklist}
\item Check that every use of \(M_{\rm mix}\), \(R_{\rm target}\), and \(\delta\) traces back to the Stage~037 continuum extraction and Stage~038 dimensionless placement map.
\item Keep \(\rho\), \(\rho_0\), and \(\rho_\alpha\) distinct: only \(\rho_\alpha\) is the outgoing-branch loading ratio.
\item Do not use the split--\(U\) deformation as if it were a generic rank--2 support rescue; Stages~045--046 show the first coherent D/N kernel tracks.
\item Apply the lowest-twin criterion only when \(\Pi_{\rm tr}\leq2C_{\rm mix}\) or \(\zeta_{\rm req}\leq1\).
\item Apply non-twin overlap and Robin softening only within the finite ceilings \(\Omega_0^2\leq\pi^2/4\) and \(A_K\leq4/(4-x)\).
\item Treat \(\mathrm{Pe}\) as a transport-selected physical bias, not as an arbitrary fitting variable.
\item Preserve the parent-overlap Cauchy no-go: if the aligned gain is below the fail threshold, source-profile engineering cannot rescue the branch in the declared support channel.
\item Distinguish the natural shell-weighted \(\Theta_w^{(\chi)}\) from the conservative lower envelope \(\Theta_w^{(J)}\).
\item Apply the cancellation of outgoing normalization factors only after the selected outgoing branch has been normalized.
\item Mark the final Family--1 success as conditional on the minimal isotropic contact-plus-pole conservative precursor; deriving that precursor is the next block, not an output of Part~III.
\end{verificationchecklist}
