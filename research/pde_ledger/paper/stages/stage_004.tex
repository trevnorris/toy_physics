\paragraph{Bundle role.}
The electromagnetic sector is treated as a projection problem before it is
treated as a reduced one-port normal form.  The source bundle fixes three
rules:
\begin{enumerate}
\item projection by parts creates a real transverse leakage/source term;
\item the homogeneous Maxwell equations project in measured fields, while the
inhomogeneous equations carry source-coupled flux fields;
\item reduction is a matched channel, not the primary parent derivation.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Projection identity.}
For any smooth profile \(Q(w)\) and observation kernel \(W(w)\),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004-projection-ibp}
\partial_w(WQ)-W\partial_wQ-(\partial_wW)Q=0.
\end{equation}
After integration over \(w\), \eqref{eq:stage004-projection-ibp} is the
bookkeeping identity that turns the parent \(w\)-flux into a boundary term
plus a kernel-gradient leakage term.
