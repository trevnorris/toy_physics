\subsection*{Stage 004.002: Covariant projected Maxwell law}
\label{subsec:stage004002-projected-maxwell-covariant}

\paragraph{Source and audit.}
This substage corresponds to
\StageFile{scripts/moving_throat_pde_stage004_002_projected_maxwell_covariant_sympy_audit.py}.
It is the covariant projection of the localized parent Maxwell equation.

\paragraph{Parent law.}
The localized parent equation is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004002-parent-maxwell}
\partial_N\!\left(Z(w)F^{NM}\right)=\mu_0J^M .
\end{equation}
For brane-facing components \(M=\mu\), apply a normalized observation kernel
\(W(w)\) and integrate in \(w\).  The result is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004001-projected-maxwell}
\partial_\nu\!\int WZ F^{\nu\mu}\,dw
=
\mu_0\int WJ^\mu\,dw
-\int W\partial_w(ZF^{w\mu})\,dw.
\end{equation}
Using \eqref{eq:stage004001-projection-ibp},
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004002-projected-maxwell-expanded}
\partial_\nu\!\int WZ F^{\nu\mu}\,dw
=
\mu_0\int WJ^\mu\,dw
-[WZF^{w\mu}]_{\partial}
+\int (\partial_wW)ZF^{w\mu}\,dw .
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Projected continuity.}
The same projection applied to parent current conservation gives
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004001-projected-continuity}
\partial_\mu\!\int WJ^\mu\,dw
=-[WJ^w]_{\partial}+\int(\partial_wW)J^w\,dw .
\end{equation}
Thus leakage is not a notation choice.  It is the exact open-system balance
term induced by observing a localized parent current through \(W\).

\paragraph{Output.}
Stage~004.002 exports the projected inhomogeneous law
\eqref{eq:stage004001-projected-maxwell}--\eqref{eq:stage004002-projected-maxwell-expanded}
and projected continuity \eqref{eq:stage004001-projected-continuity}.
