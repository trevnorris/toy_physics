\subsection*{Stage 004.004: Projection/reduction comparison}
\label{subsec:stage004004-projection-reduction-comparison}

\paragraph{Source and audit.}
This substage corresponds to
\StageFile{scripts/moving_throat_pde_stage004_004_projection_reduction_comparison_sympy_audit.py}.
It compares the exact projected law with the zero-mode reduction channel and
keeps observer dependence explicit.

\paragraph{Reduction channel.}
For a zero-mode ansatz \(A_\mu(x,w)=a_\mu(x)\), the projected equation contains
the weighted factors
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004004-projected-integrals}
I_{WZ}=\int WZ\,dw,\qquad
I_{WH}=\int WH\,dw,\qquad
I_{WS}=\int WS\,dw .
\end{equation}
The effective projected parameters are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004004-effective-parameters}
\mu_{\rm eff}^{\rm proj}=\mu_0\frac{I_{WS}}{I_{WZ}},
\qquad
\frac1{\xi_{\rm eff}^{\rm proj}}=\frac{I_{WH}}{\xi I_{WZ}}.
\end{equation}
These are observer-kernel quantities until a matching prescription is imposed.

\paragraph{Mismatch guard.}
The comparison audit checks concrete smooth profiles and mutation guards.  A
delta-like source and a smooth localized gauge profile do not automatically
produce the same coupling as a reduced brane theory; source matching is a
separate condition.

\paragraph{Output.}
Stage~004.004 exports the observer-dependent projected coefficients
\eqref{eq:stage004004-effective-parameters} and marks the reduction-first
brane law as a matched channel rather than the controlling derivation.
