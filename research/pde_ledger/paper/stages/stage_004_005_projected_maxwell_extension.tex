\label{eq:stage004002-hz}

\qquad

\paragraph{Source match.}
The source profile \(S(w)\) must be matched to the localized Maxwell weight:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004002-source-match}
S(w)=\frac{Z(w)}{Z_{\rm int}},
\qquad
Z_{\rm int}=\int Z(w)\,dw .
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
\mu_{\rm eff}^{\rm proj}=\frac{\mu_0}{Z_{\rm int}},
\end{equation}
which is the reduction-first coupling.  The audit also checks the Gaussian
case and the regulator limit, including mutation guards distinguishing
\(H=1\) from \(H=Z\).

conditions are the firewall between exact projection and reduced brane
