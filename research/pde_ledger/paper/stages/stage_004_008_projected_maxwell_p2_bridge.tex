\subsection*{Stage 004.008: Projected Maxwell \(P_2\) bridge}
\label{subsec:stage004008-projected-maxwell-p2-bridge}

\paragraph{Source and audit.}
This substage corresponds to
\StageFile{scripts/moving_throat_pde_stage004_008_projected_maxwell_p2_bridge_sympy_audit.py}.
It applies the bundle shifts to the one-pole/normalization compatibility
surface.

\paragraph{Compatibility surface.}
Let
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004008-compat-base}
S=M+B_2+Z_2,\qquad T=B_4+Z_4 .
\end{equation}
After eliminating the static wall stiffness, the isotropic compatibility
surface is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004008-compat}
\mathcal C=\frac{N_0}{P_{0,\rm target}}-\frac{3S^2}{T}.
\end{equation}
The projected variation is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004008-dcompat}
\delta\mathcal C
=\frac{n_0}{P_{0,\rm target}}
-\frac{6Sz_2}{T}
+\frac{3S^2z_4}{T^2}.
\end{equation}
The static conservative shift \(z_0\) cancels from
\eqref{eq:stage004008-dcompat}; it changes \(P_0\), but not the eliminated
compatibility surface.

\paragraph{Output.}
Stage~004.008 exports the \(z_0\)-cancellation and the projected \(P_2\)
compatibility bridge \eqref{eq:stage004008-dcompat}.
