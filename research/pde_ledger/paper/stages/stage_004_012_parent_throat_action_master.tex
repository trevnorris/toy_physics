\subsection*{Stage 004.012: Parent throat action master}
\label{subsec:stage004012-parent-throat-action-master}

\paragraph{Source and audit.}
This substage corresponds to
\StageFile{scripts/moving_throat_pde_stage004_012_parent_throat_action_master_sympy_audit.py}.
It begins the parent-action continuation of the projected EM bundle.

\paragraph{Promotion.}
The throat is promoted to an autonomous graph field \(R(\Omega,w,t)\) with
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004005-parent-action}
S_\Sigma[R]=\int dt\,dw\,d\Omega\;\mathcal L_\Sigma,
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004012-parent-density}
\mathcal L_\Sigma
=\frac12\mu_\Sigma R_t^2
-\frac12T_{w,\Sigma}R_w^2
-\frac12T_{\Omega,\Sigma}|\nabla_\Omega R|^2
-U_\Sigma .
\end{equation}
The total parent-level model is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004012-total-action}
S_{\rm total}=S_\psi[\psi,A;\Sigma]+S_{\rm EM}[A]+S_\Sigma[R].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Quadratic limit.}
The quadratic stiffness around \(R_0(w)\) is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage004012-keta}
K_\eta=
U_{\Sigma,RR}(R_0,w)
-\partial_w\!\bigl(T_{w,\Sigma,R}(R_0,w)R_0'\bigr)
+\frac12T_{w,\Sigma,RR}(R_0,w)(R_0')^2.
\end{equation}
The audit carries the boundary densities explicitly and tests both zero and
nonzero boundary-discharge probes.

\paragraph{Output.}
Stage~004.012 exports the parent throat action promotion and the exact
quadratic recovery formula \eqref{eq:stage004012-keta}.
