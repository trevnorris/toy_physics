\label{eq:stage011-compat-base}

\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:stage011-compat}

\label{eq:stage011-dcompat}

\eqref{eq:stage011-dcompat}; it changes \(P_0\), but not the eliminated
compatibility surface.
