\subsection*{Derivation}

The selected determinant becomes an equation for \(\xi\).  After collecting terms, the physical softening depth satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage027-quadratic}
\boxed{
\xi^2+B_{\rm cont}\xi+C_{\rm cont}=0},
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage027-Bcont}
B_{\rm cont}
=\delta-M_{\rm mix}(1+t^2R_U^2)-M_{\rm supp}(1+t^2R_\phi^2),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage027-Ccont}
C_{\rm cont}
=-\delta(M_{\rm mix}+M_{\rm supp})
+t^2M_{\rm mix}M_{\rm supp}(R_U-R_\phi)^2.
\end{equation}
Once the physical root \(\xi_{\rm phys}\in(0,1)\) is selected, the normalization gate is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage027-normalization-gate}
\boxed{
R_{\rm target}=F_{\rm cont}(\xi_{\rm phys})}.
\end{equation}
Two exact special surfaces are useful diagnostics: a minimal-kernel source-tied surface and an interference-matched tracking surface.  They are distinguished by whether the direction-splitting term \((R_U-R_\phi)^2\) survives.
