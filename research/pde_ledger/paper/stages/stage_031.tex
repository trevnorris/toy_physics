\subsection*{Derivation}

Define
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage031-Sreq}
S_{\rm req}=\frac{M_{\rm req}}{M_{\rm mix}}.
\end{equation}
Solving \(S(\zeta;\epsilon)=S_{\rm req}\) gives the exact inverse
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage031-zeta-req}
\boxed{
\zeta_{\rm req}=\frac{S_{\rm req}-1}{1+\epsilon(S_{\rm req}-2)}}.
\end{equation}
On the stable side the support factor is strictly increasing up to its softening pole.  Hence if the mixed-only branch is below the required load, there is a unique \(\zeta_{\rm req}\) below the pole.  The support branch succeeds iff
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage031-success-iff}
\boxed{
\zeta_{\rm phys}\geq\zeta_{\rm req}}.
\end{equation}

\stagefield{Output}{The exact threshold \eqref{eq:app-stage031-zeta-req} and success condition \eqref{eq:app-stage031-success-iff}.}
\stagefield{Downstream use}{Stages~032--040 derive \(\zeta_{\rm phys}\) from finite-throat support, overlap, Robin compliance, and transport bias.}
