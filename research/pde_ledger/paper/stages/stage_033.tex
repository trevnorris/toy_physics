\subsection*{Derivation}

The lowest symmetric twin lane has \(\zeta_0=1\).  Therefore its enhancement factor is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage033-S0}
\boxed{
S_0=S(1;\epsilon)=2}.
\end{equation}
It succeeds iff
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage033-lowest-success}
\boxed{
\zeta_{\rm req}\leq1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
S_{\rm req}\leq2}.
\end{equation}
For higher harmonics, the immediate exclusion test is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage033-higher-bound}
\boxed{
\zeta_{\rm req}>\frac{1}{(2n+1)^2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{the }n\text{th same-operator twin lane cannot reach the threshold.}
}
\end{equation}
When the immediate exclusion test does not apply, the support softness must satisfy
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage033-xmax}
\boxed{
x\leq x_{\max}(n;\zeta_{\rm req})
=\frac{1}{n(n+1)}\left[\frac{1}{(2n+1)^2\zeta_{\rm req}}-1\right]}.
\end{equation}
