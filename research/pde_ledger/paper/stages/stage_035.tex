\subsection*{Derivation}

The required support ratio in product variables is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage035-zeta-req}
\boxed{
\zeta_{\rm req}
=\frac{\Pi_{\rm tr}-C_{\rm mix}}
{C_{\rm mix}-\epsilon(2C_{\rm mix}-\Pi_{\rm tr})}}.
\end{equation}
It is monotone in \(\Pi_{\rm tr}\):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage035-zeta-derivative}
\frac{d\zeta_{\rm req}}{d\Pi_{\rm tr}}
=\frac{C_{\rm mix}(1-\epsilon)}{[C_{\rm mix}-\epsilon(2C_{\rm mix}-\Pi_{\rm tr})]^2}>0.
\end{equation}
The exact regime split is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage035-regimes}
\boxed{
\begin{array}{lll}
\Pi_{\rm tr}\leq C_{\rm mix} &\Rightarrow& \text{mixed-only enough},\\[0.25em]
C_{\rm mix}<\Pi_{\rm tr}\leq2C_{\rm mix} &\Rightarrow& \text{symmetric lowest twin enough},\\[0.25em]
\Pi_{\rm tr}>2C_{\rm mix} &\Rightarrow& \text{non-twin asymmetry required}.
\end{array}}
\end{equation}
The excess beyond the symmetric twin branch is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage035-Dzeta}
\boxed{
\Delta_\zeta=\zeta_{\rm req}-1
=\frac{(1-\epsilon)(\Pi_{\rm tr}-2C_{\rm mix})}
{C_{\rm mix}-\epsilon(2C_{\rm mix}-\Pi_{\rm tr})}}.
\end{equation}
For a general lowest lane,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage035-zeta0-general}
\zeta_0^{\rm phys}=\frac{K_W^{\rm eff}}{K_{\phi,0}^{\rm eff}}\Omega_0^2.
\end{equation}
Thus the two resources for non-twin rescue are overlap boost \(\Omega_0^2\) and support softening \(K_W^{\rm eff}/K_{\phi,0}^{\rm eff}\).
