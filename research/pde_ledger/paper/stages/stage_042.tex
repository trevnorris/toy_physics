\subsection*{Derivation}

With \(n=n_{\rm req}\), the selected eigenvector ratio is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage042-evec-rank2}
\boxed{
\frac{e_1}{e_0}
=\frac{m(q-r)+r\xi}{\delta+\xi-mq(q-r)}}.
\end{equation}
Define
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage042-Dqr}
D_{q,r}(\xi,\delta;m)
=[\delta+\xi-mq(q-r)]^2+[m(q-r)+r\xi]^2.
\end{equation}
The loading and source overlaps are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage042-z-overlap}
\boxed{
\frac{(z\cdot e_-)^2}{z_0^2}
=\frac{[\delta+(1+qr)\xi]^2}{D_{q,r}(\xi,\delta;m)}},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage042-s-overlap}
\boxed{
\frac{(s\cdot e_-)^2}{s_0^2}
=\frac{[\delta+(1+rt)\xi-m(q-r)(q-t)]^2}{D_{q,r}(\xi,\delta;m)}}.
\end{equation}
The one-vector result is recovered only when \(r=q\).  Otherwise, the selected-mode normalization sees a three-direction geometry.

\stagefield{Output}{The rank--2 eigenvector \eqref{eq:app-stage042-evec-rank2} and overlap formulas \eqref{eq:app-stage042-z-overlap}--\eqref{eq:app-stage042-s-overlap}.}
\stagefield{Downstream use}{Stage~044 inserts the actual support direction and converts this algebra into a continuum-selected branch point.}
