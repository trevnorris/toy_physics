\subsection*{Derivation}

The reduced rank--2 formalism contains an abstract support direction \(r\).  The continuum support extraction evaluates this direction from the actual support/BdG overlap data.  In the notation used later, the physical direction is written in terms of a source-side ratio \(R_\phi\), while the mixed direction is governed by \(R_U\).  Thus the support completion problem is not merely whether additional support load exists; it is whether
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage043-direction-question}
R_\phi=R_U
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
R_\phi\ne R_U.
\end{equation}
The first case collapses to tracking and the one-direction selected geometry.  The second case gives a genuinely new rank--2 branch.

The exact continuum-selected determinant remains the Stage~041 determinant, but with the physical replacements
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage043-replacements}
q=tR_U,
\qquad
r=tR_\phi,
\qquad
m=M_{\rm mix},
\qquad
n=M_{\rm supp},
\end{equation}
where \(t=\kappa_1/\kappa_0\).
