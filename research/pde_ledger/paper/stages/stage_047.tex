\subsection*{Derivation}

The total tracking load factors as
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage047-Mtr}
M_{\rm tr}=M_{\rm mix}S(\zeta;\epsilon),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage047-S}
\boxed{
S(\zeta;\epsilon)=1+\frac{\zeta(1-\epsilon)}{1-\epsilon\zeta}}.
\end{equation}
The coherent target ratio is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage047-Rtarget}
\boxed{
R_{\rm target}=\frac{\Lambda(1-\epsilon_\eta)(1-\epsilon)^2}{Z_W(1+\chi_0)^2}}.
\end{equation}
It contains no \(\zeta\).  Therefore the support lane only supplies loading; it does not move the target locus itself.

\stagefield{Output}{Support enhancement factor \eqref{eq:app-stage047-S} and target independence \eqref{eq:app-stage047-Rtarget}.}
\stagefield{Downstream use}{Stage~048 inverts \(S\) to obtain \(\zeta_{\rm req}\); Stages~049--050 compute whether physical D/N support can supply it.}
