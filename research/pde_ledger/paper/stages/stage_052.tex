\subsection*{Derivation}

The reduced support/source side has three regimes:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage052-three-zone}
\boxed{
\begin{array}{lll}
W_{\rm wall}<\mathrm{Pe}_{\rm req}/\Delta_\infty &\Rightarrow& \text{universal fail},\\[0.25em]
W_{\rm wall}>\mathrm{Pe}_{\rm req}/\Delta_0 &\Rightarrow& \text{universal matched success},\\[0.25em]
\mathrm{Pe}_{\rm req}/\Delta_\infty\leq W_{\rm wall}\leq \mathrm{Pe}_{\rm req}/\Delta_0 &\Rightarrow& \text{profile-sensitive band}.
\end{array}}
\end{equation}
The sech--Gaussian benchmark shows that realistic smooth mismatch need not significantly enlarge the middle band, but the actual PDE branch still has to supply its own profiles.
