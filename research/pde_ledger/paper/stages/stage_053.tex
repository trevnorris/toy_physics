\subsection*{Derivation}

The normalized exponential source family is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage053-sigma}
\sigma_\alpha(s)=\frac{\alpha e^{\alpha s/L}}{e^\alpha-1},
\qquad
\int_0^L\sigma_\alpha(s)\,ds=L.
\end{equation}
The exact overlap boost relative to the uniform source is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage053-Omega-exp}
\boxed{
\Omega_{\exp}(\alpha)
=\frac{\pi\alpha(2\alpha e^\alpha+\pi)}{(4\alpha^2+\pi^2)(e^\alpha-1)}}.
\end{equation}
The endpoint values are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage053-Omega-window}
\boxed{
\Omega_{\exp}(0)=1,
\qquad
\lim_{\alpha\to+\infty}\Omega_{\exp}(\alpha)=\frac{\pi}{2}}.
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage053-overlap-ceiling}
\boxed{
\Omega_0^2\leq\frac{\pi^2}{4}}.
\end{equation}
Pure overlap rescue alone is possible only if \(\zeta_{\rm req}\leq\pi^2/4\).
