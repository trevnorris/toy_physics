\subsection*{Derivation}

The lowest Robin root satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage054-robin-root}
\boxed{
y\tan y=\eta,
\qquad
0<y<\frac{\pi}{2}}.
\end{equation}
Let
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage054-x}
x=\frac{\pi^2T_X}{L^2K_W^{\rm eff}},
\qquad
0<x<4.
\end{equation}
Then the support-softening factor is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage054-AK}
\boxed{
A_K(\eta)=\frac{K_W^{\rm eff}}{K_{\phi,0}^{\rm eff}}
=\frac{1}{1-x/4+xy^2/\pi^2}}.
\end{equation}
The endpoint window is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage054-AK-window}
\boxed{
1\leq A_K\leq\frac{4}{4-x}}.
\end{equation}
Pure support softening alone can rescue the Stage~052 threshold only if \(\zeta_{\rm req}\leq4/(4-x)\).
