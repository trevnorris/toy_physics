\subsection*{Derivation}

The physical lowest-lane support ratio is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage057-zeta}
\boxed{
\zeta_0(\mathrm{Pe},\eta;\kappa)
=\Omega_{\mathrm{Pe}}^2
\frac{\kappa+\pi^2/4}{\kappa+y(\eta)^2},
\qquad
 y(\eta)\tan y(\eta)=\eta}.
\end{equation}
It is monotone in the constructive Peclet direction, with zero-bias value
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage057-zero-bias}
\zeta_0(0,\eta;\kappa)=\frac{\kappa+\pi^2/4}{\kappa+y(\eta)^2}.
\end{equation}
