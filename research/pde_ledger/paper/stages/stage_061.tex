\subsection*{Derivation}

The fixed-point strength is written as
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage061-Xi-micro}
\Xi_{\rm micro}=\kappa G_{\rm micro}.
\end{equation}
Consequently, the gain thresholds are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage061-G-thresholds}
\boxed{
G_{\rm fail}=\frac{\Xi_{\rm fail}}{\kappa},
\qquad
G_{\rm suff}=\frac{\Xi_{\rm suff}}{\kappa}}.
\end{equation}
The reduced phase diagram is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage061-phase-diagram}
\boxed{
\begin{array}{lll}
G_{\rm micro}<G_{\rm fail} &\Rightarrow& \text{fail},\\[0.25em]
G_{\rm micro}>G_{\rm suff} &\Rightarrow& \text{succeed},\\[0.25em]
G_{\rm fail}\leq G_{\rm micro}\leq G_{\rm suff} &\Rightarrow& \text{root-sensitive}.
\end{array}}
\end{equation}
