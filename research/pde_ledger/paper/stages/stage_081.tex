\subsection*{Derivation}

The branch-product demand is related to \(\zeta_{\rm req}\) by
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage081-Pi-zeta}
\zeta_{\rm req}=\frac{\Pi_{\rm tr}-C_{\rm mix}}{C_{\rm mix}-\epsilon_{\rm blk}(2C_{\rm mix}-\Pi_{\rm tr})}.
\end{equation}
Solving for \(\Pi_{\rm tr}\) gives the equivalent product threshold.  In the unblocked limit, \(\epsilon_{\rm blk}=0\), this reduces to
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:app-stage081-unblocked}
\frac{\Pi_{\rm tr}}{C_{\rm mix}}=1+\zeta_{\rm req}.
\end{equation}
Thus the \(\zeta\)-windows of Stage~080 can be read directly as product-ratio windows.
