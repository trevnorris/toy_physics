STAGE 65 AUDIT PASSED
========================================================================================
1. The first explicit parent wall family gives g_phi = V0/ell.
2. The exact equilibrium gain scales like V0^2 times a shell integral of chi_phi^2/h'.
3. In the thin-wall limit the gain is controlled by a^2/ell, so thinner and wider active walls couple more strongly.
