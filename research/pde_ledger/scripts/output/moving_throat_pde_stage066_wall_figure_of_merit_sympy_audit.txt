
========================================================================================
STAGE 66 — DIMENSIONLESS WALL FIGURE OF MERIT
========================================================================================
W_wall = 4*pi*J1*L**2*V0**2*a**2/(T_X*ell)
W_fail = Pe_req/Delta_inf
W_suff = Pe_req/Delta_0
W_wall(V0_fail)^2 - W_fail = 0
W_wall(V0_suff)^2 - W_suff = 0

========================================================================================
MONOTONICITY
========================================================================================
dW/d(V0^2) = 4*pi*J1*L**2*a**2/(T_X*ell)
dW/da = 8*pi*J1*L**2*V0**2*a/(T_X*ell)
dW/dL = 8*pi*J1*L*V0**2*a**2/(T_X*ell)
dW/dell = -4*pi*J1*L**2*V0**2*a**2/(T_X*ell**2)
dW/dJ1 = 4*pi*L**2*V0**2*a**2/(T_X*ell)
dW/dT_X = -4*pi*J1*L**2*V0**2*a**2/(T_X**2*ell)

========================================================================================
CONSTANT-COMPRESSIBILITY FORM
========================================================================================
W_H = 4*pi*I_f*L**2*V0**2*a**2/(H_w*T_X*ell)
J1 = I_f/H_w reduction = 0
W_H(V0_fail)^2 - W_fail = 0
W_H(V0_suff)^2 - W_suff = 0

========================================================================================
STAGE 66 AUDIT PASSED
========================================================================================
1. The explicit thin-wall parent branch is controlled by one dimensionless wall figure of merit W_wall.
2. Fail/succeed are equivalent to comparing W_wall against the exact bounds Pe_req/Delta_inf and Pe_req/Delta_0.
3. In the constant-compressibility wall layer, the same statement holds for W_H.
