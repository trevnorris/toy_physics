STAGE 164 — MICROSCOPIC LOG-IMBALANCE CHANNELS
========================================================================================
g/r - (g_q K_s)/(g_s lam) = 0
1/r_c - (K_s K_q)/lam^2 = 0

  dln[(g_q K_s)/(g_s lam)] = dln(g/r)
  dln[(K_s K_q)/lam^2] = - dln(r_c)
