========================================================================================
STAGE 182 LEDGER
========================================================================================
1. For two positive microscopic states x^(1), x^(2), the exact finite pairwise log-ratio vector
      Delta x^(2<-1) = ln(x^(2)/x^(1))
   is acted on by the same Stage 192 map M_*.
   Because the coherent monomials are multiplicative, this gives the exact two-point quotient packet
      q^(2<-1) = M_* Delta x^(2<-1)
   with
      q_tr = ln(Ctr_2/Ctr_1),   q_nt = ln(Cnt_2/Cnt_1),   q_eta = ln(epsEta_2/epsEta_1).
2. If the two states are on the same exact similarity orbit, then the dependent pairwise ratios are determined uniquely by the five free pairwise ratios through
      Phi_T = r_U (r_U/(r_gamma r_c))^((1+deltaU_*)/(1+chi0_*)),
      Phi_K = r_c^2 / r_U,
      Phi_mu = Phi_K r_W^2 / r_lambda^2 * (r_gamma^2 r_lambda^2/(r_U r_W))^(-E_*) * (Phi_T/r_U)^(F_*).
3. For an arbitrary pair, the exact reference-independent mismatch triple is
      m_T = r_T/Phi_T,   m_K = r_K/Phi_K,   m_mu = r_mu/Phi_mu.
   The pairwise invariant ratios collapse exactly to
      Ctr_2/Ctr_1   = m_T^(1+chi0_*),
      epsEta_2/epsEta_1 = 1/m_K,
      Cnt_2/Cnt_1   = m_mu/(m_K m_T^(F_*)).
4. Therefore the exact Packet-B logarithmic chart is again
      q_tr = (1+chi0_*) ln m_T,   q_eta = - ln m_K,   q_nt = ln m_mu - ln m_K - F_* ln m_T.
5. Applying the Stage 192 projectors to the finite pairwise log-ratio vector gives the exact two-point decomposition
      Delta x^(2<-1) = O_orb Delta x^(2<-1) + Q_quot Delta x^(2<-1),
   where O_orb carries the free ratios and the transported dependent ratios
      (Phi_T, Phi_K, Phi_mu),
   while Q_quot has support only on the dependent triple and carries
      (ln m_T, ln m_K, ln m_mu).
6. The pairwise transport, mismatch, and quotient packets compose exactly:
      Phi^31 = Phi^32 Phi^21,   m^31 = m^32 m^21,   q^31 = q^32 + q^21.
7. Hence the exact two-point orbit-lock criterion is
      x^(2) in G_* . x^(1)
   iff
      m_T = m_K = m_mu = 1
   iff
      q_tr = q_nt = q_eta = 0.
8. If the two states share the same five free microscopic coordinates, then
      Phi_T = Phi_K = Phi_mu = 1,
   so Stage 199 reduces exactly to the Stage 198 fixed-base formulas.
