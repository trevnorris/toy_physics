STAGE 183 LEDGER
========================================================================================
1. The Packet-B quotient coordinates are derived from the primitive coherent monomials
      (C_tr, C_nt, eps_eta)
   and their logarithmic Jacobian reproduces the carried Stage 192 matrix M_*.
2. The target-orbit witness packet is genuinely witness-independent because the
   finite similarity-orbit law preserves the three coherent monomials exactly.
3. The mismatch chart q = ((1+chi0_*) ln m_T, ln m_mu - ln m_K - F_* ln m_T, -ln m_K)
   is re-derived from the exact dependent-triple orbit solve rather than posited.
4. The pairwise cocycle law is inherited from microscopic ratio composition
      Delta x^(31) = Delta x^(32) + Delta x^(21),
   followed by the monomial compiler q = M_* Delta x.
5. The full linearized home-stretch compiler is
      Delta_HS^lin = (Delta_Q^lin, M_* delta x),
   where Delta_Q^lin is carried from the exact Packet-A closure law and the
   remaining three rows come from the monomial log-ratio Jacobian.
