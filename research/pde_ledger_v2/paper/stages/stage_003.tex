\paragraph{Derivation.}
The primitive brane Lagrangian (no pre-completed square used as input) is
\[
  L=\tfrac12\rho_{br}(\partial_t u)^2-\tfrac12\mu_R(\nabla\times u)^2
    -\tfrac12 B(\nabla\!\cdot\!u)^2+J(\partial_t\theta)\,\delta\rho_B
    +\tfrac12 K_\theta(\nabla\theta)^2 .
\]
\resultanchor{Transverse (earned).} For a polarization \(\perp\mathbf k\),
\(\nabla\!\cdot\!u=0\) and the \(\theta\) couplings vanish, so
\(L_T=\tfrac12\rho_{br}(\partial_t u_T)^2-\tfrac12\mu_R k^2u_T^2\), giving
\[
  \omega^2=\frac{\mu_R}{\rho_{br}}\,k^2,\qquad
  c_\gamma^2=\frac{\mu_R}{\rho_{br}},
\]
two polarizations \(\Rightarrow\) \(\mathrm{physical\_dof}=2\), massless. The
speed is able-to-fail: \(\epsilon=2\rho_{br}\) shifts it to \(\mu_R/(2\rho_{br})\)
(\texttt{FAIL\_TRANSVERSE\_DISTURBED}).
\resultanchor{Longitudinal (departure).} Number conservation slaves
\(\delta\rho_B=-\rho_{B0}\nabla\!\cdot\!u\); integration by parts gives the
Maxwell-form cross-term with the \emph{derived} sign \(C_J=-J\rho_{B0}\), and the
finite conjugate-density stiffness the Cauchy modulus
\(B_{\mathrm{eff}}=\rho_{B0}^2/\chi_c\). Since \(\partial_t\theta\) enters only
through the first-order cross-term, the momenta are
\(p_u=\rho_{br}\partial_t u_L\), \(\pi_\theta=Jk\rho_{B0}u_L\), forcing the
primary constraint \(\Phi_1=\pi_\theta-Jk\rho_{B0}u_L\); preservation gives
\(\Phi_2=-k(Jp_u\rho_{B0}+k\kappa_{\mathrm{phase}}\rho_{br}\theta)/\rho_{br}\), and
\[
  \{\Phi_1,\Phi_2\}=\frac{k^2(J^2\rho_{B0}^2+\kappa_{\mathrm{phase}}\rho_{br})}{\rho_{br}}\neq0
\]
\(\Rightarrow\) second-class pair (\(0\) first-class, \(2\) second-class),
\(N_{\mathrm{phys}}=(4-2)/2=1\). The finite-stiffness pole
\(\omega^2=k^2\kappa_{\mathrm{phase}}\rho_{B0}^2/[\chi_c(J^2\rho_{B0}^2+\kappa_{\mathrm{phase}}\rho_{br})]\)
has positive residue and a bounded reduced Hamiltonian: a real stray longitudinal
mode, not a ghost and not a gauge-removal.

\paragraph{Physical use, fidelity, and the departure.}
The brane carries light: two transverse photons at \(c_\gamma^2=\mu_R/\rho_{br}\)
from the medium's own moduli (light \(=\) in-plane brane shear). Honest scope: the
order-parameter phase \(\theta\) does \emph{not}, on its frozen definitions,

\(K_\theta=C_J^2/\rho_{br}>0\)). Fidelity: the decisive sign \(C_J=-J\rho_{B0}\)
is \emph{derived} here by an in-script integration by parts (the earned source

conventional \(K_\theta\) sign is a labeled postulate whose verdict-dependence is
