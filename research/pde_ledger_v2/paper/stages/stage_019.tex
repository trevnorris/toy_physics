\paragraph{The squared-denominator prefactor object.}
The port numerator and the conservative (``consumption'') denominator are
\[
  N(\omega)=N_0+N_2\omega^2+N_4\omega^4,
  \qquad
  D^{\mathrm{cons}}(\omega)=D_0+D_2\omega^2+D_4\omega^4,
\]
and the prefactor is the \emph{squared}-denominator object
\[
  P(\omega)=\frac{D_0\,N(\omega)}{D^{\mathrm{cons}}(\omega)^2}
  \qquad(\text{not the plain } N/D).
\]

concrete port \(N\)-moments of \texttt{build\_port\_moments}; here they are carried as
abstract, port-agnostic symbols --- the algebra below holds for \emph{any} nonzero
\(D_0\ldots N_4\), so no port value is consumed (the numerical \((D_n,N_n)\) are deferred
Gate-6 branch data).
