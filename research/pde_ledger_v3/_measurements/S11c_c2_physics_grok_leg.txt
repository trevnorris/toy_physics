## A. Fold map — **sound on the construction; two inherited caveats**

**Pressure, not `J_s`.** Independent inspect of `slab_operator` EXPANDED rows: θ and e_W carry `delta_p_±` / `d_w_delta_p_±` only; no `J_s` / `delta_j` / `relative_flux` slots (`independent_derive3.out`, `independent_derive2.out`). `build_face`@510–512 substitutes exactly those four slot names. One-case run: `SUBSTITUTION_KEYS = [d_w_delta_p_minus, d_w_delta_p_plus, delta_p_minus, delta_p_plus]`, `HAS_J_SLOT False`, `CLOSED_HAS_DELTA_P []` (slots fully replaced), `INC_HAS_J []` (`inspect_fold_once.out:5–6,33–38`). c1’s `J` and `T` leaves exist on the response and are not substituted.

**w-jets are differentiated.** `build_face`@506–509: `sp.diff(extension * kernel, NORMAL)` then eval at the reference face. One-case: `JET_EQUALS_PRESSURE False`, `JET_ZERO False` for both faces. Not hand-typed.

**Operator inverse, not scalar division.** Independent reconstruction from §1d:

\[
\bigl[I + (\Lambda_A/\rho_m^2)Z\bigr]\delta p = Z\cdot\text{source},\quad \Lambda_A=\Lambda_A^0/(1-i\omega\tau_A).
\]

Against every c1 `RESOLVENT_DEFINITION`: `INVERSE_OPERAND_RESIDUAL 0` (`independent_derive3.out:14–15` and all eight cases). `kernel_bridge`@396–398 evaluates that operand as a 2×2 matrix and `upper_triangular_solve`s it. Diagonal of `RESOLVENT_KERNEL_MATRIX` is `1/(1 + Λ_A ω /(ρ_m q_out (1−iωτ_A)))`, not a scalar `1/Z`.

**Kernel bridge, both q-legs.** `dtn_kernel` FIRST_SHAPE carries `s11cc1_q_out_input` and `s11cc1_q_out_output` (`inspect_kernel_and_velocity.out:1–7`). Whole-form tags are the empty tuple (170–180 bytes). **Caveat (inherited, not a c2 freeze):** FIRST_SHAPE has `k_input` only, no `k_output`; `k·k′` is `|k_in|²`. That is the c1 kernel c2 consumes. Both *q* legs stay live; Fourier transfer is `Ŵ(k_out−k_in)`.

**Symbol map.** Independently: `V_s` is S11c-a `face_velocity` (interfacial normal speed), `μ_θ` is `mu_theta_operator`. Script: `c1_V → face_velocity/ε`, `c1_μ → μ_θ/ε` (`build_face`@489–499). LAB_HELD `U_BODY_BALANCE` has no `δp` slots (inherited S11c-b); mechanical `δp` on LAB_HELD lives in e_W / face-force, consistent with V being thickness-only.

**Disposition A:** fold map is physically the right substitution. Inherited: FIRST_SHAPE `k`-leg content; ± `face_velocity` sign.

## G. Tautology / operand-theatre

- **§3c increment** is tagged `SELF_ENERGY_INCREMENT`, not `*_RESIDUAL`. Honest export.
- **No** `SELF_ENERGY_ADJOINTNESS_RESIDUAL` in the 154-tag index. The builder’s claim that the blocks are adjoint by construction is **not honest**: for LAB_HELD, THICKNESS→TRANSVERSE increment is identically 0 while TRANSVERSE→THICKNESS is a 268 k-char expression (and the same split appears in the uniform operand). Those blocks are independently extracted (`extract`@326–343); they are not forced adjoint. Suppressing the residual hides a real asymmetry.
- **Ordering commutator, routing, μ_R, traction-sign, N6 independence:** two-route or operand-minus-corrupted-operand; FORM / one-sided tests move them.
- **FLAT_SYMBOL residual 0:** two c1 exports of the same diagonal formula; regression-only, as specified.
- **Traction pairing residual** reduces to `FACE_POWER − TRACTION_POWER` (see D4); not a tautology.
- No physics `assert` precedes a residual emit.

**Disposition G:** increment tagging is honest. **CONFIRMED:** adjoint-by-construction is false.
