# Fixed packet guide

`method.md` is the NEW proposed local/pressure assembly method. `packet-index.json`
records complete copies and exact source excerpt/metadata extraction. No peer
report, prior reviewer commentary or external material is included.

`native/partitions/` contains all five completed source-text partitions. They
include each full native constructor and all children, not selected examples.
The pressure inventory only interpreted its pressure-bearing children; the
local children remain source data. `native/local-source-census.json` is an AST
name/call/hash inventory, not symbolic restoration or proof of a coefficient.
Read it to locate the actual local-only constants and profile/wave jets.

`native/c2.py` supplies wave_jet, WAVE_NAMES, dimensions, Inputs.infer_dimensions
and at_source. `source/composition-worker.py` supplies the existing binding,
grade, native partition, Fourier and epsilon conventions. `native/binding-context.json`
is the actual saved binding context. `physical-input.json` contains all local
constants, including those not used in that saved pressure subset. Its historical
omega=1 and cs=10 are not the proposed omega3/effective-speed binding; the new
method must record that distinction and preserve every other physical value.

`legacy/reduced-action-excerpt.json` is the exact native ReducedActionAssembly
source. It explains why an object called LOCAL_MATRICES after reduction is not
automatically the pre-pressure local partition. It is read-only provenance, not
a request to rerun that constructor. `legacy/numerical-binding.py` shows the
old finite binding route for the same reason; its numerical matrices are not
new-operator evidence.

`pressure/` contains the completed global weak certificate, actual coefficient/
address coverage, outgoing-depth and kernel bounds, Fourier order, both-face
PV/normal certificates and controls. `pressure/method.md` and `pressure/worker.py`
show the inherited analytic argument and executed instrument. No pressure
calculation is proposed again. Runtime joins of its exact scope, fields, grades,
whole definitions and normalization remain required; a status flag alone does
not supply them.

`inventory/` contains native source/consumer grade coverage, whole-tag arguments,
typed direct objects and Fourier/unit provenance needed to join pressure to
the same five native row slots. Exact inherited fields/addresses and original
runtime receipts remain at the pinned source paths in the index. The compact
packet is for the method, not a future worker's complete input manifest.

Local-only material/symbol/grade/jet/endpoint results do not yet exist. Raw local
symbols and syntax are metadata, not an executed coefficient audit. The proposed
instrument must persist its new operands and joins, refuse unknowns, and report
the untouched limits: nonzero constant-height full pressure reduction, incoming
plane waves, inverse, numerical approximation, current/loss, drain and calibration.
