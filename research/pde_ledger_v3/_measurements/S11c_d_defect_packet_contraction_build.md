2026-10-04. Concrete proposed instrument, not executed science. No READY gate
exists. The independently assessed method is unchanged. This worker evaluates
no Fourier transform, response integral, quadrature rule or Gaussian action.

The goal is to certify a continuous reordering of the ordinary J and three
ADDED direct terms using the original kernel assignment AST. The m=k+t route
clips the input k window; the m=l-t route clips output l and reverses the mapped
limits. Both retain the entire m interval [-K-T,K+T], for (27,122) and (29,124).
The worker does not assert equality of different finite quadrature sums.

`worker.py` restores 1,534 complete JSON files (66,888,904 bytes), including all
544 selected addresses and the complete 2,652-entry THETA row, 34 coefficient
polynomials/proofs, 17 complete factor proof sets, 20 template argument/proof
sets, all 544 accepted dimensional input/return pairs and eight saved geometry
plans. It restores the existing rational a and mu operands, and derives only a
new beta/kappa domain join; it never calls their original functions. Original
unit results and source/grade/response calculations remain inherited.
