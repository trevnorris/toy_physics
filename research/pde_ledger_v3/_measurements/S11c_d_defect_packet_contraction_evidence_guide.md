# Packet guide

`method.md` is the new hand-derived proposal. `packet-action-method.md` and
`pressure-readiness-method.md` are the original specifications, supplied for
the unchanged physical target, finite windows, tail/tolerance and unit context.
This proposal supersedes none of their equations or completed results. It asks
for assessment of a new exact order of ordinary J/direct integrations and a
bounded certificate before any new numerical evaluator.

Read these first:

- `original-source/S11c_d_defect_packet_inner_lib.py`: actual
  `kernel_components`, q/profile/phase conventions, separate direct terms and
  original fixed-point numerical scope. No function is called by this proposal.
- `source-contracts.json`: exact extracted source fragments with file, line,
  byte hash and fragment hash. This is newly collected source text, not science.
- `saved/preflight/numeric-factor-adapters.json` and the complete
  `numeric-factor-*-arguments/input/return.json` triplets: all 20 full templates,
  original complete factors and exact old zero joins. Plus/minus pressure
  templates agree; normal templates contain their actual opposite i*q(l) signs.
- `saved/pressure/whole-definitions.json`, `whole-tags.json`, `typed-direct.json`
  and `saved/whole-origin/{H,Jwhole,Dwhole}.json`: closed whole factors versus
  bare density and factored intermediates. Do not insert extra resolvents.
- `saved/preflight/physical-plan.json`, `saved/ends/left-match.json`,
  `saved/physical-input.json` and `saved/native/binding.json`: held omega3 versus
  original omega1, actual selected cs/kappa and W/L/memory/density bindings.

Source/test and unit transport:

- `saved/selected/pressure-addresses.json` includes all 544 entries and their
  complete original maps. `saved/inventory/THETA_BALANCE-ordered-addresses.json`
  is the full 2,652-entry original row. Do not infer numerical nonzero values
  from FORMAL statuses or omit exact-zero entries from the census.
- `saved/pressure/fields.json` and every `saved/field/*-polynomial.json` with its
  full reconstruction input/return: all 34 original coefficient fields.
- `saved/factors/`: all 17 full factor/normal proof operand/input/return sets.
- `accepted-units/complete-template-*.json`, `complete-summand-dimensions.json`
  and every `summand-*-input.json` and `summand-*-return.json`: actual source,
  wave, factor, normal, support and dimensional arguments for all 544 addresses.
  `accepted-units/journal-result.json` states the scope of that completed job;
  it does not clear this new method. Inferred gamma and prior algebra remain
  dependencies. New contraction-unit algebra remains a runtime obligation.
- `saved/native/*`, `saved/inner/physical-H-and-PV-lemma.json` and original
  profile/height/slope input/return records: native signs and profile origins.
  H's analytic expression is context only and is not adopted as a substitute
  for finite-T H by this method.

Bounds and domains:

- `saved/pressure/global-parameter-domain.json`, `profile-envelope.json`,
  `whole-envelopes.json`, `normal-growth.json`, `J-numerator-envelope.json`,
  `D-height-envelope.json`, `D-reflected-envelope.json`, `shift-root-bound.json`:
  original bounded source/response evidence. The compact Fubini estimate in the
  new proposal is a new argument, not a restored machine theorem.
- `preflight/tail-*.json` and other declared tail/radius records: original
  all-real truncation context when present in the index. `packet-index.json`
  lists the exact complete files; no absent file is implied by a wildcard.
- `geometry/*-full-arrangement.json`, corresponding inputs/returns/work-counts,
  `dependency-families.json` and `journal-result.json`: original complete eight
  finite plans and occurrence counts. The new variable maps must transport each
  applicable surface; these old plans are not silently reused as new ones.

Every packet entry is a complete original file unless the index explicitly
labels the newly authored method, prompt, guide, contracts or receipt. Arrays
are not shortened. Duplicate logical evidence paths must contain identical
bytes. `packet-index.json` maps all entries back to their original source path
and hash. The packet does not include prior peer reports, credentials, unrelated
private files, the numerical SQLite banks or a future numerical worker. Excluded
large numerical banks are not necessary to prove a new symbolic change of
variables and are not new test references.
