## Exact geometry

Use the positive basis kappa with square119/20, joined to the saved
sqrt(595)/10. This is Q(sqrt(595)); no decimal coordinate is compared or used as
a cache key. Quad stores a+b*kappa with rational a,b; signs compare exact squares
when the two terms have opposite signs. Inputs reject floats. Saved elapsed-time
metadata floats are preserved separately without entering this arithmetic.

There are8plans: each original carrier (kappa,0), K27 and29 (T122 and124,
respectively, U75 unchanged), and the square or positive-Q height domain.
Square affine boundaries retain l=+-K,+-kappa,-k,-k+-2kappa,k+d, and horizontal
carrier offsets. Required vertical cuts include +-kappa and all carrier offsets.
Offsets are0,+-1/s,+-2/s,+-4/s,+-8/s or0,+-1/L,+-2/L,+-4/L,+-8/L as appropriate.
The height domain has Q=0,U, positive profile offsets and both Q=+(v-k) and
Q=-(v-k) for each root v=+-kappa and each carrier-offset v. Clipping to Q>=0
implements both branches of |v-k| and both consumer arguments k+-Q.

All pairwise crossings, including out-of-box crossings and boundary vertices,
are saved exactly. Every in-box crossing and required vertical cut divides the
k axis. Coalesced lines/cuts retain every original label. On each open slab,
clipped boundaries are sorted at the exact midpoint. All adjacent cells,
endpoint widths and trapezoid areas are saved before audit. The audit separately
checks original line/label incidence, crossing records and cuts, complete ordered
slabs and cells, nonnegative endpoint widths, positive interior widths and
positive areas, and exact full-box area. With all affine crossings split,
positive midpoint order and nonnegative endpoint widths certify constant order
and disjoint interiors. This is an exact finite affine audit, not an evaluated
weak form or measure-theoretic proof.

Missing-collision control removes the actual l=-k+2*kappa line, constructs its
mutant arrangement and verifies it still covers the box, then requires refusal
against the original required incidence. The orientation control reverses the
actual lower/upper indices of a saved baseline cell. Both save operands before
refusal. These are geometry sensitivity controls, not numerical kernel controls.

## Dependencies and counts

The source family retains the complete coefficient, native derivative jet and
wave multiplier (derivative before coefficient multiplication). The consumer
family retains the complete coefficient and bilinear Y=2pi*hat[c*v](-l)
normalization. The response family retains face, slot, component, grade, original
and mapped response, complete native normal and full numeric adapter. Family
interning uses complete canonical JSON equality; a hash labels that exact key
and is not a replacement for equality. Address IDs and grade triples remain.

These are CANDIDATE mathematical families, not a numerical cache. Physical
carrier/center/width, actual mathematical momentum descriptor, unit certificate,
route/precision/contour/radius/tail settings must additionally join for any
future request. Pressure units are pending, so zero actual reuse is authorized
or claimed. In particular the finite banks are not interpolation tables. No
old database is opened or transform/root/rule is recomputed. The fixed inner
bank convenience assembler is never used as an independent complete route.

A24/A48 split each one-dimensional interval at its midpoint and apply one open
rule to each squared half, giving(2n)^2node occurrences per2Dcell and2n per1D
slab. The saved rule counts/precision are joined without reconstructing nodes.
B7/K15's initial physical nested rule contributes at least15^2node occurrences
per2Dcell; adaptive refinement is unknown. This instrument does not execute
Route A or B: their future complete numerical evaluators must independently
construct numerical geometry and evaluated chains from these mathematical
specifications. Immutable source/rule data may be common; samples, Fourier
arrays and inner values may not be shared across routes.

Counts keep flat, height contact, height paired PV, slope, H, J and D separate.
Flat/contact conservatively use every square k slab, with only ONE outer
integral. H and J remain distinct primitive requests within native iteration;
D remains the whole direct addend once. Both k+-Q consumers are counted for the
height branch record. Each primitive includes all active address IDs and all
four target grades. The442zero addresses remain in the dependency table and
are not scheduled for numerical evaluation.

Reported scalar occurrences are not all distinct Fourier arguments. Candidate
X families repeat the same k across cells; different Y branches/nodes may also
coincide, but no unexecuted equality is asserted. Counts show the unshared
addressed workload and conservative candidate-family workload before coordinate
reuse. The conditional number of scalar return records assumes one record per
address/primitive/outer occurrence; a future grouped/vector format may have fewer
records. Full Fourier and inner records, adaptive refinements, serialized bytes
and RSS remain unknown. Prior database sizes are historical context, not a
memory-failure certificate or a throughput prediction. There is no deadline or
automatic abort for slowness.
