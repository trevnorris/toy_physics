# Both-face raw direct increment: instrument scope

This instrument implements the bounded nongrazing portion of the corrected
S11c_d_defect_operator_applicability method. It emits a task-local raw operator,
not production exports. Strict rest bulk, LAB_HELD/RHO4_CONSTANT, omega=3,
edge momenta (1/5,1/10), actual tanh profile, positive effective bulk speed.
Eta and sigma_W remain independent through the rectangular grade selection.

The original upper coefficient, boundary-mode input, tanh transform and selected
integrand, upper closure factor, both-face zero-grade source, full published
pressure-consumer rows and their binding context are restored from pinned JSON.
They are copied byte-identically and are not recalculated by their old functions.
The original native source/extraction metadata and full source hashes remain
pinned. Restoration and equality joins are not independent revalidation.

New work:

1. Derive only the lower boundary using native normal, signed lab height and
   slope and the outgoing extension. Save every amplitude equation and return.
   Join the flat and linear terms to native c1, and the mixed term to the saved
   upper coefficient in outward coordinates. A mirror equality is supplemental.
2. Factor the upper coefficient on the two common-dispersion identities and
   test the complete contact on both faces. All raw residuals are saved before
   exact rational normalization. Nonzero or unresolved is fatal. The raw kernel
   binds all four depth arguments to the same explicit outgoing root; zeros
   remain outside its pointwise domain.
3. Build one ordered raw density with the saved normalized profile factors.
   Compare to the saved selected integrand, without evaluating a middle
   integral. Compare computed dimensions and Fourier/edge reduction to native
   sources and saved declarations. The inherited whole D is never inserted into
   another integration. First-shape iteration remains once.
4. Derive general external closure factors from actual both-face c1 resolvents,
   reference traces and normal jets. Check direct linearity, unchanged iteration,
   cancellation of explicit external depth poles and the saved upper factor.
   These rational identities do not establish the closed physical grazing limit.
5. Insert both-face pressure and normal-jet changes into the full published
   native consumer rows before selecting the mixed grade. Reuse both saved
   zero-grade source operators only after their physical binding/cs-independence
   joins. Emit raw row densities, actual source addresses and separate kernel
   factors. Source derivatives remain at the source coordinate; this is an
   operator density, not a field solution or an on-shell excitation.
6. Execute wrong lab-height sign, native slope omission, wrong sheet and double
   insertion controls. Carry lower omission and double insertion through both
   actual scalar rows. A normal-jet sign control is explicitly a trace control;
   the zero-grade jet consumer may vanish. Preserve actual lower flat/zero-jet
   checks, and do not regenerate constant-end modes/currents.

Exact-match applicability is deliberately UNRESOLVED in this instrument. The
method's later two-sided closed collision limit and integrable local bound are
still required before an exact-match finite matrix. The present raw result is
restricted to nonzero external qi and qo and nonzero resolvent/trace denominators.
Internal square-root branches and their coincident cases are identified, not
numerically integrated. No integral, finite solve, loss number, primitive
calibration or drain response is supplied. A new obstruction is reported as a
method finding rather than suppressed by changing a tolerance.

Execution is not launched by this plan or by method clearance. The worker
requires actual source/build assessment and a gate joining worker, manifest,
method record, build record and actual shared guard/supervisor hashes. Resource
policy: 4 GiB cgroup and native address space within the 16 GiB pool, zero swap,
one assigned CPU and one native thread, tasks32, host reserve4GiB, desktop
priority, no wall/native/CPU/inactivity deadline. The existing supervisor and
hook-first launcher must be used. No fallback or automatic scientific retry.

The 14 initial standard-library tests check startup order, native snippet
namespace routing and immutable input/partial-result persistence. They do not
import SymPy, restore scientific operands or validate the new algebra. Source
review must assess the actual instrument; runtime acceptance still requires all
operands, controls, source posthashes, stderr and resource evidence.
