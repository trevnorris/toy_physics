## 4. Ordered rectangular composition and a complete address census

Let F_f,h be the saved reference-pressure response at grade h. Keep its pieces
separate: flat diagonal; first height with contact/PV; first slope; the two
mixed iteration assignments including trace subtraction; and the closed direct
whole convolution. The native jet response is N_f,h=i*f*q(l) F_f,h.

The retained pressure-dependent contribution is the ORDERED composition

    Delta R_r,g = sum_f sum_{a+b+c=g}
        [ M_{c_rf,a^p} F_f,b S_f,c
          + M_{c_rf,a^n} (i*f*q(l) F_f,b) S_f,c ].

M denotes multiplication at the output spatial point. Source coefficients
multiply their wave derivatives before the face response. A source/consumer
multiplier cannot be commuted through F. At grade (1,1) there are nine grade
triples, at (1,0)/(0,1) three each, and at (0,0) one, before native zero entries
are removed. Enumerate all 16 triples for every applicable face/slot/channel;
retain explicit zeros and reasons. A sorted index generated only from nonzero
terms is not sufficient coverage evidence.

The direct F_(1,1) contribution occurs only with source(0,0) and consumer(0,0).
Its inherited whole convolution is tagged as whole and enters once. The mixed
iteration is also included once; no extra symmetrization or second full-weight
transfer assignment. No whole convolution is passed through the native second
slot's middle integral. The internal middle variable in the original response
is distinct from new source/consumer momentum convolutions.

An address records: native row and child/hash; face; pressure or normal slot;
target/consumer/response/source grades; source channel and exact wave-jet name;
profile coefficient and arguments; response component and saved operand hash;
all integration variables and their role; dimension and epsilon count; zero,
available or unresolved status; applicability obligations. Reconstruct the full
retained pressure part as a formal noncommuting operator expression and compare
to direct substitution of independent response placeholders in the actual row.
This is an algebraic consistency check, not independent derivation of physics.

## 5. One-dimensional Fourier map with separate momenta

Use the actual saved profiles w(x/L)=(1+tanh(x/L))/2 and
m(x/L)=(1-tanh(x/L)^2)/3. Translate supplied dimensionless profile jets with
the native L scaling: w1_profile_d1 represents L*d_x w, higher all-1 jets carry
L^n*d_x^n; transverse profile derivatives vanish for this selected 1D profile.
Check actual native conventions against Inputs.at_source/dx and the original
profile bindings. Do not replace a native background derivative by an ordinary
derivative of an independently graded formula in a way that silently enforces
sigma=eta*W/L. Preserve that bookkeeping distinction.

For any complete coefficient function b(x), define its normalized transform

    b_hat(t) = (1/(2*pi)) integral exp(-i*t*x) b(x) dx.

This is an unevaluated distributional definition. Constants give b*delta(t).
Products of profile factors are transformed as a whole; b_hat*d_hat at the
same momentum is NOT the transform of b*d. A transform of a product may be
stored as a single tagged object, avoiding an invented product of distributions.
No new tanh integral is evaluated. The inherited height contact and PV remain
explicit rather than replaced by sampled values.

Let p be the original incident wave momentum, k the face-response input,
l its output and r the final row-output momentum. For a source jet D^alpha,
its wave multiplier d_alpha(p) uses (p,1/5,1/10) and temporal factor -i*omega.
The Fourier route of an address is

    integral dl dk c_hat(r-l) F_f,b(l,k)
                    s_hat(k-p) d_alpha(p).

For the normal slot, put i*f*q(l) immediately before F, not q(r) or q(p).
Keep the native normalized inverse/edge-delta convention: compositions above
use plain dk dl; a constant multiplier reduces by its delta without another
2*pi factor. Confirm this against the supplied kernel_apply phases and saved
edge-measure contracts. The source derivative acts on the wave at p before
coefficient multiplication, not on the total post-convolution momentum k.
All point variables and any middle response variable are alpha-distinct.

This formula defines an ordered Fourier route, not its value or a permitted
interchange of conditionally convergent PV integrals. Use the original position
space multiplication/derivative order as the definition. Contacts/PV in the
response and nondecaying profile transforms retain their distribution tags.
Do not infer global well-definedness or a delta->0 limit of this composite
from compact response certificates. Mark each global-momentum obligation as
UNRESOLVED. No hidden projection onto [-3,3] is introduced. A future numerical
