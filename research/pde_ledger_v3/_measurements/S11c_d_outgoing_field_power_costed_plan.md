## Shortest candidate method

1. **Premises and localization first.** Use the cleared transverse/face results
   where actually available. If omega=3 was not reached by its partial grid,
   the later pilot may perform only that missing point's transverse checks,
   within its own reviewed scope and budget. Never replay completed checks or
   run an H-mode classification campaign. Match the LEFT/RIGHT transverse states
   through the amplitude grades needed for the claimed power. Determine from
   source operands whether every loss-producing baseline drive vanishes, and
   whether asymptotic matching leaves a finite localized loss-side source.
   Keep density and thickness grades, endpoint terms and half-line operands.
   Persistent uniform-end loss or an unsupported finite pairing is an early
   no-go; it is not repaired by subtracting two divergent work integrals.
2. **Complete the first-order field source and maps.** Form the actual slab,
   kinematic, closure and exterior inhomogeneities from accepted native action/
   grade operands. Preserve source order, units, Fourier measure and phases.
   Reconstruct `a1=C0 Psi1+C1 Psi0` on both physical faces. Establish the physical
   loss-sector projection and its current/work joins; a coordinate deletion or
   zero end off-diagonal block is insufficient. Source-work reduction is usable
   only when it retains all induced boundary work and direct exterior drive.
3. **Attempt a fixed-source real-axis Fourier evaluation.** Apply the existing
   algebraic inverse to those complete sources with the actual physical
   outgoing depth branch. Split quadrature at the two acoustic branch endpoints
   and use source-specific removable/endpoint limits. Test contributing real
   poles and large-momentum tails. Transverse elastic singular terms may leave
   the loss pairing only after their zero loss projection is established. An
   unsupported contributing pole, branch prescription or distribution product
   stops this route; it does not start a new root, complex-frequency or global
   Green-operator project. Keep physical dissipation throughout. The goal is
   the Fourier field and power on these sources, not a universal diagonal kernel.
4. **Compute power in two physically joined ways.** Contract the complete
   source work with the outgoing first-order field using the actual row/port
   maps. Compare it with reconstructed outward non-transverse boundary flux,
   both exterior half-space powers and dissipation inside the same control
   volume. Retain non-transverse end flux when present. Establish stationary
   storage and no unaccounted held-background work before calling the result
   nonnegative loss. Count a converted watt once; do not add its later absorption
   again. The leading loss-side quadratic power is valid only after the zero
   baseline and regular-domain premises above. Do not compute it as one minus
   first-order transmission or with a guessed Euclidean Im(f-dagger G f).
