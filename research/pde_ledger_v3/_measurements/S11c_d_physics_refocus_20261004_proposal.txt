  L_T(a;lambda) = 1 - J_T,out(all reflected and transmitted transverse modes)/J_T,in.
Here a specifies the incident transverse polarization; all reflected transverse
power survives. This is not forward extinction, a temporal decay rate, or a
bound-state capture probability. A bulk-only fraction P_bulk,out/J_T,in is a
separate observable. Interface absorption and non-transverse escape may enter
the total, but must not count the same converted energy twice. A stationary
balance must include any external work of the held profile; nonnegative loss
is not an assumption. Do not infer a loss coefficient from the norm, imaginary
part, or absolute square of the current complex-bilinear Gaussian test.
