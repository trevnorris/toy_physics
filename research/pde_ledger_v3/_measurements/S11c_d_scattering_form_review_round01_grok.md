## Findings

### Amendment blockers

**A1. Retained survival export still carries the N13 confinement identity after the bound companion is removed.**

v10:

> `N13`: "confinement of light" = **survival of the transverse polarization channel**. Conversion into a **bound** breathing/thickness mode kills the photon **exactly as** bulk radiation does — the two are **distinct emitted objects**  
> (`S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:481–483`)

> This is the `N13` confinement object; continuum `T→H` conversion and bound spectral overlap are emitted beside it, not substituted for it.  
> (`S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:535–537`)

The amendment keeps `S11CD_TRANSVERSE_SURVIVAL_FUNCTIONAL` as a required computed export (`amendment:259`) and defers `S11CD_BOUND_SPECTRAL_OVERLAP` (`amendment:264–265`). It only says the amendment itself does not claim that two-mechanism confinement is settled (`amendment:37`, `:237–239`). That sentence constrains the amendment’s prose. It does not retarget the retained tag, whose v10 identity is the N13 confinement object and whose companion overlap is no longer emitted beside it.

At the saved development point the open thickness selector has rank zero and finite current ratios sit at `0.9999995945`–`1.0000001045` (`S11c_d_remaining_case_flux_report.md:9–12`; `S11c_d_remaining_case_response_report.md:26–27`). A near-unity survival number on that domain is current accounting among open transverse channels.

**Why the present wording does not already cover this.** “Do not claim the complete two-mechanism confinement question is settled” is a prohibition on a sentence in the amendment. The export table still requires the object v10 names as that confinement object, with domain/availability carried as metadata rather than a change of identity.

**Minimal correction.** State that the retained survival functional is the continuum-channel flux ratio on the declared `(ω,k_∥,profile,channel-availability)` domain; that it is not the complete N13 two-mechanism confinement object; and that a near-unity value on a domain with no open thickness-like conversion channel does not establish confinement.

**A2. §3.3 lets bulk-depth radiation pay for the thickness-channel FORM.**

The amendment first splits two availability questions:

> There are **two distinct availability questions**: propagating thickness-like channels of the full end pencil, and outgoing bulk-depth radiation with its own current/measure. Neither is inferred solely from the other.  
> (`amendment:157–160`)

The coverage requirement then asks for

> an admissible real-frequency witness with the required incoming transverse and outgoing thickness/radiating channel  
> (`amendment:177–178`)

and a “relevant radiation measure” (`amendment:181`).

v10’s conversion FORM is the thickness-channel current of the full end pencil:

```text
C_{T→H}[a] ≡ J_H,out[a] / J_T,in[a] .
```

(`S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:459–465`)

> Total mechanical energy and bare bulk Poynting are not substitutes for the channel-resolved currents.  
> (`S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:620–622`)

The saved record already had to separate those objects: closed thickness amplitudes are matching data; depth-integrated bulk current is not depth escape (`S11c_d_continuum_currents_report.md:6–18`; `S11c_d_remaining_case_flux_report.md:24`). A bulk-depth Poynting witness, or a radiating T-channel loss that never opens `J_H`, can satisfy the slash while leaving the A9 thickness projection untested. Direct T→bulk radiation through the closed DtN is a different mechanism from `C_{T→H}`.

The symbolic fallback (“symbolically checked FORM with no open-channel numerical validation”, `amendment:190–191`) can also be read as a closed-channel identity. “Nonempty channel domain” is in the same paragraph, but “generic-domain” is not locked to the open `J_H` branch.

**Why the present wording does not already cover this.** §3.2’s distinction is immediately reused as `thickness/radiating`. That is an evidence substitution path, not a reminder.

**Minimal correction.** Require the non-vacuous A9 check to use a nonempty outgoing **thickness-like channel of `𝓛_±^full`** and the §3a `J_H` current. Treat bulk-depth radiation as a separate measure that does not discharge the thickness FORM. Lock the symbolic fallback to that same open-channel current branch (analytic in the channel projectors/currents), so a rank-zero specialization cannot pay. Keep the second numerical frequency labelled **new**.
