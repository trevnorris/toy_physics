## Findings

**F1. Blocking. The amendment identifies two different bulk-current objects.**

`S11c_d_SCATTERING_FORM_AMENDMENT.md:247-250` says v10’s end-channel current “already includes its closed/nonlocal bulk contribution: the depth-integrated interface-normal bulk tail is a component of that current, not another loss to add to it.”

v10’s sentence is narrower. `J` is the S11b quadratic energy current evaluated on the reduced closed operator, “including its closed/nonlocal bulk contribution” (`S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:440-450`). That is the slab bilinear of the operator that already contains the nonlocal bulk self-energy. It is not, by that sentence, an explicit integral of the bulk field’s interface-normal Poynting through the exterior depth.

The saved bookkeeping kept those pieces separate: “separately for slab and depth-integrated bulk normal current. The latter is not a bulk-depth escape flux” (`S11c_d_continuum_currents_report.md:14-16`). The second clause is right and should stay. The colon in the amendment goes further and treats the explicit depth integral as already identical to the closed-operator contribution.

That identity is load-bearing for `C_{T→H}` and for survival, because both are ratios of these currents. Implementing the sentence as written can drop the explicit depth-integrated normal current, or add it on top of a bilinear that already contains some bulk exchange. Later text says to derive the geometries before a total-loss interpretation (`S11c_d_SCATTERING_FORM_AMENDMENT.md:251-252`). That governs the loss split. It does not require an emitted residual between the S11b bilinear and the explicit depth integral, so it does not already block the false inclusion.

Minimal correction: until a derived identity is emitted with both operands, keep the slab modal current and the depth-integrated interface-normal bulk current as separately typed contributions to the normalized end current. State which sum is the channel current, and under what factor. Do not add the interface-normal tail to outward bulk-depth escape. Do not drop it, and do not add it twice, on the ground that v10’s “closed/nonlocal bulk contribution” already is that integral. Outward bulk-depth flux remains the distinct A12 observable.
