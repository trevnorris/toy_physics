## Numbered findings

### Blockers

**F1. The two required continuum roots have no interaction-region partition, so they can be claimed as independent N13 continuum kills.**

v10 emits one continuum photon-kill object:

> “(i) continuum conversion — transverse → the thickness **continuum** / bulk escape (the §3a amplitude projected on radiating/continuum channels and evaluated with each converted channel’s own current).”  
> `directives/S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:485–486`

Section 3a defines that current on full-end thickness channels along the interface-normal:

> “`J_{H,\mathrm{out}}[a] ≡ Σ_{e∈{−,+}} s_e 𝓙_{n,H,e,\mathrm{out}}[S_{H,e←T}a]`”  
> `directives/S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:459–465`

N13’s two distinct emitted objects are continuum conversion and bound capture (`directives/S11c_decisions.md:130–133`). The amendment splits the continuum object into two required computed roots and rewrites the consumer contract:

> “A9 must also deliver a distinct bulk-depth outgoing flux FORM…”  
> `directives/S11c_d_SCATTERING_FORM_AMENDMENT.md:134–137`

> “The definitions and consumer contracts of `S11CD_CONTINUUM_CONVERSION`, `S11CD_CONTINUUM_T_TO_H_FLUX_FUNCTIONAL` and `C_{T→H}` explicitly mean **thickness end-channel conversion only**. They exclude bulk-depth escape and closure dissipation as separately attributed losses… They are not the total continuum-loss functional.”  
> `directives/S11c_d_SCATTERING_FORM_AMENDMENT.md:631–638`

For a localized interface the physically consistent reading is one control volume: interface-normal faces carry end-channel `J_H`, exterior depth faces carry bulk-depth Poynting, and the closed-operator survival deficit is the same accounting’s remainder after dissipation/forcing/truncation. The draft never states that partition.

Existing wording does not already cover it. “Never add the interface-normal tail to that escape flux” (`amendment:287–288`) forbids one wrong summand, the saved depth-integrated `y_n` bulk current. “Do not infer bulk escape as `1−P_{T,\mathrm{surv}}−C_{T→H}`” (`amendment:266`) forbids using the deficit as a substitute for A12. “Even together, the retained end conversion, bulk escape and survival do not bound total N13 loss” (`amendment:649–651`) forbids treating the sum as a complete confinement bound. None of those sentences forbids binding `C_{T→H}` and the bulk-depth fraction as two independent N13 continuum-kill channels, which would change v10’s one continuum object into an additive pair.

Minimal correction: in §2 and in the §5 consumer contracts, state that the two FORMs are the interface-normal and exterior-depth surface fluxes of one localized-interaction control volume; that they are complementary parts of one continuum outgoing accounting together with the closed-operator survival deficit; and that a consumer may add them as N13 continuum loss only under that partition.

**F2. Supported leading bulk-depth quadratic power can still be built from the historical first-shape integrand that omits it, or classed as deferred second-shape completion.**

v10 already isolates the leading induced quadratic of first-order amplitudes:

> “Under a computed disposition that removes the relevant baseline/interference slots, the leading induced-field `B_0[a_1,a_1]` term does not require an uncomputed second-order amplitude.”  
> `directives/S11c_d_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:589–593`

The amendment keeps that result in current d work:

> “a computed baseline/interference disposition can establish a leading induced quadratic from first-order amplitudes without a second-order amplitude. That supported result remains current d work; quadratic power alone is not a deferral reason.”  
> `directives/S11c_d_SCATTERING_FORM_AMENDMENT.md:241–243`

The supplied c1 far-field construction, which the amendment points at as the restricted energy source, drops exactly that quadratic. Original lines 51–56 of `scripts/S11c_c1_bulk_closure_sympy_audit.py` define

> `poynting_through_first_shape = Re( p_flat v_flat* + p_flat v_scattered* + p_scattered v_flat* )/2`

and omit `p_scattered v_scattered*`. The call site (original lines 98–99) evaluates both legs at the same propagating `qprop`. That is a first-shape expansion of *total* power through `O(η)`. On a vanishing baseline it is not the retained-order bulk-flux FORM.

> “A flat evanescent channel is a nullspace of the zeroth-order Hermitian form; the curvature-induced radiated power there is `O(η²)`, exactly the term the first-shape-order truncation omits… test the power identity through `O(η)`… on its nullspace emit `NOT_ESTABLISHED_AT_FIRST_SHAPE_ORDER` (the `O(η²)` leakage there belongs to S11c-e).”  
> `directives/S11c_c1_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:332–338`

The amendment applies that caveat to deferred completion:

> “The [c1 first-shape power caveat] and its [carry-forward record] leave the evanescent-nullspace second-shape completion unestablished… This amendment does not require d to construct the missing higher-order bulk closure or true-area completion to turn a retained diagnostic into complete physical power.”  
> `directives/S11c_d_SCATTERING_FORM_AMENDMENT.md:245–250`

Existing wording does not already cover the trap. “The existing c1 far-field energy construction has a specified restricted subcase; its presence alone does not establish the general solved-state map” (`amendment:160–162`) is about existence of a general map, not about the integrand content of the flux functional. “Missing higher-order terms are handled below, not tested as if they were zeros” (`amendment:204–205`) addresses omitted operator/true-area terms, not the quadratic of retained-order outgoing fields. “Quadratic power alone is not a deferral reason” forbids deferring because a term is quadratic; it does not forbid copying the historical integrand that drops `B_0[a_1,a_1]`, nor classing that dropped piece as A14 second-shape completion. On a computed vanishing baseline, that implementation exports a zero or an `O(η)` interference-only bulk coefficient and treats the actual leading physical conversion as unavailable.

Minimal correction: distinguish three objects in §2: (i) c1’s first-shape expansion of total power, which omits `|a_1|^2` and remains a restricted identity check; (ii) the retained-order bulk-depth FORM as the derived current bilinear on the retained-order outgoing field, including `B_0[a_1,a_1]`, which remains current d work when that is the leading supported coefficient; (iii) omitted second-shape corrections to `Z`, true-area, and evanescent-nullspace radiation, which stay at the named S11c-e boundary.
