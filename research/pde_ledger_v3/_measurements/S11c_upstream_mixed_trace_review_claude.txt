## Substantive findings

**S1. The mechanism is correct, and the outcome is largely fixed algebraically.**
- Write M = I + cZ and M′ = I + c(Z+A), where A has only a [0,2] entry, ησD. Then R′−R = M′⁻¹ A M⁻¹.
- Because A is a corner entry and M is upper-triangular, this gives δ[0,2] = ησD / (m₀₀ m₂₂) and all other entries zero.
- The middle leg (z_middle, Z01, Z12, m₁₁) drops out. So the multiplier is independent of the middle variable. Both external response factors are retained, and no second convolution appears.
- The inverse coefficient matches the native source. `DELTA_P` has ordering R·Z·source (`S11c_c1…L834-860`), and the native record gives c = Λ_A ρ_m⁻² /(1−iωτ_A) (`native.json`, response record).
- At the saved point, c = 1/(1−0.3i), Z₀₀ = √3 and Z₂₂ = 3/2. I computed these by hand from the inherited depths. Both m's are finite and nonzero.

**S2. The trace map cannot change the physical difference at this point. State that in the scope.**
- The native `face_shift` trace is p + ½W₀η w₁ ∂p. With the w₁ factor set to zero, `height_constant` is 0 and the value coefficient is 1 (c2 L615 trace_three).
- So T has a unit diagonal, and T⁻¹ row 0 times a column-2-only δ leaves δ_ref[0,2] = δ[0,2].
- The jet change is exactly i·q_out times that (L297 versus c2 `jet_second`).
- I found no missing term for this selected comparison. The face shift has no σ dependence, and the η·σ pieces that go through T[0,1] and R[1,2] are identical in "old" and "new". The Taylor η² term is out of grade.
- Consequence: a nonzero reference/jet factor mostly restates a nonzero physical factor. It is not independent evidence that the coordinate maps preserve the term. Add this sentence to the scope so the result isn't over-read.

**S3. The source channel is only partly reported (L314–319).**
- `response_factor` is the response multiplier times the V coefficient only. At the saved point Λ_V₀ = 0, so that coefficient is exactly 1.
- The native source also has a μ_θ term, Λ_A μ_s/ρ_m. It is nonzero because Λ_A = 1/100. It is not emitted.
- Smallest fix: also emit the multiplier times ∂source/∂`s11cc1_mu_theta_lab_held_plus`. Alternatively, rename the quantities "per unit V, μ-channel unreported". As written, "physicalPressureFactor" overstates what is computed.

**S4. The fixed-edge multiplier is valid. A downstream caution belongs in the scope.**
- m₀₀ is evaluated at the output leg and m₂₂ at the input leg. Both come from the saved endpoints (Q, edge) and (0, edge), and neither depends on the saved t or q_t.
- The diagonal delta→1 convention matches native `kernel_bridge`, so no extra measure or delta appears.
- However, c2 `kernel_apply` (L15–16) integrates the `second` slot over MIDDLE again. A D that already contains its own t-integral and two edge deltas must not be routed through that path unchanged.
- Add one sentence to the scope's handoff paragraph.

## Local tooling

**T1 (blocking). The native matrix will fail with a NameError.**
- Worker L220 provides `leftmap` and `rightmap`. The native `z_three` fragment (c2 L43–44, also quoted in `native-direct-slot.json`) uses `left_map` and `right_map`. So `exec(native_matrix, ns)` at L222 raises NameError after a few journal files are written.
- Fix: rename the two keys.
- The stdlib tests can't catch this because they never execute the fragments. Optional guard: for `z_three`, `three_inverse` and `trace_three`, compare the fragment's free Name loads against the `ns` or `context` keys.
- The `three_inverse` and `trace_three` contexts already match. I checked their names by hand.

**T2. The "independent" resolvent identity uses the wrong order (L265–267).**
- It computes M⁻¹A·M′⁻¹. The correct form is M′⁻¹·A·M⁻¹.
- The two agree here only because A is a rank-one corner entry. Swap the order so the identity is correct in general.

**T3. Some guards run before the results are emitted.**
- The `require` at L323 comes before `physical-factors` is emitted (L334).
- The `require`s at L351–352 come before `response-status` and `controls` are emitted (L353–364).
- Move all `require`s after the corresponding emissions, to match the stated "emission before guards" rule.
- The middle-domain `require` isn't needed for this difference at all, since m₁₁ cancels. Make it emit-only or run it last.

**T4 (optional). Strengthen the joins and controls.**
