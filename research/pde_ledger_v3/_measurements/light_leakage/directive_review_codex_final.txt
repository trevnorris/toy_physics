# Verdict: NEEDS CHANGE

## Findings

1. **Part 2.1 misclassifies scope caveats and an unrelated open requirement as leakage routes.**  
   Directive: “Start from … `S11_stray_longitudinal.md:74–76` … `:64` … and `R-S8-02`.”  
   Records: S11 merely says decoupling is *not established* outside its tested scope; d:64 says nothing transfers automatically to live conversion; `R-S8-02` explicitly concerns the scalar `(u_L,h)` block and “not directly” the transverse count. Meanwhile S11c-b/c1 name actual couplings and bulk-radiation objects.  
   **Fix:** Seed the inventory from S11c-b:54–57, c1:35–44, and c2:63–70. Give every item a status—equation-level route, candidate beyond scope, or open/non-result—and retain the cited S11/d/R-S8 passages only in the latter categories.

2. **Part 1’s required “one class” is not a partition, and its observable bookkeeping conflicts with the records.**  
   Directive: choose one of “DIFFERENT MECHANISM” or “REQUIREMENT,” then label “redirects.” A model analog can simultaneously differ from fiber physics *and* be observationally constrained. Also, redirection is not necessarily leakage: the closeout says reflected and transmitted transverse light survives.  
   **Fix:** Split this into independent columns: model mapping (`ABSENT/PRESENT ANALOG/NOT ADDRESSED`), record status (`ESTABLISHED/CONDITIONAL/OPEN`), observational constraint, and destination (`same transverse branch`, `other brane branch`, `bulk`, or frequency exchange).

3. **The estimate instruction contradicts M3.**  
   Directive: obtain a prior-art estimate “that could bound” each route while computing nothing. M3 defines an oracle as something checked against an independently computed result and forbids assuming its result for this object. With no model-side calculation, the external formula cannot yet be a bound.  
   **Fix:** Request only oracle candidates: exact equation, assumptions, applicability domain, and parameter correspondence. Prohibit calling one a v3 bound until the future bracket independently derives the corresponding v3 quantity.

4. **The inventory does not name a common bracket object or exposure measure.**  
   The record’s existing observable is a normalized transverse-current deficit in which reflection and transmission survive; it is strongly profile-, frequency-, momentum-, anchoring-, and regime-dependent. The directive requests parameters and broad dimming/reddening/redirection yardsticks but no route-to-observable normalization, accumulation law, exposure/path/event count, validity regime, or parameter correlations. Friedland–Giannotti specifically analyzes metastable photons in plasma and stellar cooling, not a generic “number of charge encounters.” [APS source](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.100.031602)  
   **Fix:** Require a route-by-regime matrix containing source/destination branch, local coefficient or per-event probability, exposure variable, frequency/polarization dependence, validity domain, parameter correlations, survival convention, and an applicable bound—or an explicit gap.

5. **“Every known loss mechanism” has no achievable closure criterion, and one starting citation conflates two papers.**  
   Directive: “Cover every known…” and “Marcuse … (1970), on taper and imperfection loss.” Publisher records identify the taper paper as 1970 but the surface-imperfection paper as 1969. [1970 taper](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1970.tb01769.x), [1969 imperfections](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1969.tb01742.x)  
   **Fix:** Define a bounded taxonomy and search-stopping rule, and split Marcuse into exact title/year/DOI entries.
