   **Location:** inventory line 86, validity column for Eq. (8).

   **Inventory evidence**, from:
   ```bash
   nl -ba _scratch/light_leakage/light_leakage_scoping.md | sed -n '86p'
   ```
   It supplies:
   ```text
   Γ₀^vac = c_n m (m/k)^n
   ```
   and says “A perturbation that ‘lifts the zero mode, `0 → E′ ≡ m²/2k²`’, makes it metastable”. It labels Eq. (8) a “rest-frame barrier-penetration estimate”, but states no smallness domain.

   **Source evidence:** the derivation specifies “an infinitely small perturbation”, approximates the interior solution using “the unperturbed function”, and discusses “energies ≪ k”. [Primary preprint, §II around Eqs. (8)–(9)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.2164).

   **What is wrong:** the displayed power-law rate comes from a small perturbative lift and low-energy tunnelling approximation. From `E′ = m²/(2k²)`, the corresponding fixed-`n` small-lift condition is `m/k ≪ 1`. That is an inference from the quoted derivation, not an additional v3 result.

   Without this condition, a later parameter scan could test the power law outside its applicable regime.

   **Fix:** add the small-lift/low-`m/k` domain explicitly to Eq. (8)’s validity column. Keep the existing statements that its parameters have no established v3 correspondence and that it supplies no inherited bound.
