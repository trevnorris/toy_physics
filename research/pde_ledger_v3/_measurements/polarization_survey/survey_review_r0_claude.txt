**1. §4 E01 is marked "Reproduced" but leaves out a departure that its own source records. The survey's conflict rule is also stricter than the task's.**
- **Survey.** The E01 row says: "two exactly transverse in-plane directions … not … a complete slab census. The h inclusion remains **OPEN**". Line 179 adds: "Apparent conflict requires an experimentally comparable model prediction."
- **Sources** (lookups file):
  - S10:249–251: "Maxwell has no counterpart to this surviving zero-frequency longitudinal direction. That is a characterised departure".
  - S11:15–16: "Lifts S10's zero to a propagating longitudinal mode … closes the homogeneous three-finite-mode census".
  - S11:173: "A second cone is only a departure if matter COUPLES to it".
  - SUBSTRATE_REQUIREMENTS:135–136: "`D_brane = 3` went in and `D−1 = 2` came out … an assumption restated, ⛔ not a result."
- **What is wrong.** With B_comp postulated, the homogeneous brane carries three propagating modes. The ledger calls the third one a departure from Maxwell, conditional on whether matter couples to it. The E01 row omits this, so a reader leaves thinking the model, like light, has exactly two. The task defined the class as "in apparent conflict, quoting both sides". The survey added a requirement for an experimentally comparable prediction, which the task did not ask for.
- **Fix.** Split E01 into two rows:
  - (a) The transverse pair: reproduced, conditional on D=3 as an input, isotropic inertia, the in-plane field content and μ≥0.
  - (b) "No third radiating mode": apparent conflict, conditional on matter coupling (S11:161–163, 173), quoting both sides.
  - At minimum, put these quotes in the E01 row and mark the status unclear.

**2. E10/E11 and E13–E15 are held to a different standard than E01.**

**5. A load-bearing experiment is missing: the thermal count of radiating modes.**
- **Survey.** E01 says "Tomography assuming this space is not a model-independent census". E02 says the bound "does not measure the amplitude of every conceivable longitudinal material wave".

- **Fix.** Add an entry classed testable. The coupling and c_L are open (S11:161–163), and B_comp is postulated.

**6. Part 1 does not give the quantum reason light has two polarization states.**
- **Survey,** line 48: "Photon 'spin 1' therefore does not mean three freely propagating vacuum polarization states."
- **What is missing.** The reason is that the photon is massless. A massive spin-1 particle has three states, which is what E02/E03 bound. The classical side is also missing: in Maxwell's theory the longitudinal field is fixed by charge through Gauss's law and is not a mode. Without both points the user cannot see why S10:249–251 calls a retained zero-frequency longitudinal direction a departure.
- **Fix.** Add two sentences, labelled quantum and classical.
