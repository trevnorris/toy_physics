   194	## Reference and bulk input objects
   195	
   196	- **`GM`** (owned by the gravity sector). The far-field Newtonian mass parameter, as measured by the orbits of
   197	  slowly moving test matter. It is an independent symbol, not identified with any profile amplitude. Every
   198	  Part B and Part C condition is stated relative to it.
   199	- **GR reference objects (supplied comparison inputs), in PPN form with `c₀`:**
   200	
   201	  ```
   202	  Δθ_ref(b;γ) ≡ (1+γ)·2GM/(b c₀²) ,
   203	  Δt_RT,log,ref(b;γ) ≡ 2(1+γ)(GM/c₀³)·ln(4 r_E r_R/b²) ,
   204	  γ_GR ≡ 1 .
   205	  ```
   206	
   207	  The logarithmic reference has domain `Z_E, Z_R ≫ b`; Part B uses only its logarithmic slope, defined
   208	  as for `𝒮_RT` (observable 2). These equations define the oracle objects to compare against. They do not specify
   209	  either observable computed from the supplied brane dispersion, or an acceptance test.
   210	  The reference family defines each effective `γ`; GR residuals and matching conditions use its
   211	  supplied `γ_GR` member.
   212	- **Part C bulk (supplied):**
   213	  - `P = Kρ^n`, with `n` symbolic, `ρ` the bulk number density and `m` the particle mass (v3 convention). So
   214	    `c_s² = nKρ^(n−1)/m`.
   215	  - Define the fractional bulk-density change at the brane by
   216	    `f(x) ≡ δρ(x)/ρ₀ = ρ(x)/ρ₀ − 1`. It is a symbolic radial profile owned by the gravity sector or S12.
   217	    `ρ₀` is the asymptotic bulk number density, `c_s0` is the corresponding asymptotic sound speed, and Part C
   218	    keeps first order in `f`.
