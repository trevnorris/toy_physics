# S10 paper card — decision list

## What is being built

`research/pde_ledger_v3/paper/steps/S10_two_transverse_photons.tex` (299 lines at HEAD), rebuilt against
`research/pde_ledger_v3/steps/S10_two_transverse_photons.md`.

⭐⭐ **The step record is the AUTHORITY.** It is closed, and every number and cited locus in it was verified
by three independent passes. ⛔ Do not re-derive anything. ⛔ Do not open an engine transcript, a comparator
output, or an engine source to check the record. If the card and the record disagree, **the record wins**.

## ⭐⭐ THE PROPERTY — there is one, and every item below is it applied

> **Every CLAIM the card makes must be licensed by the record, at the same strength.**

- Where the record **refuses** a claim, the card refuses it.
- Where the record **qualifies** a claim, the card carries the qualification **in the same breath** —
  ⛔ not deferred to a later limitations list.
- Where the record is **weaker** than the card, the record still wins. ⭐ Report the case; ⛔ do not resolve
  it yourself.

### ⭐⭐ CLAIM vs EXPOSITION — and the hard case is decided here, ⛔ not left to judgement

The card may explain the **supplied** action, its algebra, and the geometry at whatever length reads well.
That is what a paper card is for, and the record contains none of it.

⭐⭐ **The line: an exposition may derive a property OF THE SUPPLIED ACTION. It may not present that
property as the REASON the measured result is what it is.** The moment a passage walks a reader from a
feature of the action to a measured count, it has made a claim, however it is punctuated — and the
record's control result on **that** attribution belongs at **that** site, ⛔ not in a later bullet.

⛔ **A passage does not become exposition by sitting inside a block labelled as one.**

## What must become true

1. ⭐⭐ **The card's account of what produces the count IS the record's account** — wherever the record's
   controls bear on a proposed attribution, the card carries their result, ⛔ whatever direction that
   result runs. ⚠ **Do not assume the record hands you a single feature that produces the count.** Read
   what the controls actually settle and say that; if what they settle is negative, the card says the
   negative thing. ⛔ **Do not manufacture a cause to fill this item.**
2. The card states **every condition the record says the count rests on**, and states them where the count
   is stated. ⭐ This includes the record's own statement about what the two engines' agreement is and is
   not evidence for — the record says where that one belongs; put it there.
3. **Every number in the card is one the record reports, cited to the locus the record cites for it.**
   ⭐⭐ A path that resolves is **not** a source: the cited locus must contain the object. ⛔ **Deleting a
   number is not repairing it** — where the record states a live disposition, the card states it.
4. The card carries **what the record says S10 does not establish**, including the interpretation boundary
   in the record's closing sections.
5. ⭐ **The card CLAIMS no result S10 did not produce, and imports no result from another step or sector.**
   ⚠ This is about **claiming**, ⛔ not about containing: the supplied action, the supplied premises, and
   the field identification must all appear, **presented as supplied**. ⚠ Likewise, the record's own
   sentence handing an object onward to a later step **is the record's boundary and stays** — what may not
   appear is any claim about what that later step will find.
6. **Every control is named by what it changes in the action** — ⛔ never by its tag prefix, ⛔ never by a
   material or a substance.
7. The card's **claim status is the one the record's boundary sentence supports.**
8. Reader-critical content is **visible in the default build**. ⚠ `paper/macros.tex:16-26` suppresses one
   field by name; the card at HEAD already carries a comment about this — preserve the property.
9. The paper still builds: from `research/pde_ledger_v3/paper/`,
   `pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode pde_ledger_v3.tex`, run **twice**.

## Deliverables

- a **claim ledger** at `research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S10_paper_card_claim_ledger.md` — ⛔ **not**
  inside the card.

  ⭐⭐ **ITS DOMAIN IS EVERY SENTENCE OF THE FINAL CARD, ⛔ not the sentences you consider claims.** Each

  | card sentence | CLAIM / EXPOSITION | the quoted record sentence that licenses it, or the supplied object it expounds |

  ⭐⭐ **A record line number is NOT a license.** The cell holds the **quoted record sentence**, and that
  sentence must itself state the claim. ⛔ An `EXPOSITION` row that asserts a measured outcome is
  misclassified, and that is a defect, not a judgement call.
- a **dropped list**: every claim in the HEAD card that does not survive, with the record sentence that
  refuses it or the note that nothing licenses it;
- an **unresolved list**: anything in the HEAD card that the record neither licenses nor refuses, with your
  reading. ⛔ Do not resolve these yourself; they come back to the orchestrator.
