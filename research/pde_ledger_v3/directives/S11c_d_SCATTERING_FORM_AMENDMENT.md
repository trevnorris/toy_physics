## 2. Priority deliverable: the general retained weak-order FORM

The first construction objective is **A9**, followed by its final engine/export
integration. It is not another fixed-frequency response table.

For every approved anchoring/density case, the handoff must contain the actual
computed profile-conditioned scattering/continuum expressions and weak
coefficients required by v10 §§2, 3a, 3c, 3d and 5d. In particular:

1. Preserve the full reduced local/nonlocal operator and coupling vertex,
   computed reference/left/right baselines, both-end channel spaces and derived
   signed current forms. Keep all required open incident/output channels and
   evanescent matching contributions. If a sector classification fails at a
   degeneracy, carry the domain limitation rather than a bare-sector label.
2. Preserve independent epsilon/eta/sigma grades and the complete retained
   rectangle, including the mixed grade. Only the declared physical homotopy
   relates eta and sigma. Keep frequency, tangential momentum, profiles and
   transfer/sharpness kinematics bindable within the declared admissible domain;
   this does not mean a global dispersion relation or validity through every
   threshold. A finite-contrast retained solve is not a higher-order continuum
   prediction.
3. Carry the amplitude components, actual uniform baseline, zero-jet and
   first-jet terms, induced amplitude, outgoing thickness-current form and
   incident denominator. Export the required total-conversion and weak
   coefficients, while emitting the baseline/interference/quadratic operands
   that establish their meaning. Do not conflate the physical total fraction,
   an induced-field quadratic diagnostic and their weak coefficients. A leading
   eta-squared physical leakage label requires the computed baseline/interference
   disposition; it is not a supplied answer.
4. Use transparent differentiable expressions and explicit physical/domain
   bindings under the retained export contract. Numerical mode/result records
   stay separately typed with their actual input and limits. A frequency-one
   coefficient table, opaque response symbol, fingerprint, unevaluated missing
   construction or hand-typed expected scaling is not the general computed
   FORM. Do not demand elementary closed form where a permitted transparent
   operator/integral representation is appropriate; the representation must
   still express the constructed object and support the specified binding and
   equivalence checks. Any unconstructed action remains unfinished.
5. Join the export to the saved numerical and retained-grade results using
   actual operand/source/unit/domain identities and necessary bind/encoding
   checks. Preserve separate field- and flux-normalized objects. Do not repeat
   unchanged scientific constructors merely to generate export plumbing.

All four physical cases and the v10 §5 uniform, FORM, coordinate and one-sided
controls remain. LAB_HELD versus MATERIAL_ADVECTED is a comparison of physical
anchorings; Eulerian versus material-coordinate agreement is tested within a
fixed anchoring and density. Their roles must not be exchanged.

The handoff still does not supply an order-unity slit-edge response, an
R1-dependent falsification magnitude, a generic absence of leakage, or a total
photon-loss probability combining continuum flux with stationary bound overlap.
The S11c-e strong-edge obligation remains named and unsolved. A nonzero weak
coefficient supplies no general positive lower bound on strong-edge conversion.

## 3. Practical numerical acceptance and the open-thickness check

### 3.1 Consolidated acceptance policy

The numerical target is a practical analog toy-model result, with roughly 1%
stability for well-resolved nonzero reported observables. Use the approved
absolute reporting goals, amplitude `1e-4` and current `1e-6`, in their declared
channel normalization/unit frame; do not apply those numbers indiscriminately
to dimensionful source coefficients or arbitrary matrix entries.

Use a small selected set of independent resolution, domain and regulator
comparisons tied to the claimed observable. New numerical methods need focused
sign/measure/indexing/omitted-term checks. Existing applicable evidence may be
reused. Unchanged machinery does not need an exhaustive new instrumentation or
ancestral validation campaign. Stop refining an intermediate once its effect
on the reported observable is demonstrably irrelevant at the chosen precision.

Report actual numerical spreads, conditioning, equations/boundary/current
residuals, positive regulator, finite boundaries, source/profile rules and
untested limits. A residual or refinement spread is not a rigorous error bound.
Near-zero effects remain unresolved unless the evidence resolves them in the
declared absolute frame; a denominator floor does not establish their relative
accuracy, sign or absence. Current balance must include the actual derived
slab/bulk terms and any retained dissipation, forcing or truncation remainder;
do not impose lossless S-matrix unitarity by assumption.

General operator-norm error budgets, rigorous infinite-tail/Abel limits,
outgoing-inverse certificates, global exceptional-locus coverage and analytic
Fredholm/Riesz theorems are not completion gates for this numerical handoff.
They remain necessary for any corresponding theorem-strength claim, which this
scope does not make. Ordinary-parameter channel/outgoing-domain validity is
still required; deferring a global theorem does not validate a local failure.

### 3.2 What the present spec and evidence say

V10 §3a requires all **actually open** channels at the chosen real frequency;
it does not prescribe a second frequency at which a thickness channel must be
open, nor does it promise such a point in the approved regime. Sections 3c, 3d
and 5d nevertheless require the general weak conversion FORM. A closed-channel
specialization cannot establish the correctness of its nonempty outgoing
thickness projection/current branch by itself.

The [saved four-case current record][current-report] says that `omega=1` has
four open transverse directions and zero open thickness directions; the saved
bulk-depth propagation check is also closed on its stated real-momentum domain.
The [continuum-current report][continuum-report] distinguishes the zero-rank open
selector from retained closed thickness amplitudes. These are useful
closed-channel controls. They are not evidence for radiating conversion at a
different parameter point or for a class-wide zero FORM.

There are **two distinct availability questions**: propagating thickness-like
channels of the full end pencil, and outgoing bulk-depth radiation with its own
current/measure. Neither is inferred solely from the other. A bulk radical
branch threshold or a zero-normal-momentum algebraic candidate is not proof of
an admissible flux-carrying thickness channel. The [saved frequency-source
report][frequency-report] explicitly gives its algebraic candidates that limited
status. No new frequency is selected by this amendment.

### 3.3 Proposed bounded coverage requirement — for review

This is an explicit proposed acceptance clarification, not a claim that v10
already specified an additional numerical frequency:

- Give the generic thickness projection, its current normalization and the
  weak-order FORM a **non-vacuous check on an admissible nonempty channel
  domain**, through an independently constructed symbolic/analytic comparison
  or an actual physical numerical witness with relevant controls. Testing only
  a zero-rank selector cannot pay for this coverage.
- After amendment clearance and A9 operand/form mapping, inspect existing full
  end relations, classifications and inherited validity conditions first. If
  they establish an admissible real-frequency witness with the required
  incoming transverse and outgoing thickness/radiating channel, prefer **one
  small physical response pilot and one selected comparison**. Record its
  complete parameter/profile/unit map, actual own end modes/currents, relevant
  radiation measure, precision, measured cost and stopping rule before any
  batch. Retain all channels of that problem, not just the desired component.
- Changing frequency alone is not presumed sufficient. Stay within the
  approved model, retained-order/smallness domain and continuum regularity
  assumptions, away from a threshold/resonance/gap closure. Do not alter signs,
  constitutive parameters or boundary conditions simply to force leakage. A
  change to the approved physical input needs its applicable decision gate.
- Do not scan for a witness indefinitely. If a bounded admissibility inspection
  does not establish one, record exactly what failed or remains untested. A
  valid independent generic-domain check can support a **symbolically checked
  FORM with no open-channel numerical validation**; that limitation must be in
  the reviewed handoff. If neither non-vacuous route is established, A9 remains
  incomplete. Seek a scope/parameter decision instead of declaring a zero
  result, proving a global absence by inference, or starting a broad sweep.

Thus a second numerical frequency is **not an unconditional existing spec
requirement**. Non-vacuous validation of the claimed generic conversion object
is required by this proposed amendment. An admissible open-channel numerical
point is a preferred focused test, not a guaranteed available point or a new
frequency campaign. The presence of an open channel does not itself imply a
nonzero conversion amplitude.

responses currently prepared for those searches; candidate isolating contours;

parent-operator pole-promotion claim. No schedule or automatic resumption is
created. Existing complex-frequency sources, rows, maps, responses and failures
remain available by exact references.

This deferral does **not** remove the resolvent, closed modes, radiation/current
construction or local branch/domain checks needed for the retained real-frequency
scattering problem. An unresolved local singularity remains a limitation on
that result; it cannot be hidden behind the pole deferral.

The following safeguards replace the pole-production mandate for this step:

1. Preserve the [baseline numerical search][search-report] exactly as a historical
   result: on the recorded circle centered at `1-0.01i` with radius `0.02`, the
   selected 16/32-point diagnostic closed **without a resolved candidate**.
   This is not a certified empty spectrum, a global search, a no-bound-state
   conclusion or a completed four-case pole study. Do not rerun or double it
   to make the status look stronger.
2. Retain the corrected mathematical distinctions when interpreting existing
   records: a nonlinear inverse residue or logarithmic count is not generally
   a projector; zero residue does not exclude a higher-order pole; a physical
   singular response needs its full principal part and actual forcing/
   observation maps, including their derivatives when applicable. Do not
   assert uncomputed projections or candidate data.
3. Keep end normal-momentum roots, bulk branch thresholds, zero-normal-momentum
   algebraic end candidates and profile-dependent frequency poles distinct.
   None of the first three is relabeled as a physical bound pole.
4. No physical pole, capture rate, pole-mediated loss or confinement exclusion
   is claimed by this amendment. Stationary spectral overlap, if studied later,
   is not a continuum outgoing flux or a capture probability without a protocol.
5. Preserve the [two CLEAR reviews of bounded Lean NP1–NP4][lean-review] within
   their actual scope. They establish neither the full physical pole contract's
   review clearance nor its analytic/application hypotheses. Do not commission
   another review of that unchanged bounded core merely because the physical

The amendment review must assess these kept claims and their scope. It need not
clear a new physical pole construction or all deferred Fredholm/realization
theory. Those obligations belong to any later proposal that actually uses them.
