## Deliverable and exact scope

Author `_measurements/S11c_d_two_asymptote_lift.py` to construct the exact
first-grade end-field lifts and their full unbounded forcing defects for
LAB_HELD/RHO4_CONSTANT, the unchanged physical input, LEFT and RIGHT, saved
real pole blocks 16 and 17, and independent grades 10 and 01. Use all columns
of each exact residue frame; retain its nullity-two redundancy and units.
The future worker must report whether the resulting forcing can be paired
with the saved outgoing prescription. It must not label a formal composition
as an evaluated response or a solved scattering problem.

The first stage does not calculate mixed response, a new physical binding,
new roots/modes, full-symbol LU, a finite response replay, integrals for a
numerical profile solution, current normalization or final FORM exports.
It does compute new local Laurent coefficients and end-field corrections at
already known poles. Those are the new science to be reviewed, not reuses
of the old floating-point mode derivatives.

   then restore the required saved objects. Record exact row/field/column
   units, independent grade convention and the unchanged input binding.
2. Join `P_LEFT,00`, `P_RIGHT,00` and the saved native grade-zero action to
   the accepted reference symbol/action representation. Persist the full
   comparison operands. An unjoined nonlocal zero-grade term stops the task.
3. For both inherited pole blocks, restore the accepted pole, physical branch
   value, residue, multiplicity and source identities. Compute only the new
   local coefficients needed for `-R0 P_e,h R0`. Use a branch-consistent
   finite local series of the actual saved expressions. Bound the series
   degree from the required Laurent orders; do not ask for an unrestricted
   series, general simplification or new pole search.
4. Save each local coefficient input before evaluating it and each return
   before any later guard. Build the full matrices of actual double/simple
   pole coefficients and the degree-at-most-one field polynomials. If a
   grade is structurally absent, retain its actual source zero proof rather
   than inventing a nonzero test case.
5. Check these polynomials by the separate native end-symbol action in the
   method plan, including branch transport and both left/right full symbols.
   Store coefficient residual matrices and their actual reductions; neither
   self-subtraction nor a fingerprint is the exact cancellation check.
6. Substitute the resulting lifted fields at every original field/probe slot
   in local and nonlocal actions. The substitution must be capture-safe for
   bound variables and must handle derivatives at their actual source
   coordinates. Preserve source integral order, phase and profile Abel terms.
7. Save the direct forcing defect and complete commutator decomposition.
   Compute their full reconstruction residual. Save a term/address census
   joining each original contribution, including cross-interface terms.
8. Analyze the actual remaining source tails and the regularity required by
   the proposed PV/delta test action. Save explicit bounds or reductions where
   established; label every unresolved term and limit otherwise. An algebraic
   reconstruction residual does not establish this domain result.
9. Persist strict checks, operation index and final posthashes. A scientific
   failure preserves its input, previous complete returns and traceback.
   No attempt reruns a completed operation to make it pass.

For an expression that does not simplify within the bounded operation, retain
its exact unsimplified return and distinguish `UNRESOLVED` from `NONZERO`.
Do not translate an unevaluated residual into a Boolean success. Unsupported
local branch/order/unit cases fail with their actual source operands.

## Required controls and their meaning

- **End-equation control:** check zero grade and each first grade through the
  independently applied full end symbol. Save actual matrices and exact
  coefficient identities. Numeric comparisons to saved mode data are
  supplementary and labelled as such.
- **Operator/forcing control:** compare direct native action against the
  decomposed lift/commutator action, with all 25 row/column routes and every
  inherited nonlocal term covered. Do not equate a list of terms with proof
  of convergence or equality after an unlicensed interchange.
- **Responsive mutation:** omit or reverse one actual nonzero nonlocal
  cross-interface commutator contribution on one side, hold the correct
  direct action fixed, and calculate the resulting residual. Preserve the
  mutated operand and result. If all such terms are genuinely zero, report
  that limitation and use an actual nonzero end-lift term as a separately
  labelled control; do not invent responsiveness or claim the missing control.
- **Unit and grade control:** check every summed term in native units and
  independent grades. Residue-frame column units are not automatically
  those of a flux-normalized incoming wave.
- **Domain control:** identify the source-specific forcing class and the exact
  limiting action required. The 1/k tail entries and the separated-point
  restriction must survive into capability metadata. General profile L1
  assumptions do not prove weighted tails for polynomial phase corrections.

A single bounded test of the proposed prescription on an explicitly selected
smooth rapidly decreasing source may be included only if specified in the
actual implementation and fixed review packet. It would test the action
extension, not validate all lifted defects by analogy. It is not silently
added to this contract or inferred from a successful inverse check.

completed receipt and hashes; a final index lists all complete operations and
any incomplete input. Full science remains in scratch, with concise tracked
reports and explicit diff exclusions for new generated data over 1 MiB.

operands; exact field corrections and residuals; lift functions and units;

profile/Abel/exclusion conventions; and a per-term domain disposition.

- `FIRST_GRADE_END_LIFT_AND_DEFECT_VALIDATED`: exact joins, end cancellation,

  domain is established. This is an ingredient, not a response.
- `END_LIFT_SAVED_FORCING_DOMAIN_UNRESOLVED`: construction survives but the
  actual forcing/prescription pairing needs the listed source-specific work.

  operation or failed source/control, never a fabricated complete result.

A later guarded saved-output validator can inspect only this job's new
returns if required. No additional validation job is authorized merely by
naming one here. Completion acceptance requires actual logs, resource
controls, strict stderr, stdout/checks identity, source posthashes and saved
