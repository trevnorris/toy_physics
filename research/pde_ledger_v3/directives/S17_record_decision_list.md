## Where Newtonian motion enters

Open the record with the difference between the two Newtonian constructions already in the papers. The first paper supplies a mass source for a Poisson equation, identifies its strength as `GM`, and takes the radial acceleration from the potential gradient. Its static-limit result tells us what happens to the finite-speed scalar term for a stationary source. The full 1PN paper goes further. It integrates over a compact defect in a smooth external field, uses the same monopole mass in its inertia and coupling, and neglects flux through the defect boundary at Newtonian order. Quote these assumptions beside the reduced force law. [Orbital paper](../../1pn_orbital_dynamics/paper/1pn_orbital_dynamics.tex) · [Full 1PN paper](../../4d_1pn_full/paper/4d_1pn_full.tex)

For a freely draining mouth, S15 measures the signed momentum entering a surrounding shell. To compare it with either paper's particle acceleration requires the companion's field, the momentum stored in the shell, the exchange at the core and slab faces, and the core's inertial response. Keep the Newtonian acceleration as the simulation target and the open S21 shell test conditional. S15 item 4 remains deferred.
