## Must-fix

### F1. `𝓛` is not bound to the reduced rows as construction operands

**Spec location.** After requiring a 3-D→1-D reduction of *both* closed rows, §2 still defines the mixing object from the *imported* operator:

> “After each engine performs the §1c 3-D→1-D reduction of **both** imported rows — the closed operator and the closed coupling kernel — the object `𝓛(y;ω,k_∥)` is the linear scattering/mixing generated by the **full imported closed operator**, with `s11cc2ClosedCouplingKernel` used as its canonical off-diagonal extraction.” (§2)

§4/§8 list the reduction as a *separate* computed object (with moments/reconstruction), parallel to the S-matrix, not as `𝓛`’s operands. §7 joins reduced operator/kernel *and* the S-matrix; it does not say the S-matrix is built from those reduced rows.

**Why this is wrong.** The named object is already 1-D (`𝓛(y;ω,k_∥)` on a localized interface). The convention-bearing content of both c2 rows is 3-D (hats at 3-vector transfers, `d³y` and `d³k` integrals, `π^{-3}` measures). A prohibition-style “after you reduce…” does not make the reduced rows the inputs of `𝓛` (S1: a sentence is not a control). Both engines can emit the §1c reduction as a side object and still construct the S-matrix from unreduced 3-D hats/measures inside the 1-D geometry — mixed 3-D/1-D convention in the object that is actually used. They would then agree on that mixed `𝓛`, while the reduced-row join can still look clean.

“Full” in §2 usefully means “not a modulus-only projection,” but that does not substitute for operand binding.

**Minimal fix.** State that `𝓛(y;ω,k_∥)` is obtained from the **reduced** closed slab operator, with the **reduced** closed coupling kernel as its canonical off-diagonal extraction — both reduced rows are the construction operands, not only comparator objects. Do not type a `(2π)` map. Optionally emit `offdiag(reduce(op)) − reduce(kernel)` as a same-convention residual (nit-level if the operand binding is explicit).

### F2. The CONTROL’s named Fourier content omits the 3-D momentum-integral measures both rows actually carry

**Spec location.**

> “Each engine must **COMPUTE and EMIT the 3-D→1-D reduction of its own closed-operator and closed-kernel Fourier content — every profile-transfer hat, jet hat, and hat at a middle-leg (intermediate-momentum) argument together with the rows' already-present in-plane position integrals — for both rows**” (§1c)

> “The `s11cc2MiddleMomentum*` are dummy integration variables, ⛔ not Fourier symbols; the Fourier symbols on the middle leg are those transfer and jet hats evaluated at mixed intermediate-momentum arguments.” (§1a)

**Why this is wrong.** On the real rows, the convention is not only in the hat heads. It is also in the **integration measures**. Both rows’ `Integral`s dummy-integrate 3-D **position** `s11cc2Y{1,2,3}` *and* 3-D **momenta** `s11cc1_k_output_*`, `s11cc1_k_input_*`, and (12 integrals per row) `s11cc2MiddleMomentum*`. Those `d³k` measures carry the same `(2π)` convention as the hats (`Pow(pi` is on every integral).

The CONTROL names hats + “in-plane position integrals.” It explicitly *de-classifies* `MiddleMomentum*` as Fourier symbols and does not name `k_in`/`k_out` dummies at all. A builder using that list as the reduction checklist can reduce hats and `d³y` to 1-D and leave `d³k_out`, `d³k_in`, and `d³k_middle` 3-D. Then `𝓛` is still mixed, and both engines can agree because the spec’s list matches.

Deferring the *instance census* and the typed `(2π)` arithmetic remains legitimate. Deferring *whether* the momentum measures are in the CONTROL is not: that is which object is reduced.

**Source of the obligation.** c2 actually wrote those dummies into both closed rows (counts above). The v8 claim that both rows carry “the same kind of 3-D Fourier content … over each row's already-present in-plane position integrals” and “no 3-D momentum deltas” is true as far as it goes (deltas are absent) and incomplete: the live 3-D Fourier measure is `d³y d³k`, not position alone.

**Minimal fix.** In the CONTROL, name the reduction operand as **the entire Integral as it appears** — position dummies *and* the 3-D momentum dummies (`k_out`, `k_in`, `MiddleMomentum`) — together with every transfer/jet hat at transfer and middle-leg arguments, for both rows, both operands, own convention. Keep not typing `[L_W/(2π)]` / `(2π)² L_W`. The build directive may still freeze the exact symbol list and `(2π)` arithmetic against the real rows.

Setting `η→O(1)` inside first-shape-order operators uses a truncated expansion outside its domain. Spec forbids those three. The right split is **Born in contrast**, not “weak gradient.”

### 2. Order bookkeeping — sound (derivation)

\[
A_H(λ)=ε\bigl(a_0+λ a_1+λ^2 a_2\bigr),\qquad
B_H(λ)=B_0+λ B_1+λ^2 B_2,
\]

along the supplied path `λ≡η` at fixed `L_W` (so `σ_W∝λ` tracks, grades not identified). Flux `J_H=B_H[A_H,A_H]`:
