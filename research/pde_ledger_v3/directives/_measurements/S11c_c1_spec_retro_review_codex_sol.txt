## Items 1–7

### 1. Two-momentum DtN/impedance — correct result, ambiguous specification

Using outward distance `r` in either disconnected half-space gives `N₀(k)=iq_out(k)`. For outward shape displacement

\[
a_s=s\,[h_s^0-sW_0/2]=(W_{\rm bg}-W_0)/2,
\]

the actual DtN derivative and impedance derivative are

\[
N_1=-\left[N_0M_aN_0+\operatorname{Div}(a\nabla)+\kappa^2M_a\right],
\]

\[
Z_1(k,k')
=i\rho_m\omega\,\widehat a(k-k')
\frac{q(k)q(k')+k\!\cdot\!k'-\kappa^2}{q(k)q(k')}.
\]

The direct boundary solve prints exactly this kernel and a zero residual at [stdout lines 7–11](/tmp/s11c_c1_boundary_matching.stdout:7). Thus:

- Both `q(k)` and `q(k′)` are required.
- The quoted numerator and sign for `Z₁` are correct.
- The positive composition is the bracket entering the **impedance after inverse differentiation**; the DtN derivative itself has the minus sign above.
- `q` has the sign of `ω` in the propagating sector and equals `iα`, `α>0`, in the evanescent sector.
- The outward normal is indeed `(-½∇W_bg,s)`, not bare `∇F`; see [stdout lines 11–16](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:11).
- The diagonal rigid-translation contribution vanishes on `q²+|k|²=κ²`; see [boundary stdout lines 12–13](/tmp/s11c_c1_boundary_matching.stdout:12).

This independently validates the cross-engine kernel agreement reported at [step lines 18–22](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/steps/S11c_c1_curved_bulk_closure.md:18).

### 2. Permeable response — inverse correct; density handling wrong

Let \(\beta=M_{1/\rho_{\rm br,bg}}\). The correct operator response as a function of `μ_θ` is

\[
D=I+\frac{\Lambda_A}{\rho_m^2}Z,
\qquad
\delta p=D^{-1}Z
\left[
\left(1+\frac{\Lambda_V}{\rho_m}\right)V
+\frac{\Lambda_A}{\rho_m}\beta\mu_\theta
\right],
\]

followed by

\[
\mathcal A=\beta\mu_\theta-\delta p/\rho_m,\quad
J=\Lambda_A\mathcal A+\Lambda_VV,\quad
t=-(\delta p+\Lambda_X\mathcal A)\hat n.
\]

The scalar elimination residuals are all zero at [stdout lines 22–29](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:22). Therefore `[I+(Λ_A/ρ_m²)Z]⁻¹` and the plus sign are correct.

`Λ_A,Λ_V` belong only in flux and `Λ_X` only in traction, exactly as inherited from [S11b lines 214–240](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11b_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:214) and [lines 358–363](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11b_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:358).

The defect is that \(\beta\) is a live multiplication operator. In `RHO4_CONSTANT`,

\[
\rho_{\rm br,bg}=\rho_{\rm br}(1+\eta w_1),\qquad
\beta=\rho_{\rm br}^{-1}(1-\eta w_1)+O(\eta^2).
\]

Consequently, even with `Z₁=0` the omitted pressure term is

\[
\delta p_{\rm live}^{(1)}-\delta p_{\rm frozen}^{(1)}
=
-\frac{\Lambda_A Z_0\,\epsilon\mu_{\theta0}\rho_m w_1}
{\rho_{\rm br0}(\Lambda_A Z_0+\rho_m^2)},
\]

printed at [density stdout lines 7–8](/tmp/s11c_c1_density_liveness.stdout:7). Nonzero retained terms also occur in `J` and `t` at [lines 11–16](/tmp/s11c_c1_density_liveness.stdout:11). The two-mode operator residual is explicitly nonzero at [lines 21–23](/tmp/s11c_c1_density_liveness.stdout:21).

The formal noninvertibility condition is otherwise correct:

\[
0\in\sigma(D)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
-\rho_m^2/\Lambda_A\in\sigma(Z)
\]

for `Λ_A≠0`; see [stdout lines 44–45](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:44). Deferring the profile-conditioned resolvent to S11c-d is appropriate. Calling it “Fredholm” does not itself establish Fredholmness without a named function space, but the record properly labels it formal.

### 3. Dissipation audit — three valid objects, with two wording defects

The three objects are genuinely distinct:

1. Bulk radiation:
   \[
   H_a[Z]=(Z+Z^{\dagger_a})/2.
   \]
   In flat coordinates its quadratic-form matrix is
   \[
   (AZ+Z^\dagger A)/2,
   \]
   verified at [stdout lines 35–38](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:35).

2. Closed two-port form: the Hermitian part of
   \[
   (V,\mu_s)\mapsto(\delta p+\Lambda_X\mathcal A,J).
   \]
   The computed response matrix appears at [stdout lines 30–31](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:30).

3. Independent bulk-energy check: traction work on the slab must be compared with the **negative** outgoing far-field flux:
   \[
   P_{\rm traction,slab}+P_{\infty}=0.
   \]
   Directly subtracting positive outgoing flux from traction work gives `−ρ_mωq|φ|²`, while comparing with the orientation-reversed bulk operand gives zero; see [stdout lines 17–21](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:17).

The evanescent-nullspace caveat is physically correct. A truncated block
\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&\eta c\\\eta c&R\end{pmatrix}
\]
has its apparent negative eigenvalue only at `−η²c²/R+O(η⁴)`; [stdout lines 41–43](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:41). It does not conceal a first-order passivity violation. With c1’s independent grading, however, the omitted completion should be called second shape order—`η²`, `ησ_W`, and `σ_W²`—rather than only `O(η²)`.

### 4. Rest-frame limit and grazing — convection conditions correct, shape validity incomplete

Expanding the convective dispersion
\[
(\omega-v_{\rm dr}q)^2=c_{s0}^2(k^2+q^2)
\]
gives

\[
\delta q=-\omega v_{\rm dr}/c_{s0}^2,\qquad
\delta q/q=-\omega v_{\rm dr}/(c_{s0}^2q),
\]

while the direct operator correction scales as `2v_dr q/ω`; see [stdout lines 49–52](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:49). Thus the two conditions in [c1 §2b](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c1_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:204) are correct, and must hold on **both momentum legs**. Strict `v_bulk_normal_0=0` is necessary at grazing.

The claimed `O(σ_W²)` vertical-projection statement is also correct: [stdout line 53](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:53) has no linear slope term.

But strict `v_dr=0` does not cure the separate shape-expansion singularity. The Neumann-to-Dirichlet correction contains `N₁/(q(k)q(k′))`; [stdout lines 46–48](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:46). At exact double grazing, a two-mode exact inverse scales as `1/(ηc)`, not as a Taylor series in `η`; see [lines 54–56](/tmp/s11c_c1_independent_review.stdout:54). Hence the existing first-shape operator may report a formal singular coefficient, but it cannot establish the physical threshold response without degenerate analysis.

### 5. Rule 17 and freezes — seal 5 is decidably wrong

The background integrated density is different. S11c-a supplies:

> “`ρ_br,bg⁰(y) ≡ ρ_4D,bg⁰(y) W_bg(y)`”

Therefore seal 5 should not remain `UNDECIDED`. The frozen response is wrong in the `RHO4_CONSTANT` representative.

### 7. Inheritance correctness

B0b/B0c, T-a..T-i, the affinity normalization, Λ placement, disconnected half-spaces, and opaque `μ_θ` are inherited correctly.

Two naming/representation defects remain:
