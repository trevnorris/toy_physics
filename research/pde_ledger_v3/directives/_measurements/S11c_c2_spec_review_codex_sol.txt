# Review verdict: BLOCK

Close-before-extract is physically sound, but the spec’s central claim that the substitution increment is independent of S11c-b’s unresolved sign conventions is false. I found seven MUST-FIX defects and two SHOULD-FIX gaps. A builder should not launch against this revision.

Computational evidence is preserved verbatim:

- [Real two-parent fold audit](/tmp/s11cc2_fold_audit.py) and [literal stdout](/tmp/s11cc2_fold_audit.stdout)

All three stdout files were re-run and diffed byte-for-byte. The working tree was not modified.

## 1. Non-commutation

Verdict: close first is correct, but the spec conflates the non-commutation residual with the full self-energy increment.

For
\[
R_T=ax+by+cp,\qquad R_H=dx+ey+fp,\qquad p=\alpha x+\beta y,
\]
my independent weak-block extraction produced:

```text
open block                 = (b, d)
extract(close(S))          = (b+βc, d+αf)
close(extract(S))          = (b, d)
non-commutation difference = (βc, αf)
```

The spec’s scalar counterexample also checks exactly: open \(x\to y=0\), closed \(x\to y=\alpha\).

Thus [§2 lines 136–143](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:136>) is sound about ordering. Extract-first is valid only when closure is sector-preserving, the relevant carriers vanish, or the “extraction” retains every auxiliary-pressure block needed for a later Schur complement. Extracting only the already-reduced S11c-b off-diagonal kernel is not such a construction.

However, the spec later equates three different objects:

\[
K_{\rm closed},\qquad
K_{\rm closed}-K_{\rm open},\qquad
K_{\rm closed}-C(K_{\rm open}).
\]

They are respectively called the self-energy at [line 181](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:181>), [line 190](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:190>), and [line 260](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:260>). They coincide only if closing the open extracted kernel does nothing. The real `coupling_kernel` still contains all four `delta_p_±`/`d_w_delta_p_±` slots, as the actual-slot audit proves, so that condition is not available.

## 2. Substitution-increment cancellation

Verdict:

- (a) Yes. For fixed trial/test spaces and pairing, weak restriction is linear:
  \[
  E(CS)-E(S)=E(CS-S).
  \]
  My SymPy linearity and increment-identity residuals are both `Matrix([[0, 0]])`.

- (b) No. Only terms independent of the substituted slots cancel. Slab-side carrier coefficients multiplying those slots survive.

In the example, the increment is `(βc, αf)`. If the second engine uses the opposite unresolved carrier sign, the cross-engine increment residual is:

```text
Matrix([[2*beta*c, 2*alpha*f]])
```

The diagonal coefficients and pre-existing off-diagonal coefficients cancel, but the closure-sensitive carriers `c,f` do not.

This directly defeats [§3c lines 197–200](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:197>), which say all deferred S11c-b terms and sign conventions cancel. The source record identifies unresolved signs precisely on the closure-sensitive carriers: “face generalized-force PY `+diff` vs WL `−linearVirtualVariation`” and “closure-fold sign” at [S11c-b step lines 112–115](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/steps/S11c_b_variable_coefficient_operator.md:112>).

The kinetic whole-row convention may disappear if it touches no response slot. The face-force and closure-fold conventions cannot be declared absent from c2’s residual.

## 3. Λ-channel routing

Verdict: the high-level routing is right, but the operative carrier decision is wrong and incomplete.

Correct inherited physics:

- `J_s` enters the true-area mass balance.
- `t_s`, including `Λ_X𝒜_s`, enters mechanical virtual work.
- `δp_s` enters the flux through `𝒜_s=μ_s−δp_s/ρ_m`.

That is fixed by [c1 §1d lines 150–161](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c1_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:150>) and the true-area laws at [S11c-a lines 361–367](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_a_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:361>).

But c2 [lines 103–105](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:103>) say `closure_shape_deriv` carries both flux and mechanically routed `Λ_X`. The source says the opposite:

> “`Λ_X` appears only in the traction `t_s`, not … in `S11CA_CLOSURE_SHAPE_DERIV`.”

— [c1 §1d lines 157–160](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c1_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:157>)

T-i is only the derivative of `J_s−Λ_A𝒜_s−Λ_VV_s`; traction belongs to T-d/virtual work.

There is a second gap: the S11c-b θ row has already subtracted T-i and thereby eliminated the explicit `J_s` carrier. The actual exported slab row contains `δp_±` and `d_w_delta_p_±`, but no `J_s` slot. Therefore “substitute closed `J_s`” is not a defined operation. The spec must decide between:

1. replacing the entire open flux contribution with the closed `Σ_s a_sJ_s`; or
2. retaining the already-eliminated `Λ_A𝒜_s+Λ_VV_s` row and substituting the closed pressure and all required pressure jets.

Adding closed `J_s` on top of the existing eliminated flux would double count it.

The mapping for `d_w_delta_p_±`, c1’s `V_s` symbols, and its opaque `μ_θ` symbols is also unstated.

Finally, the physical operator is a two-face sum. The source laws contain `Σ_s` in both mass balance and virtual work, whereas c2 [line 163](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:163>) requests the “closed full operator per `(α,ρ,s)`.” Per-face contributions are useful provenance, but an assembled `(α,ρ)` two-face operator and its parity blocks must also be mandatory.

  — [c1 §3b lines 328–330](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c1_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:328>)

Since c2 consumes those response coefficients to construct an off-diagonal operator, it must re-adjudicate this unless it first proves the relevant use is diagonal-only.

- The `μ_R,bg` form control is also omitted. c1 expressly reserves it for c2, “where `μ_θ` is composed with the slab variables,” at [c1 lines 448–450](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c1_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:448>).

## 5. Export topology

```text
CHECK_CONSUMER=OK roots=10 closure=524
```

So positional `load_model(base, c1_delta)` binds the delta correctly and there is no exact-key collision.

But [§7 line 329](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:329>) specifies:

```python
load_model(base=..., deltas=[...])
```

The actual signature is `load_model(base_path, *delta_paths)` at [ledger_fold lines 102–104](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/scripts/ledger_fold.py:102>). The stated call raises:

```text
TypeError: load_model() got an unexpected keyword argument 'base'
```

More seriously, [lines 339–340](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:339>) name c1 roots `face_response` and `face_response_coeffs`. Those are S11b rows. The c1 rows are `s11c_c1_face_response` and `s11c_c1_face_response_coeffs`, as frozen by [the fold design lines 186–188](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/export_ledger_bind_closure_design.md:186>) and present at [c1 exports line 101](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/scripts/S11c_c1_exports.py:101>).

The guard will not catch this provenance error: both stale S11b keys exist, so key existence and lookup-manifest equality can still pass.

Leaving the complete exact root set to the two-leg-gated build directive is acceptable under D3, but only after the spec fixes the intended c1 write-keys and adds every required physics carrier.

## 6. Rule 5 leak check

Verdict: no improper answer leak in the structural controls, but one control encodes false physics.

“Must move/nonzero” are load-bearing ablation requirements inherited from N6; uniform vanishing is the supplied S11b decoupling; these do not give the builder a sign or coefficient to target.

The defect is [§5e lines 292–294](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:292>):

> “`Z→0`, equivalently the impermeable `Λ_A⁰=0` slice”

They are not equivalent. Solving the supplied face equations gives:

```text
Z → 0:
    pressure = 0
    J = Λ_A μ + Λ_V V

Λ_A → 0:
    J = Λ_V V
    pressure = Z V (Λ_V + ρ)/ρ

Λ_A = Λ_V = 0:
    J = 0
    pressure = Z V
```

The inherited source explicitly defines impermeable faces by `Λ_A⁰=Λ_V⁰=0` at [S11b step lines 89–90](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/steps/S11b_interface_coupling_law.md:89>). The control must be split into distinct zero-DtN, zero-affinity-channel, and impermeable limits.

## 7. Rule 17 integrity

Verdict: conceptually genuine, not a disguised uniform-limit control; operationally under-specified.

The cross-engine adjudication is the two engines’ live-field `A` objects against each other. The within-engine `A−B` comparison freezes density while leaving the thickness profile and other jets live, so it is a density-liveness ablation, not `W_bg→constant`.

However, the real Python export row `rho_br_bg_rho4_constant` is literally a bare `Symbol`; the live relation resides separately in `background_density_map`. The slot audit confirms both facts. The spec must explicitly require the SymPy c2 construction to bind

\[
\rho_{\rm br,bg}^{0}(x)=\frac{\rho_{\rm br}}{W_0}W_{\rm bg}(x)
\]

and its jet from `background_density_map` before the fold. Otherwise “keep it live” is not executable from the declared c1 response, and a consumer can accidentally repeat the c1 freeze while passing the manifest guards.

## 8. Rule 3 / rule 2 corollary 3

Verdict: the increment itself is not a tautological check, and the adjointness handling is sound. Two reviewability defects remain.

The framing at [§3c lines 193–201](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_c2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:193>) correctly treats the increment as an exported definition, not evidence that physics agrees. Likewise, §3b correctly suppresses an adjointness residual when no independent route exists.

But:

- The spec must give distinct names to the closed coupling, substitution increment, and ordering commutator; currently all three are called “self-energy.”
- The §5c “one-sided corruption” has no defined pair of independent c2 routes. Parent N6 requires direct graph linearization and material-coordinate/flattened-face construction, comparison after field redefinition, and only then corruption of one route at [N6 lines 100–102](</var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_decisions.md:100>). C2 exports no such baseline route operands or representation-invariance residual. It also omits the separately reserved `μ_R,bg` ablation.
