## Verdict

**Not cleared.** The symmetry theorem is valid for a narrower, explicitly reflection-invariant operator, and A’s round-case inversion parities are correct. The packet currently overstates the background restriction, the completeness of the computed system, coverage of a physical throat, and executable feasibility.

### 1. The required `z`-invariant background is not actually frozen

A says:

> “The S11c interface background `W_bg(y)`, `μ_R,bg(y)` is an example.” ([A:43](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:43))

B then redefines `y` as a single coordinate:

> “`y` is the in-plane coordinate along which the background varies.” ([B:45](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_zinvariant_operator_blocks_directive.md:45))

But governing S11c uses a vector anchor coordinate and all three derivative directions:

> “`∂_{yᵢ}W_bg = …`, `∂_{yᵢ}μ_R,bg = …`.” ([S11c-b spec:182](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_b_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:182))

The actual engines use `{xOne,xTwo,xThree}`, all three background-gradient components, and profile jets through fifth order. The exports contain carriers such as `u_3_t*w1_profile_d3` and `u_3*w1_profile_d3d1`.

Consequently, restricting only the perturbations to `∂z=0` does not define an invariant subspace when `∂zW_bg`, `∂zμ_R,bg`, or higher jets remain live. B must explicitly set every profile jet containing a `z` index to zero, including the corresponding density, support, face, bulk-background, domain, and boundary data.

Likewise, A’s:

> “Any oblique perturbation can therefore be rotated into this form.” ([A:53](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:53))

is true for each single tangential Fourier component on a genuinely one-dimensional planar background. It is not true for the generic S11c-b background or for an arbitrary multi-directional superposition under one rotation.

### 2. Part 1 is not a complete brane–bulk leakage operator

B’s object explicitly:

> “Keep the bulk face loads (`δp_±`) as symbolic operands, as S11c-b does.” ([B:53](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_zinvariant_operator_blocks_directive.md:53))

The governing spec says:

> “the rest-frame bulk fields `v_bulk=∇₄φ`, `δp=−ρ_m∂tφ`, … S11c-b performs no curved-bulk response solve.” ([S11c-b spec:93](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_b_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:93))

The implemented operator has slab rows, face generalized rows, and symbolic bulk face operands, but no dynamic `φ`/`δp` row. Therefore a zero `u_z`–scalar slab block is not yet a computed zero of the closed brane–bulk radiation problem. The bulk closure, outgoing conditions, and drain-flow operator are absent by scope.

That conflicts with the claim:

> “Twist-type trapped light has no linear channel into anything that pushes the bulk.” ([A:65](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:65))

Part 1 can establish **reflection equivariance of the rest-frame slab/face operator**. It cannot by itself establish no bulk leakage or R-LEAK-1. “Construct all fields at once” is good discipline, but it cannot repair dynamics omitted from the governing object.

### 3. P2 is the wrong premise

A states:

> “Every brane–bulk coupling enters through scalar face/bulk quantities.” ([A:73](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:73))

The actual supplied laws include vector geometry and vector bulk operands:

> `J_s = ρ_m(v_bulk,s−v_face,s)·n̂_s`,  
> `t_s = −(δp_s+Λ_X𝒜_s)n̂_s`,  
> `δ_v𝒲_bulk = Σ_s a_s t_s·δ_vx_s`.  
> ([S11c-a spec:348](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_a_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:348))

The conclusion can still hold: normals, velocities, traction, both anchorings, the constraint fold, and the scalar response kernels are covariant contractions. My symbolic audit gives zero reflection residual for all of these and zero odd/even mixed derivatives for the entire raw Kronecker family after the complete `z` restriction.

P2 should instead require **equivariance of every scalar, polar-vector, and axial-vector operand and of all support/boundary data**. It should also cover omitted spin/director fields, not merely current scalar fields.

### 4. The round parity bookkeeping is correct, but throat coverage is overclaimed

For polar displacement, A’s inversion parities are correct at every `(ℓ,m)`:

- scalar and spheroidal: `(-1)^ℓ`
- toroidal: `(-1)^(ℓ+1)`

No rotation of `m` to zero is needed; inversion is central in `O(3)`. The audit reproduces that table and verifies a radial background has `u_tor·∇Q=0`, `div u_tor=0`, and no linear face-normal carrier.

However, A’s coverage table says:

> “light trapped at a throat … **yes**.” ([A:79](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:79))

The ontology warns:

> “The fields `h_+` and `h_-` … are not a complete nonlinear throat topology. Overhangs, necks, tubes … must be represented by the parent fields…” ([ontology:315](/var/projects/toy_physics/docs/toy_model_ontology_summary.md:315))

It also requires the support mode to be:

> “a spectrally normalizable bound state or acceptably long-lived resonance of the complete variable-coefficient transverse operator.” ([ontology:957](/var/projects/toy_physics/docs/toy_model_ontology_summary.md:957))

Thus H-round currently covers a hypothetical `O(3)` radial background with a toroidal bound mode, not an already represented physical throat. R-LEAK-1 needs falsifiers for:

- absence of such a normalizable toroidal mode;
- failure of the full nonlinear throat/background to admit `O(3)`;
- additional parent/core/support fields changing the representation content.

### 5. K1/K2 are genuine FORM changes, but are under-specified and can be null by construction

B says:

> “K1 … Add one term that couples field gradients to a fixed … vector.”  
> “K2 … Add one term containing a single Levi-Civita contraction.”  
> ([B:77](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_zinvariant_operator_blocks_directive.md:77))

Introducing a new fixed-axis spurion or a Levi-Civita tensor does change the admissible invariant family, so these are FORM rather than coefficient controls.

But the term must be fixed in the directive. My audit found two compliant choices for each control with different outcomes:

- K1 inert target block: mixed derivative `0`.
- K1 active target block: `sin²β cosβ`.
- K2 `g·(∇θ×∇e)` is identically zero under the `z` restriction.
- K2 `g·(u×∇θ)` survives as `u_z(g_y∂xθ−g_x∂yθ)`.

As written, the builder may accidentally select an unimplemented/null control or select terms differently between engines. Pin the exact dimensionally completed terms and coefficients before the build.

There is otherwise no direct expected-value leak in the filenames or tag rule; the primary object is named neutrally.

B says:

But S11c-b requires:

> “carry every independent invariant with its own free symbolic coefficient.” ([S11c-b spec:250](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_b_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:250))

The actual basis is 40 terms: 10 uniform plus 15 `∂W` and 15 `∂μ` invariants. B omits, among others, `μ_S`, `G_θu`, `G_Wu`, `κ_θ`, `κ_θW`, the `a_s11cb_uniform_*` coefficients, and all selected `gamma_s11cb_*` coefficients.

That enumeration could cause a builder to reconstruct only the old carried energy. B must explicitly say **all 40 accepted basis coefficients remain live**, along with both bookkeepers, both density representatives, both anchorings, and every allowed higher profile jet subject to the new `z` freeze.

### 8. F2 overstates what `m ≠ 0` proves and misses extra spin fields

A claims:

> “A circulating twist mode (`m ≠ 0`) carries angular momentum.” ([A:99](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:99))

The cited interpretive source says:

> “A real linearly polarized standing wave can have zero time-averaged angular momentum.” ([native-light:2377](/var/projects/toy_physics/docs/native_light_em_and_vortex_throat_interpretation.md:2377))

The computation gives azimuthal Noether density `m` for a traveling complex mode but `0` for a real standing `cos(mφ)` mode. Therefore `m≠0` alone is insufficient; the phase combination and complete angular-momentum integral must be specified.

F2 also only catches a spin carrier that breaks the background’s `O(3)`. An additional isotropic microrotation/director field can enlarge the linear operator without breaking the background. Since that candidate is absent from S11c-b’s field content, R-LEAK-1 must either exclude it explicitly or classify its parity and couplings.

A correctly excludes second order: the audit confirms the square of an odd/toroidal carrier is even and can source scalar deformation. F4 is appropriately necessary.
