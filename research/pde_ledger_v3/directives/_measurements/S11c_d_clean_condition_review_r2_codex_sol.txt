## Findings

1. **H-planar lacks the premise needed to rotate every incidence direction.**

A assumes only a single-coordinate background and one reflection:

> “all background data invariant under … `(3 → −3)`” and then “That background is also invariant under rotations about direction 1.”  
> — [A:45](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:45)

B’s R1 likewise requires background data to be independent of directions 2 and 3:

> “Every other background datum is independent of directions 2 and 3.”  
> — [B:62](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_zinvariant_operator_blocks_directive.md:62)

Neither condition implies rotational invariance. A constant support/background vector along direction 2 is independent of directions 2/3 and invariant under `3→−3`, but changes under rotation about direction 1. My computation gives:

> `R1_VECTOR_MIRROR3_RESIDUAL (0, 0, 0)`  
> `R1_VECTOR_ROTATION_ABOUT1_RESIDUAL (0, b*(cos(alpha) - 1), b*sin(alpha))`

Therefore the fixed-mirror theorem is valid, but the “for each incidence direction” TE conclusion requires an additional premise: every background tensor value, support, boundary datum, domain, and response law must be invariant under the full `O(2)` about direction 1. Otherwise only incidence planes aligned with an actual mirror are protected.

This also repairs P2: covariance of a law is not enough; the chosen background and boundary values must be fixed by the symmetry.

2. **The comparator instruction would hide the two unresolved sign disagreements.**

B says:

> “The two whole-row sign conventions … must be mapped explicitly … before any residual is computed.”  
> — [B:127](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_zinvariant_operator_blocks_directive.md:127)

The governing record says the opposite:

> “the comparator SURFACES them, does not normalize them.”  
> — [S11c-b record:112](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/steps/S11c_b_variable_coefficient_operator.md:112)

The raw PY operand, raw WL operand, and raw residual must be emitted first. A convention-mapped diagnostic may follow under a distinct name, but cannot replace the raw comparison. Otherwise an unresolved kinetic or face-force sign disagreement can be manufactured into agreement.

3. **The requested operator/dependency matrix is not uniquely defined and omits interface paths needed by A’s closure argument.**

A describes the bulk interface using only:

> “`δp_s`, `n̂_s·v_bulk,s`, `J_s`, `V_s`”  
> — [A:75](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:75)

B adds `𝒜_s`, `μ_s`, and the normal, but permits them as unspecified “rows or columns”:

> “include, as explicit rows or columns, every bulk-facing operand…”  
> — [B:77](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_zinvariant_operator_blocks_directive.md:77)

The governing face law also consumes the face-velocity vector, traction, true-area measure, and virtual displacement:

> `t_s^α ≡ −(δp_s^α + Λ_X 𝒜_s^α)n̂_s^α`  
> `δ_v𝒲_bulk^α ≡ Σ_s a_s^α t_s^α·δ_vx_s^α`  
> — [S11c-a:340](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_a_SHARED_PHYSICS.md:340)

The actual engine separately emits a `CENTER_FACE_GENERALIZED_ROW` for independent `ζ_c`:

> `CENTER_FACE_GENERALIZED_ROW`  
> — [SymPy engine:2135](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/scripts/S11c_b_brane_operator_sympy_audit.py:2135), [emission:3104](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/scripts/S11c_b_brane_operator_sympy_audit.py:3104)

B should pin one rectangular Fréchet map with an ordered input vector and ordered output vector. It must explicitly include `ζ_c`, its center row, `t_s`, `v_face,s`, `a_s`, and all bulk trace components consumed by the laws. `δp_s` is an independent input without the bulk solve, not interchangeably a row or column.

Without this pin, different builders can produce different matrices, and a missing face channel can appear zero merely because it was never represented.

The directive must whitelist imports: symbol identities and the accepted 40-term constitutive basis may be reused, but no exported slab operator, kernel, face row, or derived unrestricted payload may be consumed. R1 must enter the energy, background-jet maps, and face substrate before variation.

5. **F2’s angular-momentum statement is too strong and contradicts its citation.**

A says:

> “only a circulating (complex, travelling) twist mode carries angular momentum. A real standing twist mode carries none.”  
> — [A:113](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:113)

The cited source says:

> “A real linearly polarized standing wave can have zero … Two degenerate modes with a relative phase can form a circularly polarized bound pattern that carries angular momentum.”  
> — [native-light:2377](/var/projects/toy_physics/docs/native_light_em_and_vortex_throat_interpretation.md:2377)

My real-coordinate calculation likewise returns:

> `REAL_LINEAR_STANDING_PAIR_ANGULAR_MOMENTUM 0`  
> `REAL_QUADRATURE_STANDING_PAIR_ANGULAR_MOMENTUM A**2*omega`

Replace F2’s statement with: a single real linearly polarized eigenmode can have zero time-averaged angular momentum; two degenerate toroidal modes in quadrature can carry it. Such a bound pattern need not be a spatially travelling mode.

6. **R-LEAK-1 mixes a linear selection rule with a particle-stability requirement.**

A correctly limits the result to:

> “exact zero at linear order”  
> — [A:92](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:92)

But F4 calls second-order leakage a falsifier of R-LEAK-1:

> “Second-order leakage of the twist mode…”  
> — [A:121](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:121)

The symbolic selection rule is:

> `NONLINEAR_TERM_REFLECTION_RESIDUAL 0`  
> `NONLINEAR_SCALAR_SOURCE lambda*odd_amp**2`  
> `LINEAR_ODD_SCALAR_HESSIAN_AT_BACKGROUND 0`

Thus reflection forbids linear odd/even mixing but allows an even scalar source quadratic in the twist amplitude. Either:

- define R-LEAK-1 strictly as a linear theorem, making F3/F4 downstream nonlinear gates rather than falsifiers; or
- define particle stability as the requirement, in which case H alone is not sufficient and a nonlinear leakage bound/zero is still required.

The current text should not equate the linear exact zero with completed particle stability.

7. **“Exponential for smooth profiles” is not generally correct.**

A says:

> “suppression, exponential for smooth profiles”  
> — [A:140](/var/projects/toy_physics/research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/S11c_d_clean_condition.md:140)

A standard compact `C∞` bump is smooth but nonanalytic and has a stretched-exponential rather than generic `exp(−c|k|)` Fourier tail. The computed `−log|F|/k` decreases from `0.374` at `k=20` to `0.0675` at `k=320`.

Use “rapid suppression; exponential for suitable analytic profiles” instead. The broader conclusion—suppression is not a generic exact zero—remains sound.
