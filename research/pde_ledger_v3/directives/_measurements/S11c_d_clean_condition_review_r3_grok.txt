### 1. A’s closure criterion is false as stated, and it is how Part 1 is to be read

**A §1 (closure):**

> suppose **no face quantity depends on the twist-sector field, and the twist-sector rows contain no bulk trace input**. Then no bulk closure can connect that sector to the bulk … Part 1 prints exactly that map.

The listed face quantities include `v_face,s` and `δ_v x_s` (S11c-a `:343–345`: `δ_vx_s^α ≡ δ_vR_s^α|_X`, `v_face,s^α ≡ ∂_tR_s^α|_X`).

```
VFACE3 eps*u3_t
VFACE3_IS_EPS_U3T True
DVX3 dvu3
L_V_HAS_U3T False
L_J_HAS_U3T False
L_J_HAS_VB3 False
L_T3_IS_ZERO True
L_N0_COMPONENT3 0
WORK_L_HAS_DVU3 False
```

`v_face,3` *is* the twist velocity; `δ_v x_3` *is* the twist virtual displacement. The even scalars `V_s`, `J_s` and the traction `t_s` drop that odd part because `n_3 = 0`, so `n·v_face`, `n·v_bulk`, and `t·δ_v x` never see `u_3`.

The second half of the criterion is true: the `U_3` row and the even face scalars take no bulk-trace input. The first half is false. Part 1’s rectangular map (B §2) will print a nonzero `(v_face,3, u_3)` entry. Read with A’s “no face quantity depends on twist,” that entry looks like F1. It is the kinematic identity `v_face,3 = ∂_t u_3`.

H-planar’s earlier bookkeeping is the right statement: polar vectors have an even part and an odd part, and the odd part is the direction-3 component; the face *laws* contract with `n̂` whose direction-3 component vanishes.
