Generated by `_scratch/s15/gen/s15_cmp_setup_lookups.sh` (GNU grep, sed, awk, `LC_ALL=C`). Each block is a command
and its literal output. A "tag line" is a line matching `^[A-Za-z_]([^ :{]|\{[^}]*\})*: `: a name made of characters other than space,
colon and `{`, or of brace groups `{…}` (which may contain spaces), followed by `: `. Its tag is the text before that
`: `. This is a lookup convention for counting, not a grammar the comparator must adopt.
