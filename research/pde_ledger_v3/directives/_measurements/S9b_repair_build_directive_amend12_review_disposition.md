### Must be true in round 1

Write your own wording; these say what must be true, not the text.
1. **Locations (A).** A location names its object the same way in both copies: by key, label or case, never by
   position in a sparse or reordered container. The site parts are the union over the two copies. A part one copy
   does not emit stays in the set, and the text says how it is tried and printed, and whether its absence can
   furnish a witness. Any reading of an absent entry is the engine's own and is printed. No value the engine does
   not define is filled in.
2. **The unit (B).** What is a part and what is a container is fixed by the mathematical object, the same way in
   both engines, for matrices, vectors and lists, equations, solution sets and cases. The set is fine enough that
   round 8's confirmed witness sites are site parts. The harness prints the enumerated set and any excluded parts,
   so that an omission can be seen.
3. **A try (C).** A part counts as tried only at inputs that meet the witness requirements already in item 14.
   The inputs must not be chosen so that they alone make the knife's construction inert. Its values and attached
   conditions are decided by the engine CAS's evaluation, as item 14's witness test requires, not by substitution
   alone. The record prints, for each part, the requirement that was not met.
4. **Density sign (D).** Each density-sign part is tried at inputs whose computed density lies in that part, in
   both engines.
5. **Wording (E).** The Part 2 line names the software check's own failure, not "the guard".
