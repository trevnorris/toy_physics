## The structure, stated without this project's vocabulary

1. Space is a **3-dimensional hypersurface (a sheet / wall / membrane) embedded in a 4-dimensional SPATIAL
   bulk.** ⚠ **Four dimensions of SPACE**, ⛔ not 3+1 spacetime, ⛔ not a compactified Kaluza–Klein circle.
   The 4th direction (call it `w`) is an ordinary spatial direction extending on **both** sides, `±w`.
2. The bulk is a **material medium** — a superfluid-like continuum with its own density and flow —
   ⛔ **not** empty higher-dimensional spacetime and ⛔ not a purely geometric construction.
3. **Matter is a topological PUNCTURE through the sheet into `w`** — a defect where the sheet is pierced,
   ⛔ not a particle localised on the brane by a potential.
4. **The sign of electric charge is the ORIENTATION of that puncture** — `+w` versus `−w` — a `Z₂` label,
   ⛔ not an additive quantum number.
5. **Gravity is the medium FLOWING into the puncture** — a drain / sink carrying material off the sheet
   into the bulk, ⛔ not curvature of a metric and ⛔ not an exchange particle.
6. The sheet carries **shear stiffness** while the bulk does **not**, which is what keeps light on the
   sheet.
