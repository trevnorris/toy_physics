\chapter{Registry provenance}\label{app:registry-provenance}

The reduction registry (\StageFile{research/pde\_ledger\_v3/reduction/}) is
grown one step at a time: a step adds its quantities and relations, and the
gates must pass before that step banks. This appendix records the registry's own
history --- including changes that are bookkeeping repair rather than physics,
which have no place in a derivation part.

\section{S0.5 --- pre-S1 registry surgery}

\claimstatus{Bookkeeping repair --- \StatusText{not physics}}

\stagefield{What it did}{Deleted \(c_\gamma\), \(\lambda_\gamma\) and the
relation \(R3:\lambda_\gamma=c_\gamma/c_s\) from the medium block.}

\stagefield{Why}{\(c_\gamma^2=\mu_R/\rho_{br}\) is a shear modulus over a brane
density. Both are properties of the \textbf{brane}. The bulk medium is
shear-free by construction, so the medium block cannot produce a shear modulus
at all: a brane property had been filed as a medium property. The same category
error as the retired \(a\)-pin, and it fails the standing test --- \emph{what
does this quantity physically relate to?}}

\stagefield{Deleted, not retired}{\(c_\gamma\) is not dead; it was placed in the
medium block \emph{before the walkthrough reached the step that introduces it}.
It is reintroduced by the sector that needs it (Part~I), with its own
provenance. Nothing dead is left in the registry for a reader to filter.}

\stagefield{Effect on the count}{The medium block went from 9 quantities and 5
declared primitives to 7 and 4. The four are exactly
\(\{\hbar,\,m_{\mathrm{GNLS}},\,K,\,\rho_0\}\); the three surviving relations
determine \(\{c_{s0},\,\xi_h,\,h_0\}\) and nothing else.}

\stagefield{Acceptance}{Recomputed, never preserved. The mutation case
\texttt{C-M1} died with \(R3\) --- its entire content was \emph{drop \(R3\)} ---
and was deleted with no replacement, an independent coverage determination
having established that a defect caught only by \texttt{C-M1} is not
constructible.}
