\part{Part V --- The Knit}

\chapter{Can one medium satisfy every requirement at once?}

This part runs \emph{last} by construction. The brane and the bulk are not
assumed and then explored --- they are \textbf{defined here}, as whatever object
satisfies the union of the requirements Parts I--IV established, if such an
object exists.

Two checks, both of the form \emph{physical impossibility}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{individually admissible} --- no ghosts, energy bounded below,
        hyperbolicity, constraint count, causality;
  \item \textbf{jointly satisfiable} --- one substructure supplies every
        sector's requirements simultaneously.
\end{enumerate}

A no-go at either check is half one's falsification and is reported as a result.

Not yet walked.
