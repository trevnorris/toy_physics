zero**, the dispersion is `ρ_br⁰ω² = μ_R k²`, and the imaginary part is **zero**. Both engines, same

⛔ **This does NOT settle whether confinement is unconditional** — that is a non-uniform question (S11b-C).
⚠ Its dimension is **undetermined**, because a coefficient that vanishes identically has none.

**The breathing mode is stable iff `K₀ > 0`.** On the soluble slice (`k = 0`, impermeable, no reciprocal
coupling), derived by **both** engines from their **own** assembled determinants:


`K₀ < 0` ⇒ one growing and one decaying root; `K₀ = 0` ⇒ static; `K₀ > 0` ⇒ decay only.
⭐ Equivalently, **growth iff `C > (B_ρ⁽³⁾ + k_W W₀²)/(2W₀)`** — the density–thickness cross-coupling
beating both restoring stiffnesses.

⚠⚠ **This growing root is NOT an energy-conservation violation.** It is the stored energy failing to be

Energy accounting closes exactly; there is simply no floor. ⭐ For `K₀ > 0` every nonzero imaginary part
comes from **propagating bulk radiation resistance**, which survives impermeability.
