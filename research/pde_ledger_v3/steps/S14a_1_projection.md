## 1. Supplied objects, domain and premises

The user's committed law (2026-07-24) is supplied, **"unfalsifiable in this build"**:

```text
∂t n + ∇₄·(n u) = 0
∂t(χ_B n) + ∇₄·(χ_B n u + J_χ) = n Γ_B
Γ_B = Γ_return − Γ_drain,       [Γ_B] = T⁻¹.
```

There are three in-plane coordinates `x¹,x²,x³`, bulk coordinate `w` and time `t`. The two
live graph faces obey `w₋(x,t)<w₊(x,t)`. Regions 1, 2 and 3 are below, inside and above.
U1 sets exterior order to zero; interior order `f(x,w,t)` stays live. The inherited field
class has independent regional fields and one-sided traces, permits jumps and supplies no
face-concentrated part of `n,u,J_χ,f`; the source may have a concentrated part. The closed
projection counts each endpoint's concentrated part in full. Face deltas use coordinate `w`,
not true surface area. The core is excluded, with S22. Transfer is only **"wherever the committed
law holds and the shear-holding region has two faces over the in-plane coordinates"**.
