**Verdict. Three substantive qualifications are missing in the first two entries.**

1. **The first paper’s local 1PN orbit assumes a flux-neutral background subtraction.** S17 lines 21–23 omit “a comoving patch where the smooth background flow has been factored out” with `\sum_i Q_i^\mathrm{local} = 0`. The authors say the derivation “relies on boost invariance to discard aether--wind terms” (line 963). A pair with net local inward flux does not automatically meet that premise. State the flux-neutral, irrotational far-field and uniform-drift assumption when transferring the perihelion benchmark to moving drains. This qualification concerns the 1PN orbit. [Orbital paper, lines 215–242 and 963](../../../../../research/1pn_orbital_dynamics/paper/1pn_orbital_dynamics.tex#L238)

2. **The bridge’s half-displaced-mass coefficient assumes an impermeable exterior boundary.** S17 lines 27–29 give exterior potential flow and uniformity along the extra coordinate. The translating sphere also uses “The no-penetration boundary condition on the surface” and `\partial_n \phi \big|_{r=a} = 0`. Its applicability list requires “the far field is low-Mach and effectively incompressible” and names “boundary-condition leakage” among departures generating corrections. Its uniform throat has spherical cross-sections. State those assumptions. A draining mouth must justify the same leading translational response in the presence of through-flow, or supply its own measured added mass. [Bridge, lines 687–713, 774–793 and 3553–3569](../../../../../research/4d_1pn_bridge/paper/4d_1pn_bridge.tex#L3553)

3. **Wave support alone does not select the quartic energy coefficient.** S17 line 27 says “Wave-supported rest energy gives the quartic coefficient `3/8`.” The bridge requires a stable trapped transverse mode and says it “has an isotropic linear dispersion at the scales of interest.” It expressly says the group velocity “along $x$ must match the defect velocity” and gives the Taylor coefficient “once~\eqref{eq:vg_condition} holds.” State these kinematic assumptions for `E = γE₀`. A moving drain claiming this mechanism must support a comoving mode with that dispersion. [Bridge, lines 1226–1252 and 1275–1290](../../../../../research/4d_1pn_bridge/paper/4d_1pn_bridge.tex#L1226)
