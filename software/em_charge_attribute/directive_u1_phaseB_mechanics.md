Acceptance teeth: (a) mutating a primitive BC coefficient must propagate to the assembled residual AND all dependent outputs; (b) a **solver-bypass tooth** (replacing the solve with warehoused output must fail); (c) an **endpoint-binding tooth**: swapping two endpoints' assembled systems must alter/reject the endpoint↔system **fingerprint and dependency identity**; an OUTPUT change is required only where the assembled dependency analysis predicts sensitivity — where it predicts invariance (e.g. two systems both yielding a structural zero), a **computed invariance proof** is the accepted alternative, so the tooth cannot punish a genuine computed equality. Prose explanations cannot satisfy these gates. Identical results across endpoints for a `𝔅`-sensitive object must themselves be computed-explained (shown to follow from the assembled systems), not narrated.
