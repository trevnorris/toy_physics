## §2. Requirement R1 — §7.4 tilt: profile family, statics, susceptibility

1. **The tilt profile family.** Phase C must produce the indexed tilt-profile family the substrate has been missing (the object whose absence produced B1's 8 `UNRESOLVED(tilt_profile)` leaves and B2's `tilt_profile`-blocked cells): a native one-body ansatz family for the tilted sleeve/`χ` configuration, embedded via the collective coordinate `p` per spec §5 (the invariant `p` itself — the signed `θ = s·p` convention is **banned** from all deliverables). Every new coefficient or profile the family needs beyond the frozen 7.0 declared inputs is a **declared input** with an explicit `[OPEN input]` tag where unavailable, registered per the standing parameter-register duty — never silently guessed. If no family is derivable from committed inputs, that is a witness-UNRESOLVED landing (§5), not a license to invent one.
2. **Statics from the total four-channel force balance (spec §3).** The steady tilt `p(V)` is not free-standing: it must be obtained by solving the full spec-§3 force balance in the `p` degree of freedom, with **every contribution assigned to exactly one channel** of `{variational | flux | constraint/multiplier | Rayleigh | radiation}` — E1's placement declared, the E4 constraint-reaction (multiplier) contribution present wherever E4 is keyed, the E5 Rayleigh channel present wherever E5 is keyed — and a **runtime no-double-count assertion** that the channel-assigned contributions reconstruct the total residual exactly once (able-to-fail, with its own mutation tooth). **The total residual is not builder-defined:** stage 0 mechanically generates an **expected force-term census for the `p`-balance** from the complete set of §0.6 typed native roots actually present in the frozen action, surfaces, constraints, fluxes, Rayleigh functionals, and radiative channels — independent of any builder claim. (This force-term census and the §3.3 coupling-source census are **two distinct stage-0 censuses**, each generated over the typed native roots for its own target object and each carrying its own incidence duty; neither substitutes for the other.) Acceptance asserts (i) **force-census incidence completeness** (every applicable typed root contributes to, or carries a computed structural-zero certificate for, the `p`-balance) and (ii) **exact set equality between the census-expected terms and the terms reachable in the constructed residual** (an omitted native term fails here even if all downstream channel equalities pass). Structural zeros are stated explicitly per census entry, and the `∝V̇` partition owner (`M_AB` XOR `C_ṁ`, spec §3) is named — a **Phase-C computed result emitted as a successor record** (§1.2, §2.5): the frozen upstream partition terminals are `partition_open_pending_B2` (B1) and `UNRESOLVED(return_closure)` (B2), so this owner is never consumed as an already-closed upstream fact. On that basis: existence and stiffness/anchoring dependence of `p(V)`, computed, per `𝔅` endpoint, with the leading `V`-dependence extracted. The spec's analyticity remark (leading nonzero `p(V)` linear in `V` on an isotropic in-brane background) is a **hypothesis to test**, not an assumption. Per-cell tilt physics status is emitted from the parent tilt enum, which must include `TILT_NO_STEADY` (no steady solution) and `TILT_UNSTABLE` (steady but linearly unstable) alongside the derived-value cases — availability (§5.1) never substitutes for physics status.
3. **Parity is computed, not assumed** (spec §5): the transformation of `p` under body conjugation + ambient `R_w` must be derived from the frozen action on the declared ambient branch, with the one-sided-slab asymmetry map stated wherever the postulated two-sided background is not what is actually computed. **Every parity claim anywhere in the Phase-C deliverables** — not only the §3.6 census — carries the three-way authority tag `{body-only | ambient-postulate-dependent | one-sided-asymmetry-map}` (spec §1/G5).
4. **Susceptibility.** The dynamic response `p(ω,V)` at the same keying, obtained by linearizing the **same total force-balance residual** as §2.2 (same channel-ownership record, same no-double-count assertion), with poles/branch structure **and the stiffness/anchoring dependence of the dynamic response** reported, and the local-truncation validity domain stated (spec S5).
5. **Upstream reconciliation (immutable-overlay successor records).** Stage 0 must generate, mechanically from the frozen pinned data alone, an **exact reconciliation inventory** of every upstream obligation touching the tilt datum: all B1 `UNRESOLVED(tilt_profile)` leaves, every explicit B2 tilt-row path, and every B2 obligation ID carrying the `p`-slice `deferred_to_phase_C` disposition (including the G7/G9 records that carry it). Each inventory entry is discharged by a **successor record** containing: the unchanged `upstream_artifact_digest`; the exact canonical upstream ID / schema path; the frozen upstream disposition verbatim; and a separate Phase-C disposition — the upstream record itself is never modified (§1.3). Phase-C dispositions, exhaustively:
   - tilt-blocked `C_ṁ`/mechanics/radiative/G7-class cells whose missing datum the tilt family now supplies are **recomputed as Phase-C successors** (full §5/§6 duties on the recomputed values);
   - **G9 closure-frozen records are preserved** by exact reference or by a computed demonstration of `p`-independence — no new witness is minted and no G9 machinery is re-run (§4.6);
   - cells still blocked re-emit Phase-C witness-UNRESOLVED (§5) with the blocking datum named.
   A Phase-C successor disagreeing with an upstream **derived** value at the same slice = immediate amendment HALT (exit-42, §1.3). Acceptance asserts **exact set equality** between the reconciliation inventory and the successor set: no omitted, renamed, duplicated, or invented IDs, and no aggregation escape hatch. `M_pp`, `M_Xp`, and the Ω(ii)/(iii) channels blocked on the tilt family are recomputed where the family now supplies the missing datum.
6. **G10 (process, live):** if `p(V) ≡ 0` at an endpoint or branch, that zero is recorded honestly as the result for that cell — not treated as a failure and not massaged.

## §3. Requirement R2 — §7.5 the coupling package

For each mediator `Φ_A ∈ {h, u_T, u_L, wall/χ modes}`, on the full key (endpoint `𝔅 ∈ {E1..E5}` × ambient/parity branch × closure branch × genuinely-active OPEN stratum), with axis collapse only after computed independence. **The genuinely-active OPEN strata are not builder-selected:** stratum membership is generated mechanically from the frozen 7.0 OPEN-input register plus the typed input ancestry of each deliverable, asserted exact-set-equal against that generation (an OPEN input in a deliverable's ancestry with no stratum = fail; a stratum with no ancestral OPEN input = fail), and every axis collapse requires a computed per-axis independence demonstration before collapsing:

1. **(a) Domain + surface terms.** `S_body` on the declared control volume `Ω_c` with explicit boundary terms on `Σ` and `∂Ω_c`. Surface contributions are first-class (they are often the actual exterior source).
2. **(b) Bulk source + full matrix kernel.** `J_A = δS_body/δΦ_A` **and** the full matrix operator perturbation `O_AB → O_AB + δO_AB[V, p, s; 𝔅]` **including off-diagonal mediator/mode-mixing entries**. Computing only diagonal `δO_A` is a stated failure mode.
3. **(c) Constraint/virtual-work channel.** For E4 (and E1 where imposed by multipliers): the constraint-reaction contribution to the coupling, entering by Lagrange–d'Alembert/multipliers, not `δS/δΦ`. The report states, per endpoint, which channel each contribution sits in (spec §3 decomposition). **Every 7.5 contribution (a–d) carries the same exactly-one-channel ownership record as §2.2** — assigned to exactly one of `{variational | flux | constraint/multiplier | Rayleigh | radiation}`, with the runtime no-double-count assertion covering the coupling package as well. The census duty recurs here as its **own distinct census** (the §2.2 two-census rule): stage 0 mechanically generates a **coupling-source census** — the expected coupling-package sources per mediator, walked from the §0.6 typed native roots — distinct from the §2.2 force-term census, and acceptance asserts **coupling-census incidence completeness** and expected↔reachable exact set equality over the 7.5(a–d) contributions — a native source omitted from every component and from the G8 inventory simultaneously fails at this census, not silently.
4. **(d) Far-field multipoles of the TOTAL COUPLED linear response** — sources + the full mixed kernel together, the object the two-body computations actually consume — with **`s`-parity channel-by-channel** and the `O(V)` structure extracted. The desired `j ∝ s·V` is an **output classification**, never an input, and the verdict is **two-layered and mechanically exclusive**:
   - **Availability layer** (§5.1): exactly one of `DERIVED(…)` | `UNRESOLVED(witness, challenge)` per slot.
   - **Physics-status layer** (on DERIVED cells), per the parent v5 verdict grid exactly: `ILL_POSED(reason)` is a **separate terminal**, emitted when the defining total coupled linear response does not exist or is not constructible from the typed roots — it is NOT one of the projection outcomes. When availability is `DERIVED` and the response is well-posed, the parent-contract **fixed `c_sv`/`Δ` projection** decides **exactly one** of `{EXACT_SV | SV_PLUS_DEPARTURE(structure) | DEPARTURE_ONLY(structure) | NULL}` (parent forms including payloads): `c_sv` and `Δ` are pre-defined objects with pre-registered predicates, never post-hoc fits — **and that pre-registration is digest-bound, not prose**: stage 0 emits a **projection-freeze artifact** containing the projection operator (domain = the total coupled `O(V)` charge-channel response; range = `(c_sv, Δ)`), the inner product / residual norm it uses, and the registered zero/nonzero predicates referencing the parent v5 symbolic decision procedure; this artifact joins the §1.5 canonically-hashed stage-0 bundle and A1, and a post-ratification mutation of it must die at the `STAGE0_CONTRACT_DIGEST` assert (its own mutation tooth). Each zero/nonzero decision follows the parent's symbolic decision procedure (identically-zero vs generic-nonzero on the OPEN stratum); a projection predicate whose truth depends on the value of an OPEN leaf lands `UNRESOLVED(named leaf)` per the parent rule, never a silent pin. The complete per-cell coupling enum is therefore `{EXACT_SV, SV_PLUS_DEPARTURE(structure), DEPARTURE_ONLY(structure), NULL, UNRESOLVED(named input), ILL_POSED(reason)}` (parent forms including payloads), mutually exclusive, and the parent's computational-integrity axis (`COMPUTATION_VALID` / `HARNESS_FAILED(set)` / `NOT_RUN(upstream)`) is reported orthogonally — physics outcomes are reportable only under `COMPUTATION_VALID`. (The tilt deliverables of §2 analogously carry the complete parent tilt enum, parent forms including payloads: `{TILT_LINEAR(coeff), TILT_OTHER(structure), TILT_ZERO, TILT_NO_STEADY, TILT_UNSTABLE, TILT_UNRESOLVED(named input)}`.) **Tilt-status interaction:** the coupling package is delivered off-shell in `p` wherever derivable (parent Phase-B posture); the additional steady-substituted row at `p = p_*(V)` exists only where the tilt cell has a steady solution — under `TILT_NO_STEADY` or `TILT_UNSTABLE` the steady-substituted row for that cell carries that tilt status explicitly (no substituted value is fabricated), while the off-shell-in-`p` package remains a first-class deliverable for the cell. Amendment HALT remains orthogonal to both layers.
5. **Mass/charge split.** Every response is decomposed into drain/mass-sourced vs orientation/charge-sourced parts (the split G11 enforces). A `V⁰` orientation-dependent structure is labeled a *static-electric-candidate* object, never "magnetic" (naming discipline of `docs/em_phaseC_force_decomposition.md` §3).
6. **One-body post-mouth parity census** (`docs/em_phaseC_force_decomposition.md` §6(A), the Phase-C-owned item): for each field/profile and each `𝔅` endpoint, the `s`-even / `s`-odd components of the stationary solution (axial drain, sleeve, wall, surface flux), each parity claim tagged as body-only, ambient-postulate-dependent, or one-sided-asymmetry-map. 7. **No Maxwell import.** Every coupling object must have native ancestry (frozen action + declared inputs). Maxwell/Larmor/Coulomb forms may appear only as *comparison labels* in classification, never as sources, kernels, or intermediate steps. Enforced by a computed runtime ancestry guard, not by source grep.

The mixing enters G5's able-to-fail record (§4.4).
