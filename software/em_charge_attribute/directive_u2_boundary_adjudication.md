2. **The static/dynamical scope line (highest-priority invariant).** U2 adjudicates endpoint classes against **committed structure** — a static, variational question. *Which class the throat actually sits at under motion* is a **dynamical-selection/stability question and is OUT OF SCOPE**: it is deferred to a downstream deliverable and named as such in the results.
