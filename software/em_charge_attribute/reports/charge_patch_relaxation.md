# Does charge-induced relaxation of the order layer select a lateral radius?

**The existing scalar wall supplies zero excess area cost for spreading a prescribed charge over the same flat interface.** Its neutral profile already passes through \(\chi_B=1/2\) everywhere along the wall. The written double well is independent of carrier density, and the Gaussian Maxwell weight is prescribed. Hold geometry, imposed shear loading, and background fixed. Varying the displayed scalar terms then gives the same order equation with and without the charge. The throat and mixing energies remain open. This comparison concerns the order contribution of an existing wall. **Relaxation test.** Consider the two-dimensional footprint proposed for the charge, of area \(A=\pi r^2\), with a fixed extent \(L\) in the remaining tangential direction of the four-spatial-dimensional parent. Prescribe the magnitude \(|Q|\). As an illustrative binding law, let the double-well coefficient at surface density \(\nu=|Q|/A\) be \(a_{\rm eff}=a_B/(1+\nu/\nu_*)^2\), with \(\nu_*>0\). This makes partly ordered material energetically cheaper for the carriers. For each uniform column the relaxed width is \(\sqrt{\kappa_B/(2a_{\rm eff})}\) and the tension is \(\sqrt{2\kappa_B a_{\rm eff}}/6\). SymPy varied the profile width. Mathematica completed the square for profiles joining the two phases. [Planar wall derivation](../../../research/pde_ledger_v2/notes/stages/ledger_stage006_two_phase_chiB_ontology.md#L133) · [Phase-field benchmark](https://pages.nist.gov/pfhub/benchmarks/benchmark8.ipynb/) · [Calculations and literal outputs](../../../docs/_measurements/charge_patch_relaxation.json)

Write \(A_*=|Q|/\nu_*\) and \(\Sigma_0=L\sqrt{2\kappa_B a_B}/6\). After subtracting the neutral wall over the same area, the column result is
\[
\frac{\delta_{\rm charged}}{\delta_B}=1+\frac{A_*}{A},
\qquad
\Delta E_{\rm wall}=-\Sigma_0\,\frac{A A_*}{A+A_*}.
\]
Both this energy and a three-dimensional Coulomb contribution \(C Q^2/r\), \(C>0\), decrease with increasing \(r\). The area formula omits the boundary of the patch. The diffuse limit survives lateral gradients on the infinite flat wall. Each relaxed column costs at least \(-\Sigma_0\nu/\nu_*\) in excess per area, lateral gradient energy is nonnegative, and the unchanged neutral profile used as a trial reaches the integrated lower bound \(-\Sigma_0 A_*\) in the dilute limit. No finite footprint is the ground state in this example. Other binding laws can favor a compact state below a finite dissociation energy.

**What would select a radius?** Maintaining a distinct charged patch at excess energy \(T>0\) per footprint area gives \(E=CQ^2/r+\pi T r^2\) and \(r_*^3=CQ^2/(2\pi T)\). For example, imposing a logistic width \(\ell\) throughout that patch costs \(T=L a_B(\ell-\delta_B)^2/(6\ell)\) in the bare scalar functional. A physical reason to enforce that distortion at low density is missing. Binding an already assigned \(Q\) also leaves the mirror sign, the unit magnitude, and the barrier relative to the initially charged mouth to be established. A discriminating input is the excess energy at fixed \(Q\) near the slip compared with the same excess allowed to spread along the interface or enter the receiver.
