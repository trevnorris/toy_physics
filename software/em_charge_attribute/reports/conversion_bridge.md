# Can the converting brane source electric charge?

**The material field is a scalar.** The independent order parameter \(\chi_B(\mathbf x,w,t)\) is 1 in the shear-supporting brane and 0 in the shear-free bulk. The same constituents pass between those states. The free energy weights the displacement field’s shear energy by \(\chi_B\). In the mouth calculation a second object, the unit direction of the face normal projected into the three brane directions, supplies the wrapping sign. Its projection can vanish on a smooth face. Here \(\chi\) is the order sampled across the material collar. At fixed constituent charge, neither the order source \(n\Gamma_B\), the specified shear and neutral wave energies, nor the rim’s coupling to the mechanical bend \(H\) supplies an additional electric source proportional to the wrapping. The throat and mixing energies remain unspecified. On the saved prescribed flow, subtracting the held mouth at the same tilt and intake gave exactly zero excess carrier charge in the layer and zero excess carrier current at its outer edge. **Gauge test on the saved path.** A trial interaction \(g\int A\wedge\mathcal J\) on the collar requires a conserved current in its interior and an accounting of current at its boundaries. Write \(\omega\) for the normalized signed solid-angle form of the projected normal. At its moving zero, \(d\omega\) carries the signed index. For \(x=r_{\rm zero}/R-2\) running from 0 to 1, a gauge change with \(\lambda=x(1-x)\) pairs with the enclosed upper-mouth count as \(\int_0^1 \lambda'(x)Q(x)\,dx\). The endpoint limits are taken from inside the collar. The entries are geometric units before multiplication by \(g\).
