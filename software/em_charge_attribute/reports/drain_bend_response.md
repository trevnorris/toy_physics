# Can a draining mouth repel another mouth through the brane bend?

**A throughflow can give the electric force sign if the inlet turns with the brane, the outlet retains a separate direction, and the mouth receives the turning reaction.** A pressure-inclusive example gives inverse-square repulsion for like orientations and attraction for opposites. It fails the common-charge test with the same widening geometry at the two physical radii. The existing order-conversion equations do not select the two flow directions or the part of the force received by the mouth. **The two one-mouth measurements.** With orientation \(s\), measure source strength \(L(R)>0\) from the far bend \(h=sL/(4\pi\kappa r)\). Independently, apply a weak distant gradient \(\mathbf g\) and measure the force on the physical mouth, \(sB(R)\mathbf g\), from the negative of the holding reaction. Keep the incident value at the mouth fixed and use the same material preparation. The leading outward force on a target at distance \(d\) is
\[
F_{\rm out}=-\frac{s_t s_s B(R_t)L(R_s)}{4\pi\kappa d^2}.
\]
This assumes a single orientation-odd far-field channel. Reciprocal repulsion requires a common negative \(B/L\). A common nonzero force strength for every ordered radius pairing requires both \(B\) and \(L\) separately constant. The positive-stiffness scalar supplies \(+sL\mathbf g\) to a closed cut. Its passive sign is attractive. In the historical equal-bare-source annulus, the same target already measures 1.041412 times the strength from the larger emitter. Adjusting the target response alone leaves that difference. [Scalar and annulus calculations](unequal_mouth_electric.md) · [Guarded outputs](../../../docs/_measurements/drain_bend_response.json)

**What the energy test found.** At a stationary guide, a lossless stream can deliver a sideways force with zero mechanical power to the guide. During translation its pressure work and kinetic-energy flux account for the force times the guide velocity. A uniform height offset or a reading of steady drain power alone cannot determine the force. The old fixed-throughput controller removes the actual carrier momentum and enthalpy and copies their integrals at the remote return. There is no direct response of its kernels to an incident bend. The operative model conserves material and changes its order. Its rate and mechanical exchange during a bend remain open. **An independent receiver test.** The existing open, quiescent half-space benchmark produces a repulsive-sense reaction on the supplier of its inner cut. Its leading reaction falls as \(d^{-5}\). At inverse-square order the horizontal and vertical components of the intact-screen pressure force cancel in the projection onto the mouth’s tangent. The separate inlet supplier receives the opposite resultant. Ownership of the supplier, screen and far reaction determines how much acts on a freely translating mouth. **Decisive physical test.** For each radius, measure the far bend, the load on the assembly that would translate with the throat, and the pressure-inclusive momentum entering and leaving that assembly. Take all four combinations of mouth orientation and gradient sign, repeat at half gradient, and measure the receiver/support reactions separately. One test distinguishes whether the material keeps a direction through conversion and whether its turning force returns to the mouth. The present fixed-screen archive lacks those readings. The same physical intake law would also enter 0PN gravity; these electric measurements do not determine free-fall inertia. Working result on base e0a94594.
