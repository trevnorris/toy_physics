# Can the brane or standing wave give one electric unit per mouth?

**The signed geometry supplies radius independence for a candidate source. The specified mechanical equations give zero orientation-odd coupling to the long-range electric potential on matched mirrors.** The same constituent charge, intake, bulk preparation and brane field normalization are held fixed. The target is the excess monopole after the ordinary matter contribution is accounted for. **What the electric field would need to see.** Let \(j_N^\mu\) be a conserved current with integrated count \(N=s=\pm1\) on a once-wrapped mouth. For an electric potential \(\phi\) nearly uniform across the mouth, a coupling to this current would give interaction energy \(g\,s\,\phi\). Its current coupling must include the spatial vector potential as the mouth moves. In the paper’s normalization the charge seen by the canonical long-range field would be \(g\,s/\sqrt{Z_{\rm int}}\) for fixed localization profile. The integer fixes the relative signs and units. The coefficient \(g\) has units of electric charge. [Field normalization](../../../research/4d_em_fields/paper/4d_em_fields.tex#L845)

**Radius check.** On a round, once-wrapped conversion layer, represent the ordered-to-disordered coordinate by \(0<u<1\), with \(h(u)=-d\chi/du\) and \(\int_0^1 h\,du=1\). On a section at radius \(r\), the signed weight per area and per \(du\) is \(s h(u)/(4\pi r^2)\). Integrating over the section cancels \(r^2\) exactly. We also let \(r=2R(t)+\ell(t)u\), so the mouth radius and layer width can vary independently. The resulting geometric density and radial current obey local continuity for arbitrary smooth loss profile. For the trial electric current \(g(R,I)j_N^\mu\), its divergence per geometric density is \(g_R\dot R+g_I\dot I\), where \(I\) is trapped-wave action. Conservation without exchange requires \(g\) constant along that history. It does not pick a nonzero value or enforce equality across disconnected preparations at different fixed actions. If the layer unwinds, the current also needs a physical continuation across the point where the projected normal vanishes. **What the action supplies.** The long-range scalar potential is even under \(w\mapsto-w\), while \(s\) changes sign. The neutral wave energy \(I\omega\) can exert a radial load \(-I\,\partial_R\omega\) at fixed action, but it has zero direct derivative with respect to this potential. Even when the wave or brane reshapes the carrier density, exact mirror preparations have identical depth-integrated \(q_\star n\) at each brane position. Their electric monopole difference vanishes. The existing orientation-dependent rim term drives the mechanical bend scalar \(H\) with a postulated coefficient. It supplies no identification with the Maxwell potential. **Missing physical input.** A microscopic interface law would need to tie reversal of the mouth to an opposite electric source at fixed carrier charge. One possible test is whether each orientation binds a fixed excess or deficit of charged constituents relative to the same background, independent of radius and wave preparation. That would require an electrical distinction beyond pure depth reflection. Any such bound charge must be counted once, with the compensating charge and boundary flux tracked. A local polarization that dies off outside a closed region has zero integrated charge when its boundary charge is included. The throat and mixing terms in the material energy remain placeholders.
