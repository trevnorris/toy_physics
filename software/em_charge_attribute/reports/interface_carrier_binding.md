# Can mirrored mouths bind opposite amounts of material?

**The specified interface laws give equal integrated constituent excesses on exact mirror preparations.** Each comparison holds the inward rate \(D>0\), constituent charge \(q\), radius \(R\), trapped-wave energy \(E\), and reflected reservoir data fixed. This result applies separately at each \(R,E\). Carrier density transforms as \(n_-(\mathbf x,w)=n_+(\mathbf x,-w)\). Integrating its departure from a common mirror-symmetric background over reflected regions gives identical numbers. The oriented bend fields have a different reflection rule. A common wave-induced density change can therefore coexist with a zero charge difference. [Field transformation](../g0_closure_card_v0.md#L21) · [Wave response](trapped_wave_charge.md)

**What the interface determines.** The open-mouth matching conserves the relative number current, and the simplest permeable interface carries no independent surface number. The linear face law relates flux to chemical-potential and pressure perturbations. The order balance converts material between ordered and disordered fractions with equal and opposite sources. None selects a stationary excess on one orientation. The more general throat surface energy is an open input. **Weak contact calculation.** To see what a specified binding interaction could do, use the existing conservative contact condition \(\hbar^2[\partial_z\psi]/(2m)=h\psi\), where \(h=\partial g/\partial n\) is evaluated on the reference state. Take a stationary, locally planar, weak contact, common far density \(n_0\) on both sides, fixed order profile, and uniform flux \(j=D/\mathcal A\) through true contact area \(\mathcal A\). Linearizing the carrier equation gives

\[
-a\,\delta n''+k_j\,\delta n=-h\,\delta(z),\qquad
a=\frac{\hbar^2}{4mn_0},\qquad k_j=\mu'(n_0)-\frac{mj^2}{n_0^3}>0 .
\]

On this subsonic branch the density disturbance decays on length \(\sqrt{a/k_j}\). Integrating gives \(\Delta N=-\mathcal A h/k_j\) to first order. A repulsive contact depletes both mirrors and an attractive contact enriches both. On this single transmitting stream the entire number departure also equals \(D\,\Delta\tau\), with \(\Delta\tau\) the change in crossing time relative to the uniform stream. A density enhancement alone cannot establish independent storage. This reduction supplies a sensitivity check for the interface. The curved draining throat has no independently solved density profile in this test. **Requirement for a signed unit.** In the weak contact reduction, an integrated half-difference \(N_*=(\Delta N_+-\Delta N_-)/2\) would require \(h_{\rm odd}=(h_+-h_-)/2=-N_* k_j/\mathcal A\) at every radius and wave energy. Exact opposites relative to the same background would also require the common excess to vanish. The displayed scalar contact gives \(h_{\rm odd}=0\). A mechanism based on an independently occupied localized state would instead need to predict its occupation, why the mirror selects the complementary deficit, and where the compensating material goes.
