# Where the signed count goes on the relaxed path

**The conversion layer can lose its entire geometric count locally.** Its radial count flux is zero on both faces throughout the saved deformation. The equations also admit an exact geometric accounting in which a moving point picks up the missing unit and reaches the bulk-side edge. Whether any material or electric current carries that unit farther is open. **Follow the point.** The neck relaxation changes the shape only inside \(r=2R\). In the conversion layer \(2R<r<3R\), the projected normal points along \(\mathbf x/R-\beta\hat z\) for the upper mouth. It vanishes at \(\mathbf a=R\beta\hat z\), where the physical face stays smooth. As the tilt \(\beta\) increases, this point passes each spherical section in turn. After passage the directions on that section have degree zero. Let \(\chi\) be the remaining shear-bearing fraction, falling from one to zero. Weighting the section degrees by \(-d\chi\) gives \(N_{\rm layer}=s\chi(\mathbf a)\) during passage, where \(s=\pm1\) labels the orientation. Both saved paths give the same values. **Local balance.** Write \(\omega\) for the projected direction’s signed solid-angle form divided by \(4\pi\). The layer current is \(\mathcal J_L=-d\chi\wedge\omega\). Its flux through the history of any fixed radial section vanishes because \(\chi\) is constant there. Away from the zero, \(d\omega=0\). A small sphere around the zero encloses signed index \(s\), so distributionally \(K=d\omega\) is a unit current of that sign along the point’s trajectory. Thus \(d\mathcal J_L=d\chi\wedge K\). For the upper mouth the integrated local change per unit tilt is \(-30(\beta-2)^2(3-\beta)^2\) between 2 and 3 and vanishes outside. Its integral is \(-1\). The mirror gives \(+1\). Tilt labels configurations and supplies no physical clock. **A possible conserved current.** Adding \((1-\chi)K\) yields \(d[(1-\chi)\omega]\), whose divergence vanishes including at the zero. This assigns the point zero incoming weight and \(s(1-\chi)\) during passage. For the upper mouth it carries half a unit at mid-layer and one unit on arrival at the outer edge. This calculation ends at that edge. The material and order balances track constituent number and conversion, and the displayed Maxwell source assigns matter current \(q_\star j\). At the matched intake \(D\), its integrated carrier flux remains \(q_\star D\) even if the tilt is paused. No term in those equations identifies the weighted moving point with a physical carrier. Establishing export would require a conversion law or independent bulk-current measurement accounting for the signed excess after subtracting ordinary throughput and including any interfacial storage.
