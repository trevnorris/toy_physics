# Can a distant electric probe tell which way a drain opens?

**The modeled through-flow gives zero orientation-dependent long-range charge.** Give each constituent the same test charge \(q\) in both preparations. Take a draining mouth and reflect the whole preparation in \(w\), keeping its inward number rate positive. Vary the matter action with respect to a weak static electric potential with the same laboratory depth profile \(f(w)\) in both cases. The source is \(q f(w)|\psi|^2\). The massless electric mode has \(f_0=1\). Moving any carrier density profile to the opposite side leaves its coupling to that mode exactly unchanged. This also holds locally in the three brane coordinates and after subtracting any mirror-matched transit or background profile. The usual charge dictionary supplies opposite signs by assigning \(q_\star=s e_\star\). Here \(q\) is held fixed to test what the orientation itself contributes. [Matter action and source](../../../research/4d/paper/4d.tex#L1879) · [Depth modes](../../../research/4d_em_fields/paper/4d_em_fields.tex#L894)

**How much charge is in transit?** We solved the existing stationary carrier equation from a round inlet through a tube of length \(1\) into a finite receiving chamber. The chamber radius and total axial length were both \(3\). The inlet, tube-to-chamber cut and exit each carried \(0.25\) at mouth radii \(1\) and \(2\), on grids with 8 and 16 cells per unit length. The mirror follows by reflecting this same solution. For each cell, the solved density gives the number present and the directed link currents give its outgoing rates. A passive tracer entering with the feed then has a calculable mean time to exit. Multiplying this time by the inlet rate reconstructs the number in transit. Every occupied cell was reachable from the feed and connected to the exit by directed flow. This is a conservation check using the same carrier field. **A probe that distinguishes the two depths gives a different reading.** For \(f_1(w)=2w/\lambda\), with \(q=\lambda=1\) and the inlet at \(w=0\), subtract the uniform resting density over the same occupied volume. At radius \(1\) the fine-grid source moment was \(-0.00673816387097\) and its mirror gave \(+0.00673816387097\). At radius \(2\) the pair was \(\mp0.00139494373388\). In the adopted Gaussian Maxwell model this depth mode has inverse range \(\sqrt{2}/\lambda\) and vanishes on the midplane. Its source changes sign under reflection even as the long-range electric monopole stays the same. SymPy and Mathematica checked both mode projections. **What remains open.** The solver evolves a carrier in a fixed opening. It has no independent measurement of the material losing shear order or of a charge attached to the supported mouth. As a detection control, adding a separate charge \(g\) times the signed layer count recovered \(+g\) and \(-g\) after transit subtraction at both radii. The physical value of \(g\) remains undetermined. The next question is whether the trapped wave and rim can carry an independently conserved quantity that reverses with the opening at fixed inward flow, and whether the weak electric potential couples to it with the same strength as the wave energy and radius change.
