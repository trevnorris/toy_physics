# What makes an upper mouth positive and its mirror negative?

**None of the three mechanisms selects the electric sign from the specified mouth equations.** The comparison holds radius, inward intake and constituent charge fixed and reflects the reservoirs. A successful rule must select opposite signs, preserve one magnitude as radius and wave energy vary, and source the electric field.

**Trapped wave.** The support wave is scalar. Give it two tangent copies and measure their rotation in fixed laboratory axes. The cavity prescribes a displacement node at the mouth. Variation of the gradient energy gives the free-end condition \(T\partial_n\mathbf u=0\). At equal end amplitude \(A\), all four side–polarization combinations have frequency \(\pi c/(2L)\), oscillating rim traction of magnitude \(AT\pi/(2L)\), and zero mean axial wave power. Here \(L\) is cavity depth and \(T\) the gradient stiffness. The scalar conversion-layer loss acts equally on both copies. The full brane action also couples a polar field to the curl of displacement. Its flat-brane term supplies no matched polarization-dependent rim response for this cavity. A real symmetric local end stiffness can mix circular waves. Each circular diagonal element equals half its trace. Adding \(s\gamma(x\dot y-y\dot x)/2\) to the mode action where the displacement is nonzero splits the frequencies by \(\gamma/M\), with the preference reversed on the mirror. This sensitivity test supplies the coupling. Deriving it, selecting the occupation and coupling it to an electric probe are separate requirements. [Polarization and probe](trapped_wave_charge.md)

**Localized material.** Reflection maps the scalar carrier operator and matched boundary data to their mirror. Bound partners have equal energies and reflected densities. As an explicit linear planar test, the contact \(C[\psi']=h\psi\), \(C=\hbar^2/(2m)\), transmits the same current fraction \(4C^2k^2/(4C^2k^2+h^2)\) on either mouth. For \(h<0\) it binds a state at \(-h^2/(4C)\) carrying zero current. Intake therefore leaves its population undetermined in this example. The nonlinear draining throat's spectrum and loading law remain open. [Mirror convention](../g0_closure_card_v0.md#L21) · [Contact and fixed-intake inventory](interface_carrier_binding.md)

**Flux endpoint.** Enumerating two neighboring spin-ice tetrahedra before attaching a flux string gives 18 neutral configurations and 10 configurations for each sign of the elementary pair, with one for each sign of a double pair. The diagonal defect cost \(U\sum_i Q_i^2/2\) favors neutral sites. Add a hypothetical binding energy \(-\lambda\sum_i s_iQ_i\) for opposite mouths. The pair energy becomes \(Uq^2-2\lambda s_{\rm left}q\), where \(q=Q_{\rm left}=-Q_{\rm right}\). For \(U>0\), a single unit of the favored sign minimizes this energy precisely when \(1/2<\lambda/U<3/2\). Adjacent sectors tie at either endpoint. This static calculation omits hopping and driven loading. The auxiliary lattice supplies the flux degrees of freedom. The mouth action supplies no such binding energy.
