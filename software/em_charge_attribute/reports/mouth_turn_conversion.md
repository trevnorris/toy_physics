# Does conversion preserve the mouth’s signed turn?

**A vertical conversion region can preserve one geometric unit.** Follow a round face continuously from the flat feeding brane through the mouth. Let \(\ell\) increase toward the bulk, \(r\) be the radius, \(s=\pm1\) the orientation, and \((r',w')=(-\cos\theta,s\sin\theta)\). The continuous normal is \((s\sin\theta\,\hat r,\cos\theta)\). The three principal curvatures are \(s\theta',-s\sin\theta/r,-s\sin\theta/r\). Their product integrated over the swept area gives signed turn \(sC(\theta)\) from the flat feed, where \(C(\theta)=(2\theta-\sin2\theta)/\pi\). Continuing the normal matters after the waist. For an upper mouth, a smooth cap that turns back to \(\theta=0\) gives total \(0\), a vertical tube gives \(1\), and a fully flared second flat end at \(\theta=\pi\) gives \(2\). These are alternative continuations of the same exterior lip. **Order loss.** As a diagnostic, weight the turning by the shear-bearing fraction \(\chi\), decreasing from one to zero. Integration by parts gives \(N_{\rm ord}=s\int(-d\chi)\,C(\theta)\). A vertical segment throughout conversion gives exactly \(s\) for any loss profile or mouth radius. A layer centered symmetrically in turning angle can also give \(s\). Across angles from \(60^\circ\) to \(120^\circ\), set \(\theta=\pi/2+\pi u/6\), \(-1\leq u\leq1\), and \(-d\chi/du=3(1-u^2)(1+b u)/4\). For the upper mouth the measured counts were \(0.935866111677,\ 1.000000000000,\ 1.064133888323\) for \(b=-1/2,0,1/2\). The existing order balance leaves the actual conversion profile and its position in the throat open. **Electric probe.** Varying the displayed matter, order and shear terms against a weak static potential of depth profile \(f(w)\) yields \(q_\star f(w)|\psi|^2\). Shear loss introduces no explicit electric term in this variation. In the adopted localized Maxwell theory the massless mode is constant in \(w\); the factor \(Z(w)\) weights the field energy, while the mode samples the unweighted physical source. Equal and opposite localized electric sources at different finite depths therefore cancel in its distant monopole if the outer \(w\)-boundary field flux vanishes. A brane-local diagnostic can remain nonzero in the same preparation. Reflection of a density profile alone leaves its integral unchanged at common \(q_\star\). In the charge dictionary the sign change of \(q_\star\) is supplied separately. **Missing conversion law.** The geometric current \(j_g^\mu\) obeys a continuity identity, while \(\partial_\mu(\chi j_g^\mu)=j_g^\mu\partial_\mu\chi\). A physical identification with its weighted part needs an interface or bulk current accounting for this exchange. A stationary face has zero geometric transport even while matter drains through it. The model has yet to identify a charge bound to the mouth, distinct from through-flow, and derive its coupling to the distant mode with all compensating sources included.
