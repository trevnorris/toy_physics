**Order response at onset.** Use the constant-transverse-inertia branch and the optional order-relaxation term with positive mobility \(M_\chi\). In the cycle-averaged weak drive, the shear energy adds \(\mu_0 A^2 k^2\sin^2(kx+\phi)/4\) to the order chemical potential. Count this relaxation once in the net order rate. Assume the other source, flux and material-advection responses have no incremental phase-odd contribution at onset; equal total intake alone cannot enforce that assumption. With outward radial order slope \(p>0\), implicit differentiation of the contour and angular integration give
\[
\dot Y(w)=\frac{3M_\chi\mu_0 A^2 k^2}{8p}\,j_1(2kR(w))\sin(2\phi).
\]
A wall initially at incremental rest under bounded force retains \(\dot X(0^+)=0\). For \(M_\chi=\mu_0=p=1,\ A=0.1,\ k=1/2\), the positive-phase order speeds are **0.000282346** at the midplane (\(R=1\)) and **0.000371412** at either end (\(R=1.5\)), in benchmark units. Their ratio is **1.31545**. The earlier force ratio **2.95977** includes a section-area factor. SymPy/SciPy extracted both centroids from separate surfaces at three small Taylor increments. Mathematica differentiated the volume moment. The finest-step speed error was below \(1.2\times10^{-9}\). Reflection reversed the order signal, halving the wave amplitude gave one quarter of it at onset, and adding a uniform drift affected the extracted velocities by less than \(1.6\times10^{-14}\). **Can the rim subsequently follow?** As a sensitivity test, let \(x,y\) be the geometry and order bow amplitudes. Use unit wall inertia, restoring coefficients and order friction in dimensionless units; scale displacement by the signed initial order-bow velocity times the time unit. A passive attachment with energy \(c(x-y)^2/2\) gives \(\ddot x+x=c(y-x)\), \(\dot y+y+c(y-x)=1\) for a normalized positive step. From rest the wall jerk is \(c\). At one test time unit, \((x,y)=(0,0.632121)\) for \(c=0\) and \((0.097083,0.451051)\) for \(c=1\). Both engines recover these trajectories; the reduced energy balance gives zero residual. These illustrative contacts share the same incoming drive and give different rim responses.
