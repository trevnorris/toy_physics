# Can the sideways rim stress supply an electric charge?

**For two mouths whose lateral loads each balance locally, direct transmission through the homogeneous elastic brane gives a pair force of order \(1/d^4\) or smaller at large separation \(d\).** A sideways pull can also load the brane along \(w\) if it changes the depth separation of two attachments. That response can reverse under a mouth reflection. The equations give a continuously adjustable induced load and leave the source of an undriven mouth undetermined. **Range of the direct pull.** Use the static three-dimensional displacement energy of the previous test, with constant positive compression coefficient \(B\) and curl coefficient \(\mu\). Its point-force response along the line of force is \(G_{xx}(d)=1/(4\pi\mu d)\). For two fixed, compact force distributions with zero net force apiece, terms involving \(G\) or its first derivative cancel in the interaction. The leading possible work involves two derivatives of \(G\), hence scales as \(d^{-3}\). As an exact control, represent each mouth by two opposite collinear unit forces a distance \(b\) apart. The cross work is \(2G_{xx}(d)-G_{xx}(d-b)-G_{xx}(d+b)=-b^2/[2\pi\mu d(d^2-b^2)]\) for \(d>b\). An uncompensated lateral force per mouth on the brane can restore a \(1/d\) work term. That case requires tracking the compensating reaction in the holder, wave, or bulk and its direction. The live drain's lateral force balance is unmeasured. **Can a sideways pull source the normal bend?** Let \(Y\) be the sideways offset of the far order contour relative to the held rim, and \(h\) a broad, dimensionless displacement along \(w\), with physical displacement \(\ell h\). Relax the near contour. The layer compliance is \(C=\int dw/T(w)\), where \(T\) is its lateral tension. The surrounding brane and the rim attachment act in parallel with incremental stiffnesses \(K_e,c\). For a centered, conservative quadratic branch with \(K_e+c>0\), the effective compliance is \(C_{\rm eff}=C+1/(K_e+c)\). Writing \(P=Y/C_{\rm eff}\) for the arriving force gives

\[
 E=E_0(h)+\frac{Y^2}{2C_{\rm eff}(h)},\qquad
 Q_h(Y)-Q_h(0)=\frac{P^2}{2}\,\partial_h C_{\rm eff},\qquad Q_h=-\partial_h E\big|_Y .
\]

The derivative holds the other controls fixed and includes any prescribed motion of supports. With finite positive compliance, freeing \(Y\) gives the unique minimum \(Y=P=0\) in this channel. In a dimensionless example the tension varies as \(2(1+v)^2\), \(0\le v\le1\), and the resting length is 3. With the far depth fixed, \(\ell=1\), and \(K_e=c=2\), the \(+w\) mouth gives \(-1/800\) for **either** \(Y=+1/10\) or \(-1/10\). Its mirror gives \(+1/800\) for \(Y=+1/10\). Either half-strength shift on the original gives \(-1/3200\). Moving both ends together in depth gives zero induced bend source with identical lateral forces. These are mechanical forces conjugate to \(h\), in benchmark units. **What remains measurable.** At controlled \(h\), adding \(-s q_0 h\) to \(E_0\), for orientation \(s=\pm1\), changes the bend source by \(s q_0\) while leaving every lateral force reading identical. Thus even ideal lateral readings cannot determine the undriven source \(Q_h(0)\). The saved intake-\(0.25\) trajectories measure carrier load at the feed. The separate collar benchmark tracks wall and wave displacements. Neither supplies synchronized order slope, exterior material displacement and independent rim reaction. Reopen this route if a trapped state selects a persistent lateral load without an imposed shift. If this compliance mechanism alone supplies the source, \(P_*^2\,\partial_h C_{\rm eff}/2\) must have equal magnitude across radii and opposite signs on mirrors.
