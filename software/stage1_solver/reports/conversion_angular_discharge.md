# Can the conversion equations select the directional bulk discharge at 0PN?

**Result.** Even a known local rate of order loss and a fixed total intake \(D(a)\) leave the facing and away mass rates undetermined. The accepted model has one conserved material. With \(j=nu\) its number current, the supplied balances are \(\partial_t n+\nabla_4\cdot j=0\) and \(\partial_t(n\chi)+\nabla_4\cdot(\chi j+J_\chi)=n\Gamma_B\). The second balance includes independent transport of order \(J_\chi\). Its constitutive law and the active mouth's boundary response are open. Carrying the local brane velocity through conversion specifies the momentum per exchanged mass. It does not select the amount discharged at each angle. **What conversion can constrain.** On the upper slab face S12 gives \(C_+=-f j_N+b_{\rm out}-b_{\rm in}\). Here \(j_N\) is the common material number current relative to the face per projected area, \(f\) is the interior order fraction there, \(C_+\) is the face-concentrated order source, and the \(b\)'s are the two normal order-transport traces in the same convention. Where \(f>0\), those three inputs together would give \(j_N=(b_{\rm out}-b_{\rm in}-C_+)/f\). Where \(f=0\), that equation contains no \(j_N\). A spatially resolved material current must also be related to the downstream measuring sphere, including material that crosses the plane dividing the two halves. S12 distinguishes this native face from the excluded puncture interior. [Face coefficients](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/steps/S12_order_source.md#2-what-the-constructions-print-on-each-part-of-the-domain)

**Counterexample using the supplied balances.** On the comparison domain add a divergence-free number current \(\delta j\), and change the unspecified order transport by \(\delta J_\chi=-\chi\,\delta j\). This preserves the local material balance, the entire ordered current, and the same signed order source, including its face terms for continuous normal \(\delta j\). The two cases explore different admissible transport data. They do not establish two solutions of a completed brane–mouth boundary law. An earlier local carrier calculation likewise found an undetermined boundary amplitude even under one illustrative advected order-loss law. [Local carrier calculation](../../../docs/_measurements/conversion_region_weak_flow.txt)

In the constant-density 4D potential receiver, take \(\delta j=n_b\nabla_4[\beta(d)x/r^4]\), with \(+x\) toward the companion and the singularity inside the excised target. This decays at infinity and preserves the integrated discharge of both mouths. The change in \(B\) is \(\beta\). On the centered measuring sphere of radius \(R\), the mass rates change by

\[
\delta Q_{\rm facing}=-4\pi\rho_b\beta(d)/R,\qquad
\delta Q_{\rm away}=+4\pi\rho_b\beta(d)/R,\qquad \rho_b=m n_b .
\]

A pair-only \(\beta=k(d-d_*)(d_*/d)^2\) vanishes at infinite separation and at the chosen separation \(d_*\), yet changes \(B'(d_*)\) by \(k\). The two exterior fields therefore can agree even on the instantaneous flow distribution at \(d_*\). SymPy checked the conservation and angular integrals. Mathematica checked the face balance and recovered the dipole from the total normal velocities at two radii in three held configurations. The examples used \(k=0\) and \(k=1/20\) in benchmark units. Neither is a measured response. [Scripts, inputs and guarded results](../../../docs/_measurements/conversion_angular_discharge.json)

**Minimum readout.** In the source-free harmonic annulus of the preceding test, obtain \(M(R,d)=4\langle\mu v_N\rangle\) at two centered radii and nearby held separations, with \(\mu\) the cosine toward the companion. The two radii remove the regular incident dipole. Alternatively, an independently specified angular material-transfer and matching law can generate those fields. Two unweighted hemisphere totals suffice for the odd angular amplitude only if higher odd modes have been excluded. For example the profile \(\mu+\tfrac54(8\mu^3-4\mu)\) integrates to zero on each hemisphere but has unit dipole projection. The physical \(B'(d)\) remains an open mouth-boundary requirement.
