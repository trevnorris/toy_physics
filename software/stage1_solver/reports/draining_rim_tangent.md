# Weak sideways traction on a draining rim at 0PN

The radius-one preparation has carrier number intake \(Q=0.25\) and zero sideways reservoir speed. The observable is the change, relative to the unforced drain, in brane-material velocity along the drive at the equator. The center, rim geometry, feed, intake and support protocol are held fixed. The input \(q(t)\) is the increment in **net tangential traction on the brane**, per unit area, with the same dipole pattern around the rim in every run. [Preparation check](../../../docs/_measurements/draining_rim_material_balance.txt) · [Traction convention](one_mouth_direct_brane_traction.md)

**First variation.** Use a fixed laboratory control region. Let \(g_x\) be material momentum per coordinate volume, \(C_{xj}\) its spatial transport flux excluding force stress, \(\sigma_{xj}\) the tensile force stress on the same measure, and \(S_x\) all remaining signed volume supplies excluding carried loss. All geometric conversions belong in those quantities. For any differentiable dynamical realization of this balance, subscript 0 denotes its steady draining state and \(\delta\) the first-order change. The sideways component obeys

\[
\partial_t\delta g_x+\partial_j(\delta C_{xj}-\delta\sigma_{xj})
=\delta S_x-j_{n0}\,\delta V_x-V_{0x}\,\delta j_n .
\]

Here \(j_n\) is outward **brane material** loss on the ledger measure. Fixed total carrier intake neither supplies this local field nor enforces \(\delta j_n=0\) pointwise. The known \(q(t)\) enters once as the boundary load. The equation is the variation of conservation, conditional on a time-dependent realization. The ledger supplies steady accounting. To evaluate the left side one needs how momentum, transport and stress vary with the perturbed state, including strain history and any coupled fields. Those derivatives at this drain are open. Even granting ordinary advected momentum \(g=\rho V,\ C_{xj}=\rho V_xV_j\) and sourced continuity, the direct carried-loss term cancels against mass loss in the conservative left side. Background advection, the velocity gradient and the incremental stress remain. This conditional simplification was checked in both engines. The optical modulus and a relaxed steady stress cannot determine the missing time dependence. For example, two incremental shear laws with the same instantaneous modulus \(G_\infty\), zero relaxed modulus, and relaxation times \(\tau\) and \(2\tau\) give \(G_\infty/2\) and \(2G_\infty/3\) at Laplace rate \(s=1/\tau\). These are local examples demonstrating an undetermined derivative. Neither is selected for the drain. [SymPy and Mathematica checks](../../../docs/_measurements/draining_rim_tangent.txt)

**Minimal measurement for this rim observable.** Record net equatorial traction and brane-material velocity there as synchronized time histories on independently prepared runs. Obtain \(v_0(t)\) with zero added load. Apply opposite weak tractions \(q_\pm(t)=\pm h f(t)\) of known shape and form \(R_f(t)=[v_{+h}(t)-v_{-h}(t)]/(2h)\). Check that the sign average returns to the baseline to first order. Reserve \(\pm h/2\) and predict their histories as \(v_0(t)\pm(h/2)R_f(t)\) to first order. Include contact reaction in net traction. The coefficient \(R_f\) predicts scaled copies of this waveform. For other waveforms, identify the causal traction-to-velocity response over the required frequency band, measuring amplitude and phase where the input excites it. These histories require an independent material readout or a model that resolves the incremental material dynamics. The saved carrier fields contain no such readout. **Tracking the material at the fixed rim.** A brane tag with material label \(X\) has position \(x(X,t)\). Differentiate the position at fixed label, then sample the sideways component of that velocity at the brane-side equator. As material drains past the sampling point, use successive passing tags or interpolate the resolved material velocity to the same point at each time. The archived \(Q=0.25\) preparation has neither tag positions nor this velocity field. Its scalar phase can track carrier motion, which can slip tangentially relative to the brane. The material map in the ledger defines the observable but supplies no trajectories for these tractions. A passive brane tag obeys \(\partial_t x(X,t)=V_{\rm br}(x(X,t),t)\) up to its exit. To integrate even the first forced trajectory, the solver needs the local material velocity under the applied traction. The equal-intake calculation exhibits a change in material mass current with zero divergence, zero change in normal current at the rim, and an arbitrary sideways component at the equator. Thus even fixing the local normal material current would leave the sideways tag speed undetermined by mass balance. Selecting that speed requires the draining brane's momentum and stress response. Independently observed tag tracks would allow a measurement without first deriving that response.
