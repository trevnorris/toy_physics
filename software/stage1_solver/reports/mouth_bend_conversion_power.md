# Can conversion power a draining mouth’s restoring force?

**A bent mouth can spend less energy converting ordered brane into bulk.** At fixed intake 0.25, the saving in the passive conversion calculation exceeds the renewal cost of the proposed wall contacts. Whether those contacts receive any of it remains open. This tests internal alignment at 0PN. The mouth was held still throughout. [Previous force and contact-power test](mouth_bend_flow_reaction.md)

We used the same radius-one four-dimensional mouth, deeply ordered delayed flare, empty wave, and equal-frequency reservoirs. We relaxed both the neutral flow and the finite-width order layer for each imposed bend, with flow driving order in one direction. The positive conversion loss is the volume integral of \((\mathbf u\cdot\nabla\chi)^2/M\). Here \(\chi\) measures shear order and \(M=5\). Values below are rounded model power. **How material strain changes the comparison.** The written shear and trial contact energies depend on order. Including both derivatives increases the conversion loss at leading order on the prescribed carrier. The remaining saving per squared displacement is about 0.00366, against renewal of 0.000571 for straight-prepared contacts or 0.000556 for traction-free entry. Redirecting about **16%** or **15%**, respectively, would cover renewal if a source funds conversion in the straight state. These are formal weak-bend requirements. At a bend of 0.01, adding the leading strain correction to the bent order field gives an unphysical angular mean near −0.0000053 downstream. A bounded finite-amplitude solution is open. [Strain calculation](../tools/mouth_bend_conversion_feedback.py) · [Comparison and controls](../tools/mouth_bend_conversion_readout.py)

**Where the work and force go.** The bare conversion reaction on the material is \(\mu_\chi\nabla\chi\). Its sideways component integrates to −0.002499 at +0.01 and reverses with the bend. This is a force exchanged between flow and order in the trial law. Material energy crossing internal cuts includes both transported order energy and stress work. The distant reservoirs supplied essentially zero net bare order power in that accounting. The bare order stress has zero tangential traction at a naturally reflecting wall. The spring calculation also finds that weakening contacts *release* stored energy as they travel downstream. No measured term delivers the saved conversion power back to the renewing contacts.
