# Independent work check on the radius-four material and conversion-layer exchange

The displaced core and collar still feed a dense subsonic annulus at the held radius-four geometry. The initial pointwise transfer assigned different work to the same virtual displacement in the material and layer solvers. Using the same material quadrature cured the discrepancy and allowed both residuals to converge on the held mouth.

I reconstructed the original material labels and displaced physical positions at 3,200 points in the core and ordered half of the collar. Centered differences of these two coordinate maps gave the deformed four-dimensional gradient, including both angular stretches. The reconstructed stored energy per current volume, \(W/J\), agreed with the value returned to the carrier grid within \(5.84\times10^{-9}\) after normalization by \(\max(1,|W/J|)\). The minimum sampled Jacobian of the incremental displacement was 0.64455. Both returned carrier preparations used the displacement computed with winding and the appropriate original carrier state for their winding sector. [Independent transfer calculation](../tools/mouth_bonded_front_transfer_review.py)

The outer wall, waist and exit were fixed. I varied the amplitude of the saved bonded displacement around its relaxed value, holding the Eulerian order profile fixed throughout. The contribution from the front's order change can be integrated as \(\int\delta\chi(y(X))W(F)\,dV_{\rm ref}\) on the solid or as \(\int\delta\chi(y)(W/J)\,dV_y\) on the joined grid. These integrals are equal in the continuum. The two implementations sample different elements and quadrature points.

| 0.0004 | −0.49290621 | −0.77573969 | −0.28283348 |

The total material derivative along the same direction was +0.00000531 at the smaller step. The corresponding joined-grid discrepancy is large enough to change the stationarity verdict. The corrected scheme makes the transfer reciprocal by depositing each solid integration point’s \(W\,dV_{\rm ref}\) into the same order basis functions used to evaluate \(\delta\chi\) on the moving solid. This introduces no material parameter. The check applies to the saved displacement and its driving order at accepted iterate 309. It establishes a discrete work mismatch and leaves the physical shape force open. **Independent recheck of the corrected exchange.** I integrated the joined order basis at the displaced quadrature points using six Gaussian samples in each interval of polar angle. For the measured angle-dependent order, the direct and deposited works were −1.400828410630341 and −1.400828410630343. Their displacement derivatives were −0.492910381566514 and −0.492910381567069. An independent, angularly varying signed order perturbation agreed in value to 7.5 × 10⁻¹⁷ and in its displacement derivative to 2.0 × 10⁻¹³. [Independent deposition check](../tools/mouth_bonded_front_adjoint_review.py)

The final state gave a maximum free material-force residual of **5.07 × 10⁻⁶** and a scaled carrier/front residual of **1.82 × 10⁻⁷**. The reference volume and current measure gave consistent signed total-energy derivatives for material and order variations about a deliberately perturbed state as well. This establishes stationarity for bonded core and inner-collar displacements and the joined carrier/front fields with the exterior geometry held.
