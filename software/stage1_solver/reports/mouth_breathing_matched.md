# Breathing motion after removing the original torsional wave

**The lowest axisymmetric shape motion pushes the exit outward at both radii on the wave-empty backgrounds.** The two higher trial modes press inward at each radius. Each calculation holds the shape with constant generalized loads to permit the small oscillation. The original four-coordinate material equilibrium was prepared with a degree-four wave. **Force and displacement.** At each signed physical exit $b$ we recomputed the potential energy of the registered inner crystal, receiving-side order and crystal, and two-sided steady carrier. The interior order field is held in its material coordinates. The generalized mass is the same independently integrated lattice inertia as in the original scan. We used coordinates linear in physical depth, terminal radius and middle width. Every registered position changes affinely in these coordinates and a constant holding load contributes zero tangent stiffness. The measured generalized eigenvalue is $K v=\omega^2 M v$ and $v^T M v=1$. With $F_1=-\partial_b\omega$, the held-background balancing estimate is $J=(\partial_b E_{\rm empty})/F_1$. Using the indicated finite signed steps keeps the numerator and denominator on the same preparation. Outgoing fluid and dynamical order inertia are still absent. **Amplitude.** Each material displacement has peak modal coordinate $\sqrt{2J/\omega}$. At present depth fraction $t$ and radius $r$, $u_r=r\,\delta b(t)/b(t)$ and $u_w=t\,\delta L$. The reported displacement and absolute principal strain are sampled on the physical wall $r=b(t)$ at 2,049 depths. These are spatial lower bounds for the stated oscillatory envelope. The volume changes for both lowest modes; the coupling of a real moving phase boundary to outgoing sound remains an independent input. The slow order entry uses the existing collective interior order coordinate $z$ and the measured derivative $dz/ds=-E_{zs}/E_{zz}$ for a weak imposed mode displacement $s$, with constant background holding load. It extrapolates the largest linear material-order amplitude in this one coordinate to the conditional action. Its fully frozen material value is identically zero by construction. The frequency-dependent physical order amplitude and nonlinear material damage are undetermined. [Order probes](../tools/mouth_breathing_matched_order.py)

**Resolution and retained results.** On the fine radius-1 sample one receiving-side minimization ended at residual $2.21670\times10^{-7}$, outside its line-search acceptance criterion. Its valid central mass and stiffness were reused. The missing slopes were obtained from signed Rayleigh curvatures in the three central eigenvectors, whose stationarity gives the first derivative of each simple eigenfrequency. The radius-1 force magnitude and threshold action have strong mesh dependence, although the signs repeat. The radius-2 full and half steps track all three eigenvectors separately. The slow-order amplitude there changes from 0.18488 to 0.05742 across material grids, even though its force and wall displacement agree more closely.
