# Sound and force on a moving conversion layer

A planar layer drawn from the same reversible material radiates sound on both sides when its half-order surface is displaced. We measured the signed normal actuator force, the mass flow relative to that surface, and the independently outgoing power in both reservoirs. Reversing the steady current interchanges the two powers. This calibration determines an acoustic load a moving conversion layer can transmit. Curvature, the surrounding sealed duct, the attached crystal and a finite disk all affect how that load acts on the breathing mode. Forces are per unit interface area. Powers are per unit area and squared peak displacement. The sign convention is $\exp(-i\omega t)$, with positive direction from the brane to the receiver. For imposed displacement $X$ and complex external force $F$, the actuator supplies $\frac12\operatorname{Re}[F(-i\omega X)^*]$. At the tabulated throughflows the relative differences between this work and the two outgoing fluxes are below $1.2\times10^{-9}$ on the finest mesh. The imaginary part of the force gives positive radiated power. The real part includes the inertia of translating and distorting the profile.

**Material and drive.** The coherent reservoirs have unit distant density and the same steady normal current. The selected currents are the measured aperture averages, and the selected frequencies come from the two held shapes. The planar law uses the mouth's interface width 0.04, order inertia 0.0016, order-gradient stiffness 0.24 and double-well scale 75. The current distorts the stationary density and order through the resolved layer. We imposed a sinusoidal displacement of the half-order level by its conjugate force while allowing the remainder of the profile and carrier to respond. The projection onto an infinite plane is a choice of calibration geometry. **Equations and controls.** In the planar harmonic solve $u=j/n$, $\pi=I(\partial_t+u\partial_z)\chi$, $\partial_z\theta=u+\pi\partial_z\chi/n$, and $\partial_t\pi=\partial_z(\kappa\partial_z\chi-\pi u)-W'$. The fields include density amplitude, phase, current, order and the perturbed order traction. Across the driven level the order traction jumps by the applied force conjugate to order. Density amplitude and its derivative, phase, current and order perturbation are continuous. At either reservoir we retained the outgoing and evanescent Bogoliubov branches and the decaying order branch. The traveling wave satisfies $(\omega-u k)^2=k^2+k^4/4$. Summing its independent energy flux on both sides tests the work assigned to the actuator; the throughflow carries energy asymmetrically even for a centered drive.

The first pair of neutral scans resolved power but failed the specified tolerance on reactive force. Finer half-domains 16 and 21 with fourfold and sixfold spatial resolution reduced the largest observed relative refinement change in the reactive or radiative readouts to $2.79\times10^{-5}$. All three current settings and both sets of reference frequencies passed. The result measures a driven sheet. Inferring a lifetime of a curved mouth also requires its participating energy and the actual moving source on the side and terminal surfaces.
