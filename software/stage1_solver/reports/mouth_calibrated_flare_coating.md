# How much lateral support can the brane material give a draining mouth?

**A layer of the trial brane material can restore both sideways bends with no fitted wall tension.** Its force at the deeper flare depends on which material stays mechanically connected as shear order falls. Three ways of attaching the same material gave almost identical first-bend forces and sharply different flare forces. A uniform surface tension inferred at the flare missed the first-bend reading in all three cases.

We held the radius-one cubic mouth at intake 0.25 and wave action one in the original bath. We displaced each bend separately and measured external force conjugate to that displacement. For the flare test the entrance and middle stayed fixed. For the first bend the entrance stayed fixed and the middle and flare shifted together. All entries include the resting carrier and trapped-wave reaction. Slopes are in model force per unit sideways displacement. [Reference mouth and candidate tensions](mouth_occupied_slow_push.md)

| Material attachment along the wall | First-bend slope | Flare slope | Restores both tested bends? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Both bonded sheets follow the wall throughout | 0.224315 | 0.283529 | Yes |
| Both sheets follow, and only bonds between sheets weaken with shear order | 0.224315 | 0.283144 | Yes |
| The fraction of the entire coating carrying load falls with shear order | 0.224130 | 0.065036 | Yes |
| Sheets run around the spherical sections only | −0.012577 | 0.064194 | No |

**Where the force scale came from.** We used the compression calibration of the cold neutral two-layer material at 1% higher density, with bonds at their original resting planar spacing. We assumed full initial wall coverage by one bilayer of normal thickness 0.02 mouth radii, a fixed gap, and a thin membrane whose reference metric follows the prescribed wall. In the first three cases a material sheet runs along the wall in depth and along one angular tangent. We averaged that tangent uniformly on each spherical section. For the last case each sheet translates with a single section and undergoes zero stretch. Independent curvature elasticity, geometric selection of the resting flare, and flow-driven slip are open. [Neutral calibration](neutral_patch_zero_load.md) · [Coating and signed displacements](../tools/mouth_calibrated_flare_coating.py)

**What conversion changes.** In the second case we used the earlier material rule that weakens cross-sheet bonds, normalized at full order. Bonds within each sheet still carry load. Holding the same spacing as the other bonds weaken requires preload. In the third case the fraction carrying load was set equal to the local wall shear order on the archived deep conversion branch. At the flare that rule reduced the material contribution from 0.219336 to 0.000843. The flow and wave together supplied the remaining 0.064194. These are conditional attachment laws. The stationary order field fixes where order falls and leaves the force-bearing fraction undetermined. [Bond-resolved calculation](../tools/mouth_flare_bond_conversion.py) · [Conversion branch](mouth_free_order_depth.md)

**Check against a second displacement.** For the first preparation, the flare slope falls between the two earlier tension predictions of 0.215923 and 0.367653. The uniform tension that reproduces 0.283529 predicts a first-bend slope of 0.045147. The coating gives 0.224315. We also let common translation relax in the finite bath and checked all combinations of the two bend shapes. Both curvature eigenvalues stayed positive for each of the three depth-connected coatings. The circumferential control had a negative curvature eigenvalue. This is local static lateral stability for these shapes. Sustaining the original radial shape and accelerating a mouth require additional balances.

**Decision for 0PN.** Developing a net companion force into common acceleration requires a path for sideways stress to reach the flare. The material test supplies candidate forces for that path. The independent input is how much load the wall actually transfers to each sheet after order loss and material replacement. A discriminating reading would hold the same deep bend with only the wall driven, allow the neighboring sheet to slip, and record the wall reaction and relative motion as fresh material passes. This would test the attachment assumed here. Selecting a physical coating thickness, wave preparation and moving mass remains open. No companion release was run.
