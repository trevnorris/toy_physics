# How a companion's flow can accelerate a mouth at 0PN

A continuous carrier moving from a three-dimensional brane into a flared throat along the fourth spatial direction gives a conditional mechanism for free fall. When the entrance and flare move as one shape, solving for the center and carrier together gives an acceleration toward an incident brane flow. The calculation assigns zero bare mass to the shape. The material it moves supplies inertia. [Equations and assumptions](mouth_carrier_0pn_methods.md) · [Guarded measurements](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_carrier_0pn.json)

**One mouth.** At radius 1 and inward rate 0.25, a unit weak brane stream and a unit weak bulk stream produced wall forces of approximately +0.25 and −0.25 per unit speed. Towing through both resting reservoirs nearly cancels these reactions. Numerical resolution leaves the residual's sign open. The measured slow carrier inertia was 12.2605 on mesh 34. This predicts acceleration per incident speed of 0.0203915. An independent moving-boundary solve at angular frequency 0.01 gave 0.0204083 in phase and −0.0002012 in quadrature. The matched push gave 0.0815601 in phase per unit applied force. These are weak, slow drives around an established drain.

**Two separated mouths.** In the weak-bias model, both drains share the same distant reservoirs. Bringing them closer lowers the stationary carrier energy. Its derivative gives the attractive force. At separations of 8, 16 and 32 radii, doubling distance left 25.16%, 25.07% and 25.04% of the feeding-side force. The four-dimensional receiving flow adds a shorter-range attraction. This calculation represents each throat by its independently measured conductance and a round discharge profile. [Pair energy test](mouth_carrier_0pn_methods.md#two-mouth-energy)

**Does every radius fall alike?** I kept the material, slab thickness and distant phase difference fixed and let each intake emerge. Scaling the throat depth with its radius changed the acceleration per incident speed by a factor of 16.70 between radii 0.5 and 2. Keeping depth fixed reduced the spread across five radii to 2.12%. In a larger box, radius 4 responded 9.88% less than radius 1. Fixed depth supplies a leading scaling mechanism. This fixed-depth, fixed-bias family fails exact universality in the tested finite bath. [Radius comparison](mouth_carrier_radius_response.png)

**What the substrate must do.** Most of the incident pull acts near the entrance, while motion of the deeper flare accounts for most of the carrier’s inertial reaction. In two weak-intake partitions of the wall, common acceleration required transmitting 72.61% or 94.22% of the held load to the deeper coordinate. A tensile wall can transmit it in the trial calculation. A common excess pressure and an ordinary wall tension, separately or combined, also failed to select the depths needed for equal weak intake per carrier inertia at radii 2 and 4. [Depth selection and falsification](mouth_carrier_depth_selection.md)

The physical depth, lateral attachment stiffness and intake-setting phase relation need independent calibration. The decisive observation would be the relative displacement of the entrance and deeper material under a known sideways drive, repeated across radii. The earlier shear-contact calculation uses the same incoming momentum and cannot be added to the carrier force.
