# Can a wave-loaded conversion layer sustain a drain?

**A curved interface can drain steadily when the reservoirs supply energy. The saved radius-one throat lacks that supply under the passive conversion law.** We let coherent material occupy both sides of a test interface and included its reaction to the changing order. This extends the earlier test in which the carrier was fixed. [Earlier wave-force measurement](mouth_wave_attachment.md)

**What powers conversion?** Let $\chi$ fall from ordered brane to disordered bulk. Under $D_t\chi=-M_\chi\mu_{\rm eff}$, the reciprocal force on the carrier is $\mu_{\rm eff}\nabla\chi$. A stationary layer consumes power
\[
P_\chi=\int (\mathbf u\cdot\nabla\chi)^2/M_\chi\,d^4x .
\]
At intake 0.25, three saved meshes gave 0.0128051, 0.0128068 and 0.0128076. The finest costs **0.0512306 per converted constituent**. A number-conservation bound gives at least 0.0124923 power on those fields. The native carrier energy flux is $-\mathrm{Re}(\dot\psi^*\nabla\psi)$. Its fixed spatial phase bias supports current with zero net steady power. A common temporal frequency adds the same energy per constituent at both baths. A stationary wave at fixed action can push while supplying zero continuing work. [Energy current](mouth_stress_energy.md) · [Power, mesh and bound](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_coupled_conversion.json)

**Does the intact brane leak?** A smooth steady single-phase flow with only this reciprocal exchange requires $dB=(\mu_{\rm eff}/n)d\chi$, where $B$ is specific head. On a connected regular order level, $K=\mu_{\rm eff}/n$ is constant and the current toward bulk is $j_\perp=M_\chi n^2 K/|\nabla\chi|$. With positive finite mobility, a draining cap and intact face on that level must pass current of the same sign. At half order, a neutral flat three-area of $4.16R^3$ would carry as much as the saved cap at equal $K$. The saved cap itself gives $K=0.0654$–$0.1106$ across its surface and fails this compatibility test. A joined intact face was absent from that mesh. Physical leakage remains unmeasured. [Surface integral](../tools/mouth_connected_order_current.py)

**What can a powered layer do?** A free planar layer under bulk overpressure passed current toward the brane. In a four-dimensional spherical annulus, surface tension opposes the outward wave. At brane pressure excess 0.0045 both occupied and empty-wave states drained toward the bulk. In a separate annulus at the saved spatial phase difference of +0.450142783 radians, the occupied state required mechanical head 0.00462897 and passed 0.0138196 current per front area. Reservoir power and conversion loss both measured $8.09684332\times10^{-5}$ per inner area. These controls have their own units, a specified shear mirror and a trial cavity energy. A near-boundary solution failed the end-traction check and was excluded. [Planar control](../tools/mouth_free_front_wave_head.py) · [Curved control and four-dimensional momentum balance](../tools/mouth_curved_free_front_head.py)

**Would it settle?** At fixed reservoir pressures, we let flow and order adjust to a weak local holder, then removed it without a mechanical impulse. An outward shift of $0.035723\delta_B$ gave initial outward velocity $1.99963\times10^{-6}$. The opposite shift gave inward motion. All six full-strength, half-strength and wave-off releases initially departed from their reference radius. The occupied wave reduced the departure speed per unit displacement by **2.36%**. This test resolves the initial response on this branch. Long-time stability and the actual hypertrumpet remain open. [Signed releases](../tools/mouth_curved_front_release.py)

**Next physical input.** Determine the energy per constituent available when brane becomes bulk at the physical reservoir conditions. Compare conversion work and current relative to the moving surface at the mouth and on an intact face, first without a wave and then with a measured occupation at the mouth. These inputs test whether a mouth can concentrate and sustain the drainage used in the exterior 0PN force calculation. [Free-fall roadmap](../../../docs/zero_pn_free_fall_roadmap.md)
