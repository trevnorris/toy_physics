# Moving conversion layer at the radius-2 exit

The mean current of the prepared exit admits a reciprocal material response. The much faster flow concentrated at its outer edge prevents applying that planar calibration across the whole disk. A curved front can divert current sideways or emit a stationary wave. Its coupled radiation and force remain open.

**What the calibration measures.** We used the reversible supersolid–superfluid equations with their existing density, phase, shear-order gradient and order inertia. At frequency $\omega=3.06655573696824$ and steady current per area $j=0.28334069168539316$, we solved the diffuse layer at four transverse wavenumbers. The external data are the perturbations of phase and square-root density on each side and the normal displacement of the half-order surface. The outputs are outward mass currents, outward density-gradient tractions and the force required to move the surface. The current crossing relative to the moving surface is $\delta j+i\omega nX$ in the $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention. The intrinsic interface map contains no propagating acoustic loads. The outgoing fields of the two baths enter once, in the subsequent scattering calculation. [Interface operator](../tools/mouth_curved_cap_ports.py)

These are planar powers per area per unit squared interface displacement, using half-window 0.48. The held layer at zero current transmitted 99.997968% of incident normal plane-wave power. At the draining preparation the lossless power-normalized scattering matrix and actuator work balance had absolute errors below $2\times10^{-10}$ in the three propagating tests shown. The finite-current response obeyed the reciprocity relation with reversed background flow. At normal incidence, moving the half-window from 0.32 to 1.2 changed receiving power from 0.000121038 to 0.000121020. The virtual near-field response was more sensitive. At transverse wavenumber 4.33 the phase jump per unit mean current with other mean inputs fixed changed from +0.00767 to −0.22044. The cutoff and the increasing exponential continuation of evanescent traces make this an unsuitable prescription for the arbitrary fields at a finite rim.

**How much layer to include.** A separately solved ordered half and disordered half reproduce the whole-interface response when their central density, phase, flux and density slope match and their actuator forces are added. Relative errors were below $3.4\times10^{-9}$ for the full map and $1.3\times10^{-7}$ for its moving-surface column. The native carrier includes order-gradient entrainment on the upstream side and represents the downstream half by a steady phase delay of $\delta/12$. A solver retaining the upstream order dynamics should replace that delay with the downstream half alone. A homogeneous dynamic carrier on both sides requires the full interface. **Current limit at the rim.** In uniform material of density one, the stationary density amplitude obeys $\delta f''=4(1-j^2)\delta f$ away from the interface. Above $j=1$, both homogeneous density solutions oscillate. Requiring a quiet bath removes both. We integrated the remaining decaying order tail towards the midplane from each side and checked conservation of normal stress. At $j=1.001,1.1,1.6$, the two traces of $f'$ disagreed by −0.013398, −0.016175 and −0.034167, respectively. The first integral agreed within $1.1\times10^{-12}$ on the tightest tail starts. The native quiet-bath preparations at $j=0.28334,0.6,0.8$ did join. This rules out continuing that monotone planar quiet-bath front through the tested supersonic currents. The next physical discriminator is the stationary two-dimensional meridional carrier and order profile through the finite edge with the existing coherent material law and fixed reservoirs. It must allow tangential flow and test the no-incoming-wave condition for any stationary wake. Its linear response could then be computed on that background. The immediate missing input is a curved background solution. Whether an additional edge condition is necessary remains open. The fraction of current in the fast annulus alone supplies no bound on the radiated power or exit force.
