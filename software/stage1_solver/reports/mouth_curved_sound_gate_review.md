# Independent review of the radius-2 sound and exit-force test

The reference is the lowest breathing motion of the held radius-2 mouth with the original torsional wave removed. The stationary profile, reference closing force and inertia must come from this same preparation. A spatially resolved test has to determine the normal current on the terminal disk and the sound from both sides of the curved wall. The earlier 7.20050% and 0.18564% per-cycle costs used two imposed sources in a homogeneous receiver. They provide comparison cases. A physical result can fall outside their interval. The physical loss at the spatially responding rim remains open.** On carrier sound grids 12, 16 and 22 I independently recovered pole losses of 13.27891%, 13.91519% and 14.38919% per cycle. **Physical scope.** The calculation includes both sides of the curved wall and solves for the acoustic terminal current. Its terminal material response is a planar, normally incident law evaluated at the mean current. The volume wave operator varies coherent phase and density around a flowing order-adjusted background, with the interior order held. A physical test would require a spatially varying current-carrying stationary conversion front through the rim and its coupled time-dependent response. The present result supports stopping this breathing-mode relaxation. It leaves the existence of other weak-wave mechanisms and of static circulation support open.
