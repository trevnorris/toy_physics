# Can dense brane material pass a sideways force to an ordered feature?

**A fixed patch of ordered material took a reproducible sideways load. The neighboring material retained its crystal structure, so this test leaves the force on a converting mouth open.** This is step 1 of the 0PN roadmap. The experiment measures the supporting force and crossing momentum in the same four spatial dimensions and on the same clock. The normal direction is across the material surface on the side of the throat. The modeled drain intake would run along that side. [Free-fall roadmap](../../../docs/zero_pn_free_fall_roadmap.md) · [Earlier interface tests](mouth_converted_interface_gate.md)

**What was held.** A periodic sample contained 192 identical constituents interacting through the existing isotropic Morse pair potential. We fixed the 32 sites in one ordered layer and let the other 160 move. We introduced unit constituent inertia, a Langevin thermostat with damping rate 1, and separate bath temperatures of 0.064 and 1.024 in bond-depth units. Density was fixed at 1.65. Six thermal histories began on the same crystal lattice. Following 6 native time units of preparation, each history received zero, opposite and half-strength body forces, and all four combinations of full sideways and normal drive. The measurement lasted 4.5 native time units. Separate controls used equal bath temperatures or half the time step. The model supplies no conversion from this duration to a draining-mouth transit. | 16 | 15.41605 | 14.94837 to 15.88373 |
| 8 | 7.76345 | 7.52389 to 8.00301 |

Each force is half the difference between opposite drives, averaged over the measuring window and then over histories. At full strength the thermostat exerted −0.31847 and mobile sideways momentum increased at a rate of +0.26548. Thus the layer received `16 − 0.31847 − 0.26548 = 15.41605`. Halving the time step gave a layer force of 15.89644 at full drive in three histories. Relative sideways speed next to the layer was too uncertain to infer a force per unit slip at both drive strengths. The applied body force has no gravitational term. [Force readout](../tools/mouth_dense_interface_readout.py)

**Where the material went.** All 99 trajectories recorded zero crossings through the fixed layer in either direction. At the opposite counting plane, the 78 primary runs recorded 2,432 forward and 2,471 reverse crossings, including returns by the same sites. Interparticle stress transmitted force to the fixed layer throughout the experiment. Independent checks reconstructed both counting planes, storage in the whole sample and in one half, and the applied, thermostat and contact impulses. All 396 block balances closed, with maximum absolute momentum residual 4.24 × 10⁻¹¹ or less. An independent pair-potential sum also checked force and energy at eight saved endpoints. [Measurements and local review](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_dense_ordered_interface.json)

**Why this matters for free fall.** In six undriven histories, an average of 0.77366% of the original warm–warm mobile neighbor pairs were absent at the end, versus 0% of cold–cold pairs. Both sites were assigned to their starting side. The mean whole-cell normal stress expressed as pressure was −13.00915, tensile in this convention. The earlier rearranging sample was prepared at zero external pressure. A separate static calculation found that a test constituent at even the most favorable point in the fixed layer has an interaction energy with that layer of about +147 bond depths. One lattice step outside the sampled opening that contribution was −6.18. Fixing every surface site gives a geometric reason for suppressed passage. The full many-body free energy and the permeability of the converting material remain undetermined. **Roadblock and next measurement.** First prepare a dense receiving material at the relevant ambient pressure and establish whether a weak held shear relaxes over the required time. Then hold the mean sideways position of a small patch *behind* the ordered surface, leaving the surface sites free to rearrange. Register the order-loss surface independently, count transfers that reach the other material separately from recrossings, and measure the supporting force and both directions of momentum flux during measured slip. This would test how much load survives actual conversion. The present results do not select the force or acceleration of a free mouth. Working baseline `a91a633f`. Formal repository review pending.
