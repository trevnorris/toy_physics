# Can an energy release power the conversion front?

**Yes, on a patch.** Giving the ordered constituent 0.015 extra energy selected a relative planar current of 0.045 per unit area. The current and pressure followed from one law throughout the layer. Two curved preparations also supported stationary throughput. In both, the half-order surface initially moved farther from its equilibrium after each signed weak displacement. ### Which energy is available?

Add \(\epsilon n H(\chi)\), \(H(\chi)=3\chi^2-2\chi^3\), to the existing carrier and order energy densities. A brane constituent arrives with \(\epsilon\) more internal energy than a bulk constituent at equal density. This is a trial intrinsic energy difference. An independent comparison of the two prepared homogeneous phases' specific enthalpies at equal pressure would calibrate it. The extra homogeneous pressure is exactly zero since \(n\partial_n(\epsilon nH)-\epsilon nH=0\). The density still feels a specific potential \(\epsilon H\), and the order affinity contains \(\epsilon nH'\). Counting a second pressure jump of \(\epsilon n\) would double-count the tilt. Both pure phases remain local minima at the test density. In interface units we used \(n_0=m=\delta_B=a_B=1\), \(\kappa_B=2\), and mobility \(M_\chi=1/2\). The shear order and the coherent carrier occupy the same domain; the trapped transverse mode is empty.

The local exchange law gives \(D_t\chi=-M_\chi\mu_\chi\), and the reciprocal normal force on the coherent flow gives \(\partial_r B=(\mu_\chi/n)\partial_r\chi\) for steady radial flow. Here \(B\) contains enthalpy, kinetic energy, quantum pressure and the new specific potential. The reservoirs prepare pure phases with equal densities and homogeneous specific energies including their local kinetic energies. The spatial phase difference and the current both follow from the solve. These are open, fueled conditions; a persistent measured brane–bulk energy difference would be necessary to use this law in the physical model. ### Planar rate and heat

In the frame of the flat front the constant-density solution has \(\chi'=-\chi(1-\chi)/\delta_B\). The bare order potential and gradient forces balance. The remaining affinity \(\mu_\chi=\epsilon nH'\) then selects \(u_n-V_\Sigma=6M_\chi\epsilon n\delta_B\). The independent boundary-value calculation gives +0.0450000000001, zero within \(3\times10^{-18}\), and −0.0450000000001 current per unit area for positive, zero and negative tilt. Positive and negative tilt each supply 0.000675 power per area, which is lost to order relaxation. At equal density and zero fluid motion, a mobile front at positive tilt would melt back toward the ordered feed. Keeping intact faces quiet still relies on the parked face condition. Translating this planar profile at the selected flow costs no additional steady work and gives no preferred front position.

We set the inner reservoir radii to 100 and 200 interface widths and used a common outer radius of 400. The pure ends fix the material preparation. The half-order radius, current and phase drop are unknowns; no interior holder acts in the base solves. The spherical geometry resolves the radial normal stress and all three equal angular stresses. It has no lip, free flare, or physical calibration between interface widths and a mouth radius.

This is an initial release observable. It does not determine the long-time spectrum.

The four-dimensional controls demonstrate a fueled interface and an initial nonrestoring response on this preparation.
