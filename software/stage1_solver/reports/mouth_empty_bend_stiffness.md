# What holds a draining mouth straight when the standing wave is absent?

**The flow amplifies a sideways bend.** We displaced spherical sections of one flared mouth in four spatial dimensions, first near the entrance and then deeper in the flare. The counting-opening radius was 1, the inward intake was 0.25, and both distant reservoirs stayed in place. We allowed the density and phase to settle after each displacement. Forces and lengths below use model units. [Carrier equations and geometry](mouth_carrier_0pn_methods.md) · [Finite-displacement solver](../tools/mouth_empty_bend_offsets.py)

| Location of bend | Flow force along a +0.02 offset | Flow force along a +0.01 offset |
|---|---:|---:|
| Near entrance | +0.00040388 | +0.00020183 |
| Deeper in flare | +0.00017515 | +0.00008757 |

Reversing the offset reversed the force. Allowing *both* bends to vary gave holding-force stiffness eigenvalues −0.0215783 and −0.0073592 on the finest mesh. Every small combination of these two shapes therefore receives a push from the flow in the direction of the displacement, in the sense of positive work. The wall-pressure integral independently reproduced every stiffness entry within 0.00000113. [Two-shape calculation](../tools/mouth_empty_bend_stiffness.py) · [Opposite-offset figure](figures/mouth_empty_bend_stiffness.png)

Keeping intake fixed requires the reservoirs to adjust their phase difference. Accounting for their work gives the mechanical potential \(E-Q\delta\), where \(Q\) is intake and \(\delta\) is phase difference. For the near bend at step 0.01, stored flow energy alone gave curvature +0.02085. Including reservoir work gave −0.02018, in agreement with the holding force. An independent calculation found that allowing the phase alone to adjust already gives negative curvature in both directions. [Guarded measurements and review scopes](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_empty_bend_stiffness.json)

The layer where shear order disappears also exerts wall stress. On the deep-conversion preparation used in the wave test, its modeled response amplified each individual bend. Its magnitude depends on the assumed interface tension, and the conversion force has yet to be fed back into the draining material consistently. The shallow preparation gave a smaller entrance response on two meshes. Its finest archived state met the original residual criterion but failed a separate Newton-correction check and was excluded. [Order-stress calculation](../tools/mouth_empty_bend_order_load.py) · [Conversion and feedback](mouth_free_order_depth.md)

**Requirement for 0PN.** If uniform wall tension alone opposes the flow, tension must exceed **0.010237878678124177** in model energy per unit wall area for a restoring force in both shapes. Widening the reservoirs gave **0.010238078467321828**. The absolute wall tension is still unknown. The next independent input is the wall’s force under a known strain at the same intake, or a material law fixing that response and the reaction from conversion. This test concerns whether the entrance and flare stay aligned as one body. It leaves their free acceleration open. Working base `49dca23e`. Independent local checks are recorded.
