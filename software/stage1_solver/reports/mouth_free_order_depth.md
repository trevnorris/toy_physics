# Where shear order can end in one flared throat

At 0PN, one held-open throat carrying inward number rate 0.25 admits **two locally stable positions for the loss of shear order** when the previously solved longitudinal flow is held fixed. This is a result for the passive order field in the modeled geometry. It gives a discriminating local shear measurement. Selection during formation and the response of a free mouth remain open.

**Preparation.** Retain the radius-one counting opening, the inherited waist at $w=0.5R$ and both [four-dimensional flares](mouth_order_and_flare.md). Allow ordered feed and disordered bulk to meet anywhere in the connected domain. Use the existing equal-depth order energy, constant mobility $M$, and the trial passive relaxation $D_t\chi=-M\,\delta F/\delta\chi$. Set the wall’s preference for either material state to zero. Free variation gives $\partial_{\rm wall\ normal}\chi=0$, so a regular order level meets the wall orthogonally. The distant order values are fixed. The half-order level was free across the opening. Depth is measured from the feed-side reference plane, with $R$ the counting-opening radius. Since the archived velocity is $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$, the stationary order equation has integrating factor $e^{-\phi/\nu}$ for $\nu=M\kappa=0.4$. Minimizing the resulting weighted order energy from different initial depths gave distinct equilibria with positive order Hessians on the axisymmetric mesh. A separate thin-front calculation balanced advection against curvature, $u_N=\nu H$, and located both depth minima along with two intervening or downstream depth maxima on each scanned profile. The constrained functional measures stability of the passive evolution with this carrier. It does not establish physical formation probabilities.
