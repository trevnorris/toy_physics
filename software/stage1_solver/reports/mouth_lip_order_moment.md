# Wave-off bending of the ordered lip

**A neutral Route A front exerts zero moment when flat. A weak cylindrical bend lowers its relaxed order energy in either direction.** The measured moment therefore supplies no positive crystalline bending stiffness for holding the lip open. We repeated the bend on an ordered strip between two superfluid baths, with thickness fixed at 25 interface widths. Its center stayed ordered (\(\chi=0.99999255\) when flat) and its order energy likewise fell for either sign. This is a measurement of the specified material terms at rest. A physical crystal spanning the lip could transmit additional stress. [Signed measurements and exact calculation](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_lip_order_moment.json)

We held the curved half-order surface at fixed three-area, allowed the profile normal to it to relax, and measured the holding moment per mid-surface three-area, \(m_i=\partial(E/A)/\partial k_i\), for each principal curvature \(k_i\). The two-face strip fixes its mid-surface area and both half-order heights. Positive \(m_i\) denotes external work per positive increment in curvature. There was no excited wave, phase gradient, or density difference. With normal coordinate \(s\), the exact area factor at parallel surfaces is \(P(s)=\prod_i(1+k_i s)\). The order density is \(e=W(\chi)+\kappa_B(\partial_s\chi)^2/2\). We integrated \(e\,\partial P/\partial k_i\) on the relaxed profile and independently differenced energies after re-solving at the two displaced curvatures. The resting carrier and Route A order momentum contribute zero static work in this preparation. The full-strength single-face pair predicted \(m/(a_B\delta_B^2)=-0.00222621\) at the positive half-step, within 0.135% of the reading. The flat energy is even by the symmetric wells and matched baths. Flatness is stationary, and the model supplies no preferred curvature sign. Its cylindrical order energy is locally concave about zero. These data do not select a finite preferred curved profile. Under a cylindrical bend a frozen kink has exactly the same energy per area at each curvature, since \(P=1+ks\) and its energy density is even. Profile relaxation makes the stiffness negative.

For a single registered half-order surface, to quadratic order in the three principal curvatures, the measured law is
\[
\frac{E}{A}=\sigma + \frac{B_\chi}{2}\left(\sum_i k_i\right)^2
  + C_\chi\sum_{i<j}k_i k_j,\qquad
\frac{B_\chi}{\sigma\delta_B^2}=-\frac56,\qquad
\frac{C_\chi}{\sigma\delta_B^2}=\frac{\pi^2}{3}-2 .
\]
Here \(\sigma=a_B\delta_B/3\). In kink units \(\chi_0=(1+\exp s)^{-1}\), \(C_\chi=\int s^2 e_0\,ds\). For a cylindrical bend the first-order profile response on each half satisfies the linearized kink equation with a fixed central order. Writing that response as \(\chi_0'f\) gives \(-[(\chi_0')^2 f']'=(\chi_0')^2\), with \(f(0)=0\). Integrating the relaxed energy gives \(B_\chi/(a_B\delta_B^3)=-4\int_0^{1/2} t(3-2t)^2/[36(1-t)^3]\,dt=-5/18\). The three-moment prediction at \(\delta_B(k_1,k_2,k_3)=(-0.006,0.009,0.009)\) agreed with the full fields to within \(1.4\times10^{-6}a_B\delta_B^2\) in every component. This expansion applies near planar geometry on scales long compared with the interface width. A sharp rim corner needs separate local treatment. [Exact limits](../tools/mouth_lip_moment_limits.py)

**How far does the curvature expansion hold?** Across 12 smooth local preparations with equal hoop curvatures from \(0.02/\delta_B\) to \(0.10/\delta_B\) and meridional curvature of the same magnitude, opposite sign or zero, the largest areal-energy difference from direct normal relaxation was \(0.05297\%\) of \(\sigma\). On the first radius-1 descent state above its declared curvature limit, the narrowest section had \(\delta_B(k_1,k_2,k_3)=(-0.04912,0.12249,0.12249)\). Direct relaxation there lowered areal energy by \(0.06930\%\) of \(\sigma\) relative to the quadratic prediction, with an appreciable difference in local hoop moment (\(-0.02683\) measured, \(-0.02286\) predicted). Across the entire smooth side, replacing the quadratic expression with relaxed normal profiles at both of the last two saved shapes preserved the measured decrease in total energy at each entrance radius. At radius 1 the totals became \(21.26397\to19.74504\), and at radius 2 \(70.59257\to67.89594\). This checks the direction of the last resolved step. The normal solves omit tangential variation of the order field and the attachment to the flat slab and exit.
