# Force on the mid-depth wall during a held first bend

**Fresh material continued to pull back on the displaced wall. The measured force depended strongly on the sideways motion of the arriving material.** We held the entrance fixed at intake 0.25 and shifted the deep end by ±0.000005 mouth radii. In a 32-section patch with centered incoming material, the force on the wall for the positive step began at −0.0579234 and averaged −0.0266647 over the final eight arrivals. With the same prepared patch and fresh sites entering at the local wall displacement, the late force was −0.000616256. Forces below are native patch force units. **Where the force was measured.** The archived first-bend shape goes from zero at depth 0.55 to the full displacement at 1.15. The twelve-section gauge, centered at 1.175, spans depths 1.13435–1.21577. Even at its upstream edge the wall moves by 99.983% of the deep-end displacement. We applied the actual profile to the wall ends of the finite-displacement Morse contacts, let the material sites move freely sideways, and summed the incremental force they exerted on those ends relative to a matched undisplaced copy. This is one local tangential component, with the previous patch's wall coverage, prescribed throughflow and frozen mid-depth material conditions. In the 32-section patch the gauge contained entirely new material by the two-arrival window ending at arrival 24. The quoted late values span arrivals 89–96 for 32 sections and 137–144 for 48 sections. Increasing the patch length moved both boundaries. **How much comes from bending?** Uniformly translating the wall with the same centered feed produced −0.02654776. It accounted for 99.56% of the late centered-feed reaction. Subtracting it left −0.000116915 for the bend. With wall-following feed the matched subtraction gave −0.000615887. The independent complementary displacement reproduced the centered-feed difference with reversed sign to within 1.66 × 10⁻¹⁰. Halving the time step gave −0.02666628 for the centered-feed bend.

**Checks and 0PN implication.** The initial trial moved the entrance and was retained as a complementary-shape check. At the archived fixed-entrance shape, the larger 0.00001-radius drive exceeded the preset absolute tolerance on full- versus half-displacement response. All ten runs at the smaller drive passed that tolerance, the energy-derivative check and the resource guard. To predict which local force a physical mouth receives, track the sideways motion of material from the fixed entrance into this gauge with the compression and shear order varying along its path. Free acceleration still requires the force integrated over the mouth and the response of its freely moving center.
