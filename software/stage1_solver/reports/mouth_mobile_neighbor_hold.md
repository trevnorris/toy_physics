# How mobile brane neighbors change the force on a held first bend at 0PN

**A passing patch can stay internally stiff while giving up its grip on the bend.** With the incoming sites one pitch apart at the waist, the bend retained about **+21%** of its early signed force. With the same pitch specified at mid-depth, the measured force reversed to about **−6%**. The force is the sideways support on the waist-to-middle band of a held, radius-one mouth at intake 0.25. [Guarded measurements and independent review](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_mobile_neighbor_hold.json)

**The independent material measurement.** We let the 12 sites of the arriving brane layer settle under their original same-layer Morse forces. Small opposite displacements measured the response of the whole patch, including how different sites move one another. In a direct hold of each of the nine tagged sites, the other eleven could translate, rotate and rearrange. The initial holding force per displacement spanned 149.02–300.11 in patch units, fell to 36.27–52.18 by time 0.0625, and was numerically indistinguishable from zero by one transit. A separate balanced load held a tag displaced *relative to the patch center*. Here the nine tags retained **64.27–66.49%** of their instantaneous radial stiffness, or 117.11–229.71 force units per unit relative displacement. The two tests distinguish internal elasticity from the ability to hold position against the wall. An independent nonlinear drive on several sites at once reproduced the calibrated displacement response to within one part per million of peak. [Material measurement](../tools/mouth_mobile_neighbor_probe.py) · [Balanced internal load](../tools/mouth_mobile_neighbor_internal.py)

**The bend prediction.** We applied the measured response to the nine wall contacts as fresh patches carried them through the same held bend. The early force was read at 0.0625 and the full-transit force at 28.43103. Values below use the reference spatial discretization. Force from the integrated wall support agrees with the independent stress reading at the middle cut. Both read the signed sideways component between depths 0.5 and 1.175. [Segment calculation](../tools/mouth_mobile_neighbor_segment.py)

| Preparation of the arriving material | One pitch at waist | One pitch at middle |
|---|---:|---:|
| Free patches, neutral-patch contact coverage | +21.45% | −6.36% |
| Free patches, coverage adjusted to match the earlier instantaneous grip | +11.61% | −4.95% |
| Free patches, earlier scalar same-layer force law and same neutral coverage | +22.94% | −6.45% |
| Patch frame held, sites free to rearrange, neutral coverage | — | +66.48% |

Reversing and halving the weak bend while halving the time step preserved both ratios. Refining the exterior spatial discretization then gave +21.4493% and −6.3610% at transit, changes below 0.006 percentage point. At the late reading, fresh material occupied the full measured band.

The earlier renewal laws predicted +45.93% and +81.32% at their last sample, time 28.3125. The free-patch calculations at that same instant gave +21.45% and −6.37%. Changing the pitch station changes both the arrival rate and the number of wall contacts sharing each patch. Holding the patch frame removes both its translation and its rotation. The remaining force in that control shows how strongly the prediction depends on how the wider brane supports an incoming patch. **What remains for free fall.** The first-bend hold is a local wall-force test. The model assumes cold, overdamped sites with unit drag, one shared patch for nine staggered contacts, and no microscopic bonds between successive patches. For each wall traction component it applies the same force in two planar directions and feeds their average displacement back into the wall. A larger continuous brane can resist collective sideways motion even while every neighbor is free to move. The next independent input is that resistance and the actual site arrival rate. Measure the drift of a tagged neighborhood relative to more distant material during passage through the bend, together with its arrival spacing at the waist and middle. These measurements would fix inputs for an independent test of how much companion-induced load reaches the mouth. The center acceleration remains open.
