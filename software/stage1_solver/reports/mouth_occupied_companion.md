# Does a wave-supported draining mouth move as one body at 0PN?

**The entrance accelerates toward a weak companion stream in the supported preparations. Its acceleration remains sensitive to how the load reaches the flare.** We kept the radius-one cubic geometry, intake 0.25 and axial profile fixed. The ground standing wave occupies the same trial confining well in each run. Its stored action $J=E/\omega$ sets both its sideways restoring force and its added inertia. The entrance, first bend and deeper bend respond together in the calculation. Only the feed is driven. The imposed harmonic stream represents the leading, locally uniform feeding flow of a distant drain. The accelerations are the in-phase components at angular frequency 0.005 in model units on carrier mesh 34. The rigid control locks both bends and retains the same wave and flow. Mismatch is the amplitude of the difference in the entrance and flare displacements divided by the entrance amplitude, including their relative phase. The entrance accelerations were 14.57%, 6.71% and 24.23% below their respective rigid controls. Halving the drive frequency reduced the mismatches to 2.42%, 1.03% and 6.04%. The acceleration deficits there were 14.48%, 6.70% and 21.83%. These are finite-bath harmonic measurements. The zero-frequency limit remains open.

**How much support the wave supplies.** With $J=1$ and zero independent wall tension, an unforced sideways bend grows at rate 0.14881666 in the cycle-averaged model. At that action the marginal independent area tension for positive static bend stiffness is 0.00630135. Doubling the action with zero independent tension still leaves a negative bend stiffness. Both modeled bend stiffnesses become positive above $J=2.71179$ for this preparation. At $J=4$ the wave supplies 9.87241 units of common-translation inertia, compared with 2.46810 at $J=1$. The $J=4$ result is a conditional example of the wave alone carrying a sideways pull. For a source of intake 0.25 at 64 radii, the leading slab flow sets a drive amplitude of $4.8570\times10^{-6}$. At frequency 0.005 it gives an in-phase entrance acceleration of $4.2152\times10^{-8}$ in the $J=4$ example. This uses the leading feeding-side carrier flow to set the amplitude of an imposed oscillation. A stationary source alone does not set its frequency. **Opening area.** The moving sections retain their shape by construction. Independently, a weak uniform stream drives a dipole about this spherical ground state. Its angular average vanishes, so the first-order change of either the spherical surface area or the enclosed three-dimensional opening measure is zero for a regular response at fixed intake and action. The square of the dipole has a nonzero mean. A finite area reading needs the mean second-order pressure and the breathing response of this same occupied state. A companion-induced change in the mean reservoir head is a separate drive.

**Decision.** The rigid calculation remains a comparison with a quantified error at these frequencies. Selecting a physical preparation now requires an independent wave occupation and wall tension consistent with the same radius, depth and intake. A slow-load check should use that preparation before extending across radii. The angular and two-mesh field checks and independent review scopes are recorded below. Working base `1dc45d09`.
