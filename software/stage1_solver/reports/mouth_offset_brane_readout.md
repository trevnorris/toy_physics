# Can the brane supply the force holding one attachment off center?

**The 0PN fields give a measurable background stress response. They leave the actual offset and holding force of a particular attachment undetermined.** In the held radius-one mouth at intake 0.25, the archived carrier and order fields let us follow flow markers. Their spacing at fixed angular separation grew by 0.225% on the finer mesh between 95% and 30% wall order. There are no paired site identities, individual site positions, or forces conjugate to their relative alignment in those fields. The earlier off-center trajectories used a trial binding force, drag and support. [Previous matched-force test](mouth_matched_attachment.md) · [Material inputs left open](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/O2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md#32-open-material-and-constitutive-inputs)

**A control using the brane equations alone.** We prepared two perpendicular components of bulk shear inside the guide where the local shear order was one half. The curl-energy density on the uniform part of the imposed displacement was \(f=\mu_0\chi(g^2+h^2)/2\). The existing order equation senses \(\partial f/\partial\chi\). We held the first strain at \(g=10^{-3}\) and compared \(h=0,+10^{-5},-10^{-5}\) on copies of the same state. These are dimensionless strains. No wall spring or microscopic binding force enters this control. [Shear energy](../../../docs/moving_interface_conversion_work_proposal.md#3-what-the-existing-shear-gate-can-and-cannot-supply) · [Calculation](../tools/mouth_offset_brane_readout.py)

The original-direction shear traction \(\tau_g=\mu_0\chi g\) was unchanged at the instant of either step. Allowing conversion to respond gave an initial difference in its rate, relative to the zero-step copy, of \(-M\mu_0^2gh^2/2\). At the preparation’s \(M=5,\ \mu_0=1\), both meshes gave **−2.50 × 10⁻¹³ model stress per model time** to three significant figures. Halving the step quartered the result. Reversing the preload reversed its sign. A delayed stress reading can therefore contain a contribution from the surrounding brane even in the absence of any contact.

**Why a single-contact answer remains open.** An exact calculation allowed a smooth change in contact energy on the interior of a live patch. It left both one-direction force curves, the unperturbed offset force, and the uniformly averaged transverse stiffness identical. The change in holding force common to the two opposite steps could still have either sign. The extra term tests uniqueness and was kept out of the brane control. [Exact check, numerical results and review scopes](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_offset_brane_readout.json)

**Required measurement.** Independently locate both sites and verify they remain connected. Hold their measured relative offset fixed with a calibrated force probe. At matched order, history and elapsed time take three readings of its reaction in the original direction. The even change is \([H(+h)+H(-h)]/2-H(0)\), and the half-difference measures the odd part. Measure the surrounding material’s reaction under matched loading so that a change in total load can be attributed to the tagged pair. Until there is a resolved material interaction or such a measurement, this channel supplies no independent force for 0PN free fall.
