# Can shear during conversion pull a draining mouth into free fall?

A shear contact that breaks and reforms as material enters the bulk transmits some of the companion-directed momentum to a held mouth. Doubling separation from 8 to 16 mouth radii reduced the calculated pull to 0.24921 and 0.24920 of its original value for the two conversion depths. The companion supplies the incident field in this test. The test establishes how the trial contact transmits its finite-range falloff. **What moves with the mouth.** In resting brane, tow the same mouth at speed \(V\), then impose a slow acceleration \(a\). Write the required actuator force from this channel as \(G V+M_{\rm app}a\). For a freely translating, inertia-free surrounding support, the deeper preparation gives \(G=0.0708542\) and \(M_{\rm app}=+0.00194349\). Clamping that support gives \(M_{\rm app}=-0.00762367\) with the same mass passing through the layer. A negative coefficient means force left over from the preceding motion helps the imposed acceleration. The balance includes momentum still inside the layer and momentum crossing its end.
