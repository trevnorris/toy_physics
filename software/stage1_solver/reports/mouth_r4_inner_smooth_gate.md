**The pointwise smooth gauge removes the energy jump.** We used the descending cubic $s(c)=(1-c)^2(1+2c)$ on $[0,1]$, continued as constants outside. Each Gauss point uses the interpolated physical order. Both gauge strengths gave positive total work for a +10⁻⁹ order step at all **52,955** free zero-order nodes, including **16,394** bordering positive order. The largest gauge-work magnitude was 2.46761 × 10⁻⁸ of the positive physical work. [All-node energy test](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_r4_smooth_order_boundary.json)

Independent review concluded, “Scoped clear for the repaired fixed-field inner solve, the frozen label, order and six-shape derivatives, the inversion controls, and the positive one-sided energy readings. The required nodewise quadratic gauge-work scaling fails at 677 front nodes. The paired held-shape solve remains gated.”

**Decision.** Radius 4 stays held. We recommend accepting the measured linear term subject to checking the sign at every zero-order node again at both stationary endpoints. Requiring quadratic scaling at every zero node would instead call for testing interpolation of nodal weights. Paired stationary forces and free release remain open.
