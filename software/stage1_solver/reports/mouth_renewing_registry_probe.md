# Does flowing material acquire a reproducible sideways grip at the flare?

**The microscopic calculation predicts a local force once the incoming positions and velocities are specified. It leaves the physical wall grip open.** On a hypothetical triangular wall made of the calibrated brane constituents, passing material can carry off sideways momentum. For a uniformly populated lattice cell the mean response to a constant wall offset cancels exactly. Material arriving along a common row gives a finite response. This distinction survives free sideways motion of each incoming site. [Previous wall-only test](mouth_replacing_flare_slip.md)

**Experiment.** At radius 1, intake 0.25 and on the same deep conversion branch, we followed a representative wall-facing site from depth 1.55 to 1.65. The carrier took 2.00247 model time units to cross 10.3965 microscopic pitches. We retained the actual axial speed and shear order along this short patch, represented its tangent plane as flat, and let the site accelerate transversely under the central Morse forces at fixed normal gap. Axial motion was prescribed. The assumed facing layer carries half the represented resting mass and the static site potential comes from the neutral compression calibration. The measured external holding traction is the transverse momentum gain of outgoing sites multiplied by the incoming site flux. Opposite and half-strength offsets and inlet speeds checked linearity. No local transverse damping was prescribed. [Trajectory calculation](../tools/mouth_renewing_registry_probe.py)

| Incoming positions within a wall cell | Local holding-traction slope per unit wall offset |
|---|---:|
| Uniform, with thermal transverse speeds | 0 exactly |
| Equilibrated at the weak inlet order | +6.41 × 10⁻⁸ |
| Inheriting a distribution prepared at full order | +7.56 × 10⁻⁵ |

All three rows have the same transverse Maxwell speed distribution at the neutral test temperature, the same downstream order-weighted wall interaction, and a supply held fixed in the laboratory when the wall is offset. The inherited row is a prescribed preparation. For uniform supply, translating the wall shifts the origin of a periodic integral and leaves its mean unchanged. Even then the response to a change of incoming transverse speed can be finite. These are traction slopes on a local patch in model units. Breaking the translational symmetry along a bent wall, normal forces and sheet elasticity can contribute to the whole-mouth load. [Thermal preparation and momentum export](../tools/mouth_registry_thermal_supply.py)

**Does throughflow select the missing registration?** For cold sites injected along a common lattice row at depth 1.55, weakening corrugation with local order gave an offset slope of +0.00052939. In a separate test we started a nearly aligned stream farther upstream at depth 1.175 and carried its small deviations through conversion without a reset. At 1.55 an initial position error at fixed incoming speed had grown by an RMS factor of **6.93** across initial positions along the row. The factor was 6.87 on the coarser carrier mesh. The exact centered trajectory exists, but neighboring arrivals did not focus into it in this test. Reversible position–velocity exchange, phase mixing and momentum export can change the measured impulse. No physical coefficient of dissipative drag was extracted. An independent check found that small angular disturbances of the passive order field on this same branch decay with the carrier held fixed. The occupied scalar ground mode also supplies no angular locations for microscopic wall sites. [Upstream transfer](../tools/mouth_registry_upstream_memory.py)

**Roadmap decision.** Step 2 still needs an independent observation or formation law giving the wall’s microscopic pattern and the distribution of incoming positions *relative to that pattern*, together with lateral velocities and site flux. A discriminating measurement would tag the incoming brane and a persistent feature of the held mouth simultaneously before conversion, then read their relative positions and velocities again at depth 1.55. This tests whether the inlet reference follows a displaced wall, stays tied to the feeding brane, or loses registration. The held-flow and push calibration, physical pair release and universality tests remain pending.
