# Does pinning the material give a mesh-independent radius-four exit force?

**The four-cell and eight-cell motions still give different reactions after the fields settle.** Local refinement reduces the receiving-side gap by 82.0% and the fixed-wall duct signal by 72.1%. The test used one circulation quantum, an ordered interior and the same physical terminal strip on both meshes. Each axial reaction is minus the derivative of draining-minus-resting energy. All 24 signed field pairs passed the archive and stationarity checks. The maximum preconditioned field residual was 4.99 × 10⁻⁹, and the maximum mismatch between reservoir currents was 4.37 × 10⁻¹³. **Receiving face.** For axial steps of −0.000010 and +0.000010, the fine-grid four-cell reactions were +0.02544956 and +0.02573650. The eight-cell reactions were +0.02547114 and +0.02575806. The prescribed order has different one-sided slopes. At that step size the centered correction for sampling the order at moved nodes, relative to carrying the old nodal values with the mesh, was +0.00197608 for four cells and +0.00221710 for eight. The crystal correction on this face was zero. These are energy differences on the same settled fields. The physically stationary supports do zero displacement work.

**Bonded duct.** The crystal and wall share material points. Displacing the sloping wall while fixing those same points would violate the bond. Subtracting the two mesh motions keeps the entire wall stationary, so the measured duct signal is purely sensitivity to the interior discretization. An independent work calculation on the tightly settled fine-grid zero state gives +0.00017990 from sampling the order field at the fixed crystal sites, −0.00000435 from the carrier and approximately +0.00000028 from the imposed order. Their sum, +0.00017583, agrees with the signed result to 3.0 × 10⁻⁸. The analogous difference on the doubled grid is 1.05 × 10⁻⁷. This comparison also accounts for most of the small offset from the earlier, less tightly settled duct predictions. The previously tested alternative that evaluates the receiving order directly at integration points remains a fixed-state calculation. [Pin definitions and earlier predictions](mouth_terminal_physical_pins.md) · [Independent signed review](mouth_rim_settled_pin_review.md)

**Decision.** Keep radius four held. The terminal motion vanishes at the common disk rim, where the whole-rim force measurement has unit weight. The existing near/far axial residuals of −0.02304 / −0.02345 on the doubled grid and −0.04043 / −0.04078 on the rim-refined grid therefore still stand. The next numerical target is the transfer between fixed material sites and the changing carrier mesh. First require a vanishing energy derivative when only the interior mesh moves. An absolute bonded-wall force also requires a common physical actuator motion for the crystal and the wall. Radius three remains blind.
