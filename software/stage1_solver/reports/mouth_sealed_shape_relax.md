# Does the sealed throat select a depth and flare at two entrance radii?

**At both training radii the sealed duct shortened and developed a constriction inside the entrance. Neither trajectory reached a stationary profile in the range of the measured weak-curvature law.** The wave stayed localized over that part of the test. An extrapolation drove the constriction down to about two order-interface widths. Determining whether an ordered channel survives there requires the finite wall and conversion front to respond together. [Guarded shape, field and force measurements](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/mouth_sealed_shape_relax.json)

We fixed the entrance at radius 1 or 2. The intact slab and duct sides had zero normal carrier flow. Coherent receiving material supplied exterior backing. The terminal melting disk alone transmitted current at fixed reservoir phase difference 0.4510084283. Slab thickness 1, interface width 0.04, surface tension 1 and ordered wave speed 1 were fixed in common reference units. For both mouths we prepared action 1 in the same transverse degree-four mode, with the action unit defined using the radius-1 reference. Uniform reservoir pressure added no separate volume work. The registered side and planar terminal disk both paid the calibrated interfacial energy per area. The sharp joins at the entrance and exit were assigned zero additional junction energy. [Carrier boundary and force controls](mouth_sealed_side_comparison.md) · [Independent wave-off calibration](mouth_lip_order_moment.md)

Starting at the constant-mean-curvature profile, each energy descent freed depth, exit radius and two interior widths. Both end slopes could change. At each signed step we solved the carrier density and phase and the outgoing transverse wave on the new shape. We minimized their energy plus surface and weak-curvature energy. This is a quasistatic energy test with a material-rest-frame transverse law. Its iterations do not measure elapsed physical time. [Relaxation solver](../tools/mouth_sealed_shape_relax.py)

The screen used maximum absolute principal curvature times interface width of 0.10. A direct normal-profile control still gives falling energies across that convention. Independent discretizations confirmed the sign and magnitude of the axial force. Removing the disk-area term from the exit derivative still gives positive slopes of +2.16255 and +38.96080 at the last screened profiles. The separate field energies, the unused shape slope and the half-size step controls identify residual forces well before the extrapolation. The tabulated losses come from outgoing flux at frozen geometries. Extremely narrow linewidths deeper into the extrapolation are numerically unresolved.

**Beyond weak curvature.** Following the same formal energy brought the interior radii to 0.08706 and 0.06880. For comparison a flat interface of this material requires 0.08789 to go from half order to 90% order. A protected narrow channel, pinch-off and another equilibrium remain possible outside this model’s resolved range. At radius 2, resetting to the lowest closed-cavity mode switched modes near the constriction. Continuation from the previously occupied field preserved overlap. A finer free-step trajectory rejected a proposed state for failure of the carrier current balance. On the accepted coarse geometries, separate finer-mesh transfer preserved the mode and gave final energy 56.43031 against the coarse 56.43989. The narrowest currents remain mesh-sensitive. These are two paths from one shape family at one specified occupation, with conditional surface registration and junction mechanics. They supply no equilibrium intake, translational mass or free-fall ratio. A direct next discriminator is whether a connected ordered core and a restoring wall force persist as opposite conversion layers approach in the sealed duct.
