# Does the held exit force depend on how the carrier mesh moves?

**Radius four stays held.** Moving the same bonded disk, rim and adjoining lip gave a reproducible axial load near +0.50. Two ways of extending that displacement into the carrier gave measurably different forces on each grid. The refined difference was +0.00030359. The prediction committed at `4576e6a4`, before the signed runs finished, was +0.00030268. This locates the remaining discrepancy in the discrete carrier and conversion-order work. The ordered mouth had one circulation quantum. Each force is minus the axial derivative of the draining-minus-resting energy on the same held geometry. All 24 signed pairs (48 fields) passed the archive, stationarity and current checks. The largest signed field residual was 4.99 × 10⁻⁹. The largest difference between reservoir currents was 4.07 × 10⁻¹³.

**What carries the difference.** At half step the refined four-minus-eight difference was positive for both signs of motion (+0.00026907 for the positive shift, +0.00033810 for the negative shift). Moving only the carrier mesh now does zero direct work on fixed crystal sites, yet the total stationary-wall residual increased slightly on both grids. In the moving test the reference crystal itself strained. At the positive and negative half steps its energy changed by −0.00132652 and +0.00132773 for both extensions, canceling in the draining-minus-resting reaction. The refined swept-layer contributions to this reaction were +0.01216181 and +0.00850175 for four and eight cells. Other carrier terms offset most of their difference. The precomputed net regional contributions include +0.00018298 upstream inside the duct and +0.00009030 downstream outside the disk radius. The signed solver uses a first-order swept-layer correction, independently checked by finite-strip quadrature on the original fields. **Comparison with exterior momentum.** At the solve's quadrature the near surface gave +0.50016177 and +0.49895832. The far surface gave +0.45412352 and +0.45303011. Including the work between the two displacement profiles (+0.04603826 and +0.04592822) reproduces the near value within 7 × 10⁻⁹ on each grid. At half step the absolute four- and eight-cell reactions changed by −0.00177567 and −0.00127497 under refinement. The older −0.01739082 drift used a wall displacement that halved in physical width. Keeping its width fixed gives +0.00292530 for that separate observable. [Independent enclosure and width audit](mouth_shared_rim_surface_review.md)

**Decision.** The mismatch is small on the scale of the held load (0.061% on the refined grid) and large on the scale resolved by halving the step. These results establish neither a sharp-corner instability nor a free-mouth equilibrium. The next fixed-geometry discriminator is to keep the carrier's physical approximation space common to both extensions and integrate that same physical field independently on the two maps, splitting cells where their boundaries cross. Test its zero-wall-motion work and then its signed bonded-rim reaction before changing the lip or relaxing the whole mouth. Radius three remains blind.
