# Can static circulation hold a draining exit open?

**One imposed winding quantum reverses the net exit force on both held mouths.** It also strongly depletes their carrier density and reduces their intake. The measured normal crossing lies outside the conversion-front calibration. The radius-1 crystal still lowers its energy by reordering at the previously melted site. These results stop a free-exit relaxation.

Positive force opens the exit. We kept the same wave-empty held shapes, crystal registration and meridional reservoir phase bias. The measured exit radii are 0.449778 and 1.036974. A straight vortex through the coherent feed and receiver has phase \(N\phi+\theta\), circulation \(\kappa=2\pi N\) in solver units, and azimuthal speed \(N/s\), where \(s\) is distance from the rotation axis. The core extends as a surface into the fourth spatial dimension. The meridional flow and carrier density respond throughout. At the disk the transmitted axial angular momentum per time is \(N D\) for mass intake \(D\). The calculation prescribes an already prepared vortex. **Force and threshold.** At fixed phase bias we measured \(F_{\rm net}(N)=-L_0-\partial_b(E_N-E_0)\), with \(L_0\) the matched closing load, \(b\) the exit radius and \(E_N\) the two-sided carrier energy relative to the same straight vortex in the undivided domain. One quantum adds +0.520650 and +0.770849. With axial drive removed the vortex alone contributes +0.269298 and +0.425639. The remainder comes from how winding changes the drain load at fixed bias. An independent integration of the original field energy reproduces the force reversal. A larger domain gives net forces +0.173676 and +0.377170. One quantum is the smallest allowed circulation that overcomes the load; none of the tested integers balances either held shape exactly. Doubling the winding reduces its support. **Speed, order and strain.** Near the equatorial rim at one quantum, total speed divided by local long-wave sound speed was 2.42658 / 0.00342423 at radius 1 and 1.22859 / 0.0834244 at radius 2. The smallest ratio of normal speed to sound speed across the sampled disk was 278.87 at radius 1 and 5.1201 at radius 2. The quiet planar conversion law has no calibration for this depleted, fast normal flow. The registered collars kept positive volume Jacobians at both held shapes. Their incremental strain after release and their damage threshold remain unknown. At the melted radius-1 site a small increase of crystalline order still lowers the energy; raising order over a wider patch costs energy. For 0PN this supplies a candidate outward support and a measured cost in intake. A nearby equilibrium remains possible. This steady calculation leaves stability against vortex motion or shedding open. Radius 3 stays reserved.
