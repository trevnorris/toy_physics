**Scope.** The external carrier solve recomputes the density and phase to both distant reservoirs for each material state. The finite layer then takes its port fields from that solve. The incoming and upper/lateral receiving fluxes close in the finite patch. The side rim retains its registered material values, and the receiving order continues to the finite outer boundary. The patch does not send its modified field traces or stresses back into the exterior or relax the collar internally. The measured intake and annular fraction therefore describe conditional transmission at each recorded shape. Equilibrium of the complete free mouth and persistence of the crystalline order in the interior require separate shape, stress and order tests.
