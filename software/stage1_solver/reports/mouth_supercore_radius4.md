# What happens to a radius-4 mouth threaded by one circulation quantum?

**At radius 4 a dense annulus carries substantial inward flow while rotation and drainage coexist. The initially melted pocket recrystallizes during joint relaxation.** The calculation establishes a transmission mechanism for this preparation and a limitation of using circulation alone to maintain a melted core.

**Bare length scale.** In the neutral unit-density bath, the coherent amplitude f at distance s from the vortex sheet solves

\[
 f'' + f'/s-f/s^2=(f^8-1)f/2,\qquad f(0)=0,\quad f(\infty)=1.
\]

The carrier density n=f² reaches half its far value at **1.5479513**. The azimuthal velocity is 1/s, and the local long-wave sound speed is n². They meet at **2.0499349**, where density is 0.69844. Recovery is gradual: density reaches 90% at 3.02652 and 99% at 7.20956. Independent finite-volume differences reproduce both characteristic radii. With no axial drive, 63.32% of a radius-4 disk's three-dimensional measure is dense and subsonic. That percentage measures space available before a drain or a collar changes the density and velocity. **Held preparation and response.** We fixed the entrance at 4 and prepared a one-slab-thickness puncture with exit 4, a nearly cylindrical waist and a melted central pocket. The same coherent vortex continues into both baths at the earlier phase bias. We used the existing elastic law, a flat-bore collar with unit initial radial stretch at the rim, and a bonded core that is unloaded in the shallow starting shape. The material law and reservoir conditions were held fixed. Depth, exit, interior widths and spatial order were allowed to change.

**Where the geometry balanced.** Four shape coordinates on the finer mesh gave depth 0.81717 and exit 3.57543. An independent deformation of the middle of that meridian exposed a residual force of −53.19 in solver units. We released that fifth shape coordinate with the others. The resulting depth was **0.81583** and both the minimum radius and exit were **3.57310**. The largest generalized force on the five-coordinate mesh was 0.000066, without active search limits. On an independent finer carrier mesh the axial, exit and added waist forces were −0.00299, −0.000175 and +0.01457. This is a numerical balance in the tested shape family with the crystal registration held. [Shape calculations](mouth_supercore_shape.md) · [Independent review](mouth_supercore_review.md)

**What happened to the material.** In the initially melted sites (order below 0.01), the mean order rose to **0.99909** and the minimum to **0.99736**. Winding was active from the first order update on the finer mesh. An independent contraction of the prepared melted pocket lowered the total energy by 0.43081 while raising the carrier energy by only 0.00000569. In this axisymmetric material law, swirl is tangent to the order contours. Its energy depends directly on carrier density; the much weaker indirect response through density and meridional flow did not preserve the melted pocket. The density-depleted vortex and the melted crystal are physically distinct. [Initial and final crystalline order](figures/mouth_supercore_section.png)

The two collars remained orientation preserving, with principal stretches **0.61557–1.30186**. Within the constitutive law a small redistribution of material inside the ordered collar can still release energy while the entire outer shape remains fixed. A signed test gave an energy slope −62.6973 per unit perturbation amplitude, compared with −62.6972 from direct differentiation. This measures the reaction holding those internal material labels in place. A material damage criterion is uncalibrated. [Signed collar-work calculation](../tools/mouth_supercore_collar_work.py)

**What drained through the resolved layer.** The final mouth carried **12.76385** inward on the finer grid, with **9.80829 (76.84%)** passing through material above half bath density and below sound speed including swirl. Its lowest total Mach number was 0.35582. On matched grids the wound mouth retained 85.31% of the intake of the same mouth without winding. Moving the matching cuts outward gave 12.88924 and 77.16%. The annulus transmitted +0.91401 of the +1.13871 flow-induced axial stress integrated across the disk. Mass closed to within 8.3 × 10⁻¹⁰. The resolved layer took its boundary fields from the two-sided exterior solution; a full feedback solve and dynamical release remain open. **0PN decision.** The neutral sonic scale explains why this preparation has room for a draining annulus. It does not determine a minimum self-supporting mouth radius. The immediate physical question is what would keep a vortex core *crystallinely disordered* while the surrounding material relaxes. One clean test of any proposed interaction would be whether an isolated vortex favors a disordered pocket in neutral material before using it in a throat. The collar also needs an unloaded registration or a restoring law that balances internal as well as shape forces. The blind radius-3 prediction and free release remain gated.
