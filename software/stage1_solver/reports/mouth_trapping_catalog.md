# Torsional spectrum on the two reference mouths

Each row is one distinct outgoing pole on the saved, connected, fine material background (order mesh 72 and wave mesh 20). We searched angular degrees 1–6 from the three lowest positive clamped and free cavity starts and the archived degree-four radial profile. Multiple starts can reach the same pole. The depth-uniform channel has zero cutoff for each degree. Entries below the first thickness threshold still have that escape channel. The angularly continued higher-degree branch is recorded separately.

The last two columns give the wave-energy slope at unit action per unit increase in exit radius, and how a 1% log-radius narrowing changes the number of propagating depth channels. A negative slope supplies outward force. Collar is the percentage of total kinetic energy on registered collar material. Waist and exit are percentages of cavity kinetic energy in a band within one eighth of the total depth of the narrowest section and in the final quarter of the depth, respectively. These regions can overlap. A dash denotes an unresolved signed continuation. Frequencies and decay rates are in inverse model time.
