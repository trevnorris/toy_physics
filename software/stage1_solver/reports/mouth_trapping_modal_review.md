# Independent check of torsional trapping and radiation

**Verdict.** “Scoped clear for the sampled outgoing resonances, the angular and axial radiation channels, and numerical trapping on the disconnected coarse-grid island. The high-Q connected pole leaks through two channels. Total internal reflection, a continuum bound mode and a wave-supported terminal radius remain unresolved.”

**Axis and outgoing boundary.** For the radial unknown $u=U/r$, regular flat solutions behave as $r^{\ell-1}$. The degree-one amplitude may be finite on the axis. Higher degrees vanish there. We evaluated the outgoing Cauchy traction $k h_\ell^{(1)\prime}(ka)/h_\ell^{(1)}(ka)-1/a$ independently at three recorded poles, using the outgoing or decaying radial wavenumber for each axial channel. The scan's curl traction differs by the real term $2/a$, matched by the feed stiffness boundary term. All three boundary operators agreed to relative error below $10^{-15}$ after the correction. The physical stored symmetric-strain energy uses the corrected feed stiffness. This changes the radiated power per unit energy of the broad radius-2, degree-one resonance from 0.58635 to 0.63319. It leaves its pole and linewidth unchanged. **Long-lived connected wave.** On the fine radius-1 mesh a fixed-frequency reconstruction gave cavity kinetic fraction 0.99368858 at frequency 6.27306936. The pole has $Q=6361.61$. The first two clamped starting vectors recover this same resonance. Their labels identify search starts. The corresponding coarse result gives $Q=4317.57$, so the lifetime remains mesh sensitive. The two open depth channels carry 0.000643581 and 0.000342508 per unit physical stored energy. At the same frequency, the uniform exterior already suppresses degree-six radiation. At a unit-radius matching surface its radiation conductance divided by radial wavenumber is 0.38599 and 0.22502 in those channels, compared with 0.97522 and 0.96717 at degree one. These numbers use equal boundary amplitudes. They do not give the coupling of a specific interior wave to the boundary. A claim of total internal reflection needs an incidence or transmission test that separates the effect of the speed change from the angular barrier and the mode amplitude at the mouth. [Spherical-wave Wronskian](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.50)

**Detached wave.** On the coarse radius-2 mesh the six occupied nodes lie at depths 0.70513–0.73077 of the mouth length and radii 0.71429–0.78571 of the local wall radius. The wave has zero port amplitude. Polarizing the stiffness and mass derivatives against the saved mode yielded $d\omega/db_{\rm exit}=+0.00009121014$, consistent with the signed eigensolver result +0.00009120968. At fixed positive action its local energy slope favors narrowing. The existence of these six nodes supporting a detached mode has yet to be reproduced on the fine mesh.
