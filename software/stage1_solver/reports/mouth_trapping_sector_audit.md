### Angular and polarization coverage

These are tangent to each angular sphere and have zero angular divergence. There is no toroidal monopole. Different $\ell $ decouple, and all $m$ within a degree are equivalent here. Under inversion in the three brane coordinates the toroidal displacement has parity $(-1)^{\ell +1}$. Radial, tangential-gradient, fourth-direction and scalar perturbations of the same degree have parity $(-1)^\ell $. This excludes their linear mixing with a pure toroidal mode in the specified symmetric, achiral preparation. The fourth-direction reflection that defines slab thickness parity is a different symmetry. A standing toroidal harmonic requires no winding of the coherent carrier phase in this benchmark.
