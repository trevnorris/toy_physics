# Can normally bound brane material pull a held mouth sideways?

**A smooth attraction to the curved surface transferred sideways momentum from a weak incident stream, even with free angular slip.** At the cut where shear order had fallen to 1%, the calculated transfer was about 32% of the tagged stream’s incident sideways momentum. What the geometric mouth ultimately retains remains open.

**Contact hypothesis.** We held one radius-one cubic mouth at intake 0.25. Independent sites entered uniformly over its spherical section at depth 1.175. Their fourth-coordinate speed followed the archived carrier. A smooth radial Morse well, using the neutral pair parameters and compression energy scale, was centered on the prescribed surface. Its strength fell with shear order. The axial drive supplied no sideways force. Attaching the potential to the geometric mouth, preparing sites at its minimum and supplying a chosen tagged mass flow are assumptions. [Contact equations and trajectories](../tools/mouth_upstream_normal_contact.py)

**Displaced bend.** With cold incoming sites, the fine-mesh holder force divided by tagged mass flow and imposed lateral displacement was +0.39928 from entry to mid-bend and −0.00336 from entry through the entire bend. Opposing loads nearly canceled. The corresponding coefficient for the actuator that preserved the bend’s shape was −0.30596. The coarser mesh gave −0.00419 for the small net residual. Opposite and half-size steps, a common translation, an independent energy derivative and linearized trajectories checked the distinction. All cold tags stayed in the half-depth neighborhood through the measured cuts. Removing the well gave zero force and almost the same front-to-back crossing asymmetry at the bend end. Counting sites there cannot establish grip. [Independent linearization](../tools/mouth_upstream_radial_tangent.py) · [Curved and straight guide controls](../tools/mouth_upstream_guide_flux.py)

**Held mouth in a sideways stream.** We removed the bend displacement and gave the incoming sites a common weak lateral velocity. For a Gaussian spread of initial radial speeds, the material-on-well force accumulated to each cut was:

| 50% | 0.2734 |
| 25% | 0.3204 |
| 1% | 0.3218 |

Angular momentum conservation and the dependence of exit speed on launch speed supplied an independent estimator. The coarser carrier gave 0.2554 at half order and 0.3220 at 1%. The last cut is inside the throat. [Incident-stream calculation](../tools/mouth_upstream_incident_flow.py)

**0PN decision.** The steady carrier put 2.12% of the current at the tag injection section (depth 1.175) within one assumed microscopic length of the wall. This is an integral of the archived interpolated field over a subcell band. It measures proximity only. A physical coefficient still needs the participating mass flow, the reaction borne by the mouth, and the outgoing lateral momentum at discharge. A retained contribution proportional to the companion’s incident speed would inherit its far-field inverse-square falloff. Free acceleration also needs the same mouth’s measured inertia.
