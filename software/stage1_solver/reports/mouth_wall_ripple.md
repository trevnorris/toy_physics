**What would settle it.** Drive the wall with a known weak local force and record its independent displacement in space and time at the same intake. On the observed spatial modes, form the complex force per displacement from independent drives. Subtract the fluid and trapped-wave force matrices for the same spatial and frequency coverage, with the same constraints on other modes. Free coupled harmonics must be included before reducing that system. Account for any additional static contact with the carrier. With known tension, extract the intrinsic speed of a resolved wall mode, allowing for drift, reflections and dispersion. Direct local flight times require a propagation length scale between the front thickness and the axial variation scale. Weight its inferred mass per area, \(\sigma/c(w)^2\), by \(\sin^4(\pi w/H)\) along both halves. This inference requires the wall law to apply across the bend’s spectral drives or its dispersion to be determined there. The four-patch calculation provides sample corrections. A physical result needs independent wall motion or a lower-level equation fixing its kinetic response. This fixed-end test also leaves the freely translating mouth at 0PN open.
