# Can the standing shear wave hold the downstream exit open?

**The strained crystal can confine a long-lived wave that pushes the exit outward at both training radii.** Every connected torsional resonance found still radiates. The one exactly silent wave found on a coarse grid sits on an isolated piece of ordered material and weakly favors narrowing. Thus the tests establish a source of outward force. They leave physical selection of the occupied wave, full force balance and closure open.

**Three trapping mechanisms.** We used the same permanently bonded, finite-thickness, strained backgrounds and material constants as the exit release. Different angular patterns and wave-solver resolutions introduce no new material parameters. [Model and exit preparation](mouth_exit_release.md)

| Proposed mechanism | What the measurements show |
|---|---|
| Symmetry protects a silent ground mode | The search found 19 distinct torsional poles at entrance radius 1 and 27 at radius 2 on the fine material grid across angular degrees 1–6. Every pole has a nonzero depth-uniform outgoing component. Increasing angular degree strongly suppresses leakage on a tracked family. Polar and breathing vibrations require a coupled wave operator of their own. |
| Slab thickness blocks escape | With unit flat shear speed and thickness, the cutoffs are 0, π, 2π, … . The aperture is on one face. A mode below π can still radiate in the uniform channel. The occupied radius-2 wave does precisely that. No connected, exactly bound pole was found in the scan. |
| A strain-induced slow region confines shear | For a unit wave normal **n**, the local torsional speed relative to flat is ‖Fᵀ**n**‖ wherever shear order is positive. On the fine preparations the axial speeds were 0.69226 and 0.82834. Holding density, order, geometry and exterior fixed while flattening the directional speeds reduced the degree-six quality factor from 6362 to 13.08 and from 290.5 to 9.15. Reversing the control recovered both poles. At the collar midpoint the local plane-interface calculation admits totally reflecting shear directions at both radii. Angular radiation suppression in the flat exterior also contributes; the global power attributable to total internal reflection is unresolved. |

From degree 6 through 12, loss decreased at every step on both meshes, by factors of 1891 and 18.3 on the finer preparations. Refining only the wave mesh on the same material moved the degree-12 quality factors from 20.45 million to 31.55 million at radius 1 and from 9358 to 10111 at radius 2. The last refinement changed the linewidths by 8.96% and 1.90%. Outgoing power remained positive through four channels at radius 1 and two at radius 2. The degree-six radius-1 pole stayed below the second thickness cutoff throughout its fixed-material mesh and narrowing tests. Narrowing the exit leaves the above channels open and reduces leakage at radius 1, while increasing it at radius 2. This finite sequence establishes strong suppression. It leaves the asymptotic decay law and the lowest energy preparation open. **Closure verdict.** On the coarse radius-2 state, a six-node island supports a wave at 1.81256 with exactly zero port displacement, entirely near the waist. Its slope is +0.00009121, so this mode favors further narrowing. The opening still carries inward current of 1.26365. On the fine grid the saved occupied wave remained connected and radiating at iteration 2425. The fine relaxation continues under its original guard. A positive-radius chart, a pinned half-ordered terminal surface and a permanently registered, invertible crystal map cannot follow a healed exit. An independent numerical check also found a sign error in an individual one-sided work comparison at roughly 10⁻¹¹ energy units. This limits interpretation of the late convergence. It leaves the separately verified finite narrowing work intact. For 0PN, the remaining discriminator is whether the *prepared* wave keeps enough action and supplies restoring force as the free order field and exit move together. A branch reaching contact also needs a material continuation for the carrier and newly joined lattice. The present results give no basis for requiring a vortex or winding charge. Radius 3 remains reserved.
