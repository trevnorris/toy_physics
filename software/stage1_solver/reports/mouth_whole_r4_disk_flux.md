**The weak balance includes transport around the disk edge.** Set the phase test function to one on the common disk nodes, including the rim, and to zero on all other nodes. Integrating $\mathbf j\cdot\nabla v$ on the duct elements gives 11.53917812. Integrating on the disjoint receiving elements with the opposite orientation gives 10.93270621 from above the disk, 0.52039554 from the exterior below the plane, and 0.08607654 from the exterior above. The difference between the two independently assembled totals is $1.62\times10^{-7}$. When the test instead tapers to zero across the last radial cell, the exterior contributions vanish and the two currents are 11.02738158 and 11.02738159. The resting wound control gives zero on every bank. The exterior contributions are work against a shared nodal variation. A physical discharge through the sealed wall would require a separate interface law.

**The one-sided physical trace resolves a normal-slope error concentrated at the rim.** On the original fine solid, the degree-12 and degree-18 face rules agree within $2.1\times10^{-12}$ for either signed current. Density, order and the two tangential gradients agree at each sampled point. Write the local axial current as $j_w=A\partial_w\varphi+B$, where $A=n(n+I|\nabla_{\parallel}\chi|^2)/(n+I|\nabla\chi|^2)$ and $B=-nI(\partial_w\chi)\nabla_{\parallel}\varphi\cdot\nabla_{\parallel}\chi/(n+I|\nabla\chi|^2)$. Taking the receiver minus duct difference with arithmetic means for the product gives −0.65840542 from the physical normal phase slopes, −0.00885148 from the change in $A$ and +0.06509797 from $B$. The measured deficit is 0.60215892. The region outside $r/b=0.97633136$ accounts for 0.57551877 of it. The coarse solid on this same carrier mesh gives a 5.2402% deficit. Phase conservation supplies one scalar check. For the same disk displacement, integrating the physical fluid and order stress in excess of wound rest over the incident and receiving elements gives respective axial works of 0.89483519 and 0.19964095 on the original fine grid.
