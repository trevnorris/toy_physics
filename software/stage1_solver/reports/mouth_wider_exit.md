**What was varied.** We held the entrance, depth, crystal registration and reservoir phase difference fixed, and continued one straight vortex into both reservoirs. Increasing the exit by an amount Δb widens the meridian by tΔb at fractional depth t. At each width we added the two-sided carrier force to the signed work of the crystal and order fields there. The receiving material relaxed on both signed shapes. A second integration reproduced the radius-2 force reversal. On a finer carrier mesh the forces at 1.040 and 1.042 were +0.090615 and −0.109811. These are restoring crossings in one shape coordinate; the full shape and crystal order have additional degrees of freedom. **What crossed the layer.** We freed order, density and phase within a finite thickness at the outlet and measured axial momentum flux, including pressure and material stresses, relative to a control with the same vortex and zero meridional drive. At 1.040 the entire disk lies inside the straight vortex’s half-density radius and transmits +0.012535 in additional axial stress. At 2.450 that inner region carries 47.37% of intake and contributes +0.023330 to a net +0.005773. Only 8.59% of the intake passes through material above half the bath density with *total* speed below sound. That annulus contributes −0.009793 to the axial stress change. Stress and mass current can be distributed quite differently. The force column measures radial work on the whole exit. The finite layer used fixed exterior field values; matching the layer back to the reservoirs remains open. At entrance 1 its current was over twice the sharp-interface estimate, although both gave zero subsonic area there. [Resolved layer and controls](mouth_winding_resolved_patch.md)

**Order and strain.** At entrance 1, exit 0.455, a positive order change at a melted site still lowers the energy (slope −0.00015278). At entrance 2, exit 1.040, the least-ordered free interior site already had order 0.42920 and raising it lowers the energy (slope −0.07694). The collar map preserved orientation. At the radius-2 crossing its outer principal stretches span about 0.01574 to 9.78. The constitutive law gives no fracture threshold with which to certify those strains. Core depletion is already sufficient to fail the near-crossing flow criterion; the measured large-width closing force includes elastic and order stresses. For 0PN, the vortex supplies a static restoring force along one coordinate, but this branch did not yield a self-supporting, steadily draining mouth within the validated regime. Revisiting it would require a physical front solution matched back to the exterior that can carry the depleted fast flow, or independent material evidence for a collar that balances at a wider exit. The original unwound baseline has its own completion hook. The breathing-mode relaxation stays parked and entrance radius 3 stays blind.
