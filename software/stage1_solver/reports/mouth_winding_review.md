# Independent check of static circulation at a draining exit

**Geometry and preparation.** Write the brane coordinates as \((x,y,z)\), the extra coordinate as \(w\), and the distance to the circulation axis as \(s=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\). For an axisymmetric straight defect the coherent phase is \(N\arg(x+iy)+\vartheta(s,z,w)\). Its zero set extends along \((z,w)\), giving a vortex sheet in four spatial dimensions. A linking circle on either side of the conversion front has phase winding \(2\pi N\). On this preparation \(\partial_\phi\chi=0\), so the order-entrainment correction to the azimuthal velocity vanishes and \(u_\phi=N/s\). This checks circulation on linked loops. The winding does not specify the flow rate. The feeding and receiving reservoirs must share the winding sector, with regular vortex cores through the reservoir cuts. Holding the residual phase difference fixed is the ensemble used for the unwound comparison. [Reversible carrier Hamiltonian](mouth_reversible_front.md)

**A two-sided force sign check.** The swirl dyadic \(n u_\phi^2\hat e_\phi\hat e_\phi\) has zero normal contraction on the azimuthally symmetric wall and terminal cut. The swirl can still produce a normal load by changing pressure, density gradients and the rest of the flow. The derivative of the matched full field energy at each signed exit radius measures held-shape work. As a control I partitioned a single spatially fixed trial swirl between the inside and outside of each measured shape. With control density \(s^2/(s^2+0.4^2)\), the radius-2 inner angular-energy force is −4.14024356 and the outer is +4.14024356. The two independent volume integrations cancel to \(2.1\times10^{-10}\) or better across both radii and both control widths. This identity checks orientation and area weighting. The model’s actual wall separates coherent fields that may relax differently on the two sides. [Independent geometry and signed-work calculation](../tools/mouth_winding_geometry_audit.py)

**A continuum check on speed.** The published local sound-speed comparison uses \(c^2=nU''(n)=n^4\). For unit-density bath conditions, clipping \(f=\sqrt{n}\) at one can only decrease the gradient energy and reservoir-subtracted potential. The two stationary phase-stiffness eigenvalues \(n\) and \(n^2/(n+I|\nabla\chi|^2)\) and the terminal stiffness proportional to \(n^2\) also increase with density. A stationary minimum therefore has \(n\leq1\). Away from the zero-density core, \(|\boldsymbol u|/c\geq |N|/s\). The exit at fixed \(w\) is a three-ball of radius \(b\); the fraction with \(s<a\leq b\) is \(1-(1-a^2/b^2)^{3/2}\). For one unit of winding the total speed exceeds the local sound speed over the whole radius-1 cap and at least 98.1464% of the radius-2 cap volume. The corresponding guaranteed fractions of their rim surfaces are 100% and 73.5350%. For two or more units both caps are covered. The lower bound uses only the tangential motion. A normally incident planar calibration probes normal current, while tangential propagation can also be Doppler shifted. These speed comparisons alone establish neither radiation nor instability of a stationary resolved vortex.

**Scoped verdict.** One imposed winding quantum overcomes the original closing load on both held sharp-front geometries. The finite exit forces survive an independent original-field energy integration. The nearly exhausted phase-biased drain at radius 1 and the fast, depleted core at both radii lie outside the calibrated conversion front. These results do not establish a stable, freely released crystalline opening or physical free fall.
