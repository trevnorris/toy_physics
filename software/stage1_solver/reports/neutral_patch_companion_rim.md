# Companion-driven force on a held rim at 0PN

A companion drain changed the force on one equatorial patch of the target rim **toward the companion** in every matched thermal history. Doubling the separation from eight to sixteen mouth radii left 24.93% of the increment for either attachment law. The source was the change in the solved brane flow when a second drain was present. Both feeding mouths had radius 1 and intake 0.25 to numerical precision. Each difference compares identical initial sites, intake, thermal noise, and wall preparation. **How flow reaches the patch.** We took the paired-minus-alone change in log amplitude and phase across the archived three-dimensional feeding aperture. We matched it at equal fractional radii on the curved waist and solved the linearized carrier response into the fourth spatial direction with the distant receiving bath held fixed. The directional harmonics preserve integrated intake to first order. At 8R the companion-directed velocity beside the converting wall fell from 1.04426 × 10⁻⁵ to 5.55104 × 10⁻⁶ during passage. Matching phase from the radius-1 feeding face to the radius-0.52484 waist rescales the tangential velocity gradient. The physical junction still needs local current and traction matching. The time and order along the transit come from the isolated preparation. [Field calculation](../tools/neutral_patch_companion_field.py)

**What sets the size.** In the primary trial the 12 brane-facing sites experience drag relative to this velocity. Cross-layer bonds, initially unloaded grips on the lower sites and an elastic strip transmit the response to the waist. We assumed one patch length unit equals 0.01 mouth radii and used the previous patch clock and unit drag. Exposing both layers to the same flow increased the increment at 8R by factors of 2.9053 and 3.1664. Disconnecting the central patch from the strip gave zero rim force. Halving the velocity reproduced the local responses within 0.01% after rescaling. The coarser time and strip grids shifted them by about 0.8%. An independent calculation differentiating the bond forces along a saved isolated trajectory reproduced the direct weak response for both laws. **From a patch to the rim.** If active patches cover solid angle uniformly and have the same linear response along each meridian, projection toward the companion retains two thirds of the degree-one velocity. Higher angular harmonics cancel in the average. At 8R this gives +0.000338112 (central) or +0.000308956 (layered) per active patch, estimated from opposite weak drives. The total through this channel requires its independently measured active patch count. For free fall we also need the other traction and momentum channels and the inertia of the same mouth. The next discriminating measurement is the relative velocity and drag on each material layer under an independently measured brane flow, with the rim grip fixed. This tests the factor-of-three ambiguity before inferring a whole-mouth force. [Angular calculation](../tools/neutral_patch_companion_projection.py)
