# Does removing the patch edge fix the compression curve?

**The excess stiffening persists without an edge.** We compressed cold two-layer triangular samples with 24, 72 and 200 sites at the same reference pitch and with the same within-layer and cross-layer Morse bonds. All sites could rearrange in their planes in the periodic cells. For comparison, we cut the same lattices open and held their perimeters under identical strains. The three brane directions strained equally, the fourth-direction thickness stayed fixed, and each pressure reading subtracted its rest value. The primary case holds the layer gap fixed. [Periodic calculation](../tools/neutral_patch_periodic_compression.py) · [Earlier finite-patch test](neutral_patch_carrier_compression.md)

| Sites | Open perimeter, pressure excess at 5% higher density | Periodic, pressure excess at 5% higher density |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 24 | 5.825% | 5.902% |
| 72 | 5.860% | 5.902% |
| 200 | 5.878% | 5.902% |

The open controls each used their own 1% density calibration. One scale from the smallest periodic cell sufficed for all three periodic sizes. At 2% higher density the periodic fixed-gap sample overpredicted the pressure increase by 1.508%. At 2% lower density it underpredicted the magnitude of the pressure drop by 4.649%. Placing the layer gap along a strained brane direction increased the periodic 5% error to 6.904%. Using the previous irregular finite patch’s force scale without recalibration overpredicted the periodic 1% increment by 65.59% for the fixed gap. The old and new preparations differ in both environment and arrangement.

**Starting from the original material.** We also wrapped the saved 24 sites onto the smallest periodic cell in two orientations and let every site relax. Both preparations reproduced the ordered cell’s energy, pressure and compression curve. Enlarging the interaction range preserved the readings. All sampled periodic states were locally stable under in-plane site displacements, allowing for common translation. The size check describes this packing; other structures and gap motion remain possible. [Original-frame preparation](../tools/neutral_patch_periodic_seed.py)

**What the bonds contribute.** For \(\eta=\log n\), the carrier requires \(p''(0)/p'(0)=5\). An independent reviewer differentiated the energies of successive groups of lattice neighbors. The periodic ratios were **8.127036** for the fixed gap and **8.633214** for the strained gap. In the fixed-gap case, bonds within a layer contributed 63.70724 to the first derivative and 515.34710 to the second. Bonds across the gap contributed −0.42174 and −1.02355, respectively. In both gap orientations each bond class gives a positive value of \(p''-5p'\). Changing only their positive well depths therefore cannot recover the carrier ratio at this spacing and layer alignment. [Guarded measurements and independent review](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/_measurements/neutral_patch_periodic_compression.json)

**0PN decision.** The common pressure scale can still be matched to first order at rest. Its nonlinear mismatch in this material persists in the sample interior. Neither this discrepancy nor its size measures an error in a mouth’s acceleration. The reference pitch has a nonzero initial stress. Before changing the short-range force law, test a cell that selects its in-plane spacing under an independently specified ambient load. Zero applied in-plane stress is a testable first preparation; the physics of the brane may select a different ambient stress. The force per actual wall area and the total lateral reaction remain open. Working base `1dc45d09`. Independent local review covers the periodic and original-frame tests.
