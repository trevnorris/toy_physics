# Can the neutral patch identify which material carries the shear?

**We measured force and tracked every receiving site. The existing rules for identifying a bonded pair give different answers about which sites are ordered.** Inferring slip requires an independent marker of material order. A stress-relaxation law can be tested without individual phase labels, but transferring it to the mouth also requires an independent physical traction scale and the reaction on the other side.

**Preparation.** On the saved 24-site neutral central-bond patch, we held each plane flat at the existing gap, relaxed the 12 receiving sites at each uniform order level, and used the existing cross-layer bond strength proportional to order. With zero intake and zero temperature, the lower face translated by a smooth sideways pulse of peak displacement ±0.001 or ±0.0005 bond lengths. Every upper site moved freely with the patch's original unit mobility. We subtracted opposite drives at the same time. Direct bond work and the opposite face's force checked the material force. The readings are at peak imposed displacement, half a patch time unit into the pulse. The nominal two-dimensional area is 10.392305 squared bond lengths. At quarter order, the signed half-difference in total force was **+0.00428158** in patch force units at full drive. Each tag requires mutual nearest neighbors and binding greater than 60% of the chosen well depth. At half order and below, even the entire softened well is shallower than 60% of its entry depth. That cutoff explains the zero count. The current-depth rule identifies the same nine sites at all four resting preparations. Neither rule measures a quarter population on this sample.

**Why the phase velocities remain unknown.** At quarter order, three equal-count ordered sites on this particular face would give the prescribed fraction exactly. We considered every way to give three sites fixed identities on both signed drives. The 220 assignments have the same total force and the same 12 measured velocities. At the peak, the ordered mean speed minus the remaining mean speed ranged from **−0.10730 to +0.09833** per unit peak displacement in inverse patch time. These are bounds from the count alone. A physical microscopic rule could restrict the assignments. The fraction may also describe an ensemble or a continuously varying property. The site law is overdamped; the summed site drift and the net interlayer force are linked by its specified mobility. These traces alone do not measure transverse inertia. **Decision for 0PN.** Preserve the force in patch units. Converting it to the total force on a physical mouth still needs the amount of active material per actual three-dimensional wall area and the reaction on its other side. The earlier compression calibration gives a conditional scale for an assumed cell volume. On the branch where order counts constituents, independent measurement of the order current relative to the material would distinguish slip. The default zero relative current is an assumption. On the structural-order branch, the mechanical test would measure how long the local material retains shear stress, with any constituent fraction calibrated separately. A useful next hypothesis is that a site's persistent local shear rigidity identifies its contribution to order. Establish this on independently prepared ordered and disordered material before testing intermediate states and slow force. The force law for a whole mouth and its free acceleration remain open.
