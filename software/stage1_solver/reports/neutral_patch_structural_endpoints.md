# Can the same bonds produce an ordered and a shear-free patch?

**Random packing changed how much force the patch transmitted, but every tested cold preparation retained static shear stiffness.** We kept the Morse pair forces, layer spacing, mean density and unit site mobility fixed. The experiment had no shear-order input and no bond weakening. At each of two sizes we prepared a registered triangular crystal and three independent random arrangements of sites in two flat periodic planes. We minimized the energy with one common translation removed, then let every internal site move as the cell was sheared at fixed area. This tests a candidate mechanism for the neutral material. The planes and cell shape still constrain it. Each stiffness is the change in shear stress per unit simple shear strain, in patch energy per projected two-dimensional area. We subtract each sample's own initial stress. All three randomized 32-site preparations recovered triangular order and the reference stiffness. The three randomized 72-site preparations retained lattice defects and residual shear stress in their fixed rectangular cells. We counted same-plane neighbors within 1.35 reference pitches. Their local sixfold orientational order ranged from 0.904 to 0.965, where the ideal triangular value is one. Even the two samples with 62 six-coordinated sites had very different stiffnesses. Counting such sites alone cannot predict the slow force in these preparations.

**Held and released.** For the softest sample the linearized equations gave an instantaneous stiffness of 114.211. After one patch time unit under held strain its stress per strain had fallen to 22.878 and the fully relaxed value was 17.666. Removing the imposed strain at one time unit caused a short-lived reaction of opposite sign. An independent time integration of the full nonlinear bond forces at strains ±0.0001 gave 22.875 just before release and −91.337 immediately after release, versus the linear prediction of −91.334 after release. These incremental stresses are divided by peak strain. Recoil after releasing the strain and the sustained positive held load are different observables. The calculation uses overdamped motion; it tests stress relaxation and supplies no transverse wave speed. [Nonlinear time-domain control](../tools/neutral_patch_endpoint_pulse.py)

**Checks and decision.** Direct energy and stress differences checked the relaxed stiffness at opposite strains. The softest sample required two extra strain halvings. An independent image sum recovered 87.09086570 for the crystal. On the soft sample's settled coordinates, independent energy derivatives recovered 17.66591378, versus 17.66591363 from the primary calculation. These are local responses within the prepared minima. The physical bulk can explore motion along the fourth spatial direction and may rearrange at finite temperature or during conversion. The confined cold calculation supplies no shear-free reference from which to predict a partially converted mouth. The next physical test is to release confinement toward the bulk at matched ambient conditions.
