# Can a long wrinkle remove static shear from the neutral patch?

**Both interaction laws produced a wrinkle that persisted after release. Both settled patches continued to resist a small sideways displacement.** We used the same cold, two-layer material, pitch and projected area per site as in the small-cell test. The 24-by-24 periodic cell contains 1,152 sites, all free in two tangential directions and in $w$. Each law assigns bond energy from the pair's current separation along $w$. The weights $f$ and $f^2$ reproduce the same confined bilayer and differ at intermediate separations. [Interaction laws and flat-crystal reference](neutral_patch_open_w.md) · [Full-cell calculation](../tools/neutral_patch_wrinkle.py)

| Interaction weight | Flat crystal shear stiffness | Settled wrinkle shear stiffness | Crest-to-trough height |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $f$ | 85.0061 | 84.8586 | 0.28352 |
| $f^2$ | 84.2434 | 78.3229 | 1.86851 |

The flat column is the earlier simple-shear response of the flat hollow crystal. That shape is unstable to a long wrinkle in the larger box. For the settled column we sheared the large cell at fixed area by opposite strains of 0.0001 and 0.00005, let the sites relax and measured the change in average shear stress. The energy curvature gave the same stiffness in patch energy per projected area. The height is the span of the mean $w$ coordinate of corresponding sites in the two original layers, in units of their original confined spacing. [Wrinkle profiles](figures/neutral_patch_wrinkle.png) · [Figure calculation](../tools/neutral_patch_wrinkle_figure.py)

**Direction control.** The table uses perturbations varying along $x$ for $f$ and $y$ for $f^2$. Swapping the directions gave 84.8586 again for $f$. All three settled profiles vary primarily along the longer $x$ side of this rectangular box. For $f^2$ started along $x$, the first search and a continuation from its saved positions both ended without verifying a stable equilibrium. The reviewer independently confirmed a small downhill move from the first checkpoint. Its shear stiffness remains undetermined.

**Where the softness goes.** The lowest internal mode in each of the two original settled patches shifts the wrinkle's crest through the material. An independent calculation measured its squared overlap with that shift above 0.99999998 in both cases. Its coupling to uniform simple shear was below $10^{-12}$ in patch units. A crest can move with little work while the patch as a whole still transmits a sideways load.

**What this gives 0PN.** Cold buckling by itself left a finite shear response in the three settled states. The model patch has no intake or receiving bath. Predicting the force on a flexible draining mouth still needs the slow shear response during conversion. The next material test would allow contacts to exchange under the same bond law, track which neighbors change and measure whether a held shear load decays. This would test whether loss of rigidity emerges with bond strength fixed. Resolving the fourth cold preparation separately would require following the energy as the crest slides and checking stability after release. Working base `1dc45d09`.
