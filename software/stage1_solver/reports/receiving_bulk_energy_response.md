# Does a fixed intake determine the receiving bulk's energy flow at 0PN?

**Result.** The pure radial 4D monopole gives a unique prediction. Specifying the two radii, intake rates and far-bulk state leaves a freedom as soon as the receiving flow can redistribute over angle. Two exterior solutions can have identical velocities at the comparison instant and the same static force. They transmit different amounts of energy under slow approach. The distinction is the local time-dependent pressure. This tests sufficiency of the available exterior data. The full brane-to-bulk matching law remains open. [Stationary-mouth test](stationary_mouth_energy_test.md) · [Conversion and energy premises](../../../research/pde_ledger_v3/directives/O2_SHARED_PHYSICS.md#6-energy-input-and-forcepower-pairing)

**Two exterior responses.** In the benchmark's constant-density, irrotational 4D bulk, put the fixed mouth at the origin and the companion at \(x=d\). Write \(s=-\dot d\), \(q_i=D(a_i)>0\), \(\Omega_4=2\pi^2\). Use a fixed sphere centered on mouth 1 with the companion outside. Start with the two source potentials \(-q_i/(2\rho\Omega_4 r_i^2)\). In the second response, add \(B(d)h\) only in the pair, where \(h=x/r^4=\cos\theta/r^3\) is a decaying harmonic dipole about mouth 1. The isolated mouths remain radial. For an explicit smooth response on the disjoint-mouth domain, take \(B(d)=k(d-d_*)(d_*/d)^2\). It vanishes at infinite separation and at \(d_*\), with \(B'(d_*)=k\). The pure monopole has \(k=0\). The same pressure head at infinity fixes the potential gauge in each case. The added mode carries zero integrated mass through either enclosing sphere. With sufficiently weak disturbances near \(d_*\), the outward flow can be maintained at both mouths.

**Why the difference survives the sphere integral.** At \(d_*\) the pair fields have identical normal and tangential velocities, but \(\Delta\partial_t\phi=-skh\). Bernoulli therefore gives \(\Delta p=\rho skh\), with the same pressure at rest. The three control states are identical between responses, so the difference in their four-state energy rates is \(\Delta J=\rho sk\int h(\mathbf v_1+\mathbf v_2)\cdot\mathbf n\,dA\). The radial self-flow contributes zero. The divergence theorem and the volume mean of the harmonic \(\partial_x\phi_2\) give \(\int n_x\phi_2\,dA=(\Omega_4R^4/4)v_{2x}(0)\). Divide by \(R^3\) and differentiate in \(R\) to obtain \(\int h\,\partial_R\phi_2\,dA=(\Omega_4/4)v_{2x}(0)\). Thus the interaction energy rate per unit signed approach speed is

\[
L_{\rm bulk}(d_*) =
\frac{q_1q_2}{\rho\Omega_4 d_*^3}
-\frac{k q_2}{4d_*^3}.
\]

The first term is the static bulk attraction magnitude for both responses at this separation under the benchmark load assignment. For the illustrative \(k=q_1/(\rho\Omega_4)\), the energy slope falls to **three quarters** of that value. Mathematica integrated the four primitive currents and the static stress independently at two radii, for approach, recession and both half speeds. SymPy gave the exact expression above. The choice of \(k\) illustrates nonuniqueness at one separation. Its magnitude, distance dependence and order in companion strength for a physical mouth are unknown. The leading 3D inverse-square force is outside the scope of this bulk comparison. [Calculations and checked outputs](../../../docs/_measurements/receiving_bulk_energy_response.json)

**Datum to obtain.** In this family the missing scalar is \(B'(d_*)\). Under a quasistatic response, measure the outward bulk velocity as a function of direction at nearby held separations. After subtracting the two radial source fields, the remaining \(v_N^{\rm res}\) gives \(B(d)=-(4R^4/3)\langle \cos\theta\,v_N^{\rm res}\rangle\), with a normalized angular average over the centered 4D sphere. Neighboring separations give its derivative. A response with memory requires measurements on matched moving histories instead. More generally, measure the part of the total outward energy rate on the receiving cut that reverses with speed. Subtract both isolated controls and restore the shared background. For the neutral carrier, time-resolved \(\partial_t\psi\) and \(\partial_N\psi\) determine its contribution. Include any additional live energy channels once. A matched receiving measurement can then be combined with the feeding-side rate and compared with an independent total holding reaction. The physical value of \(k\) and free acceleration remain open.
